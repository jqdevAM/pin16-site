P0099

Intake Air Temperature Sensor 2 Circuit Intermittent/Erratic

Severity
Low
Driving
Drivable — book it in

The second intake air temperature signal drops out or jumps about. As with any intermittent electrical fault, the cause is far more likely to be a connection than the sensor itself.

Possible causes

  • CommonLoose, corroded or backed-out pin in the connector
  • CommonChafed wiring that opens only when the engine moves on its mounts
  • CommonOil or water that has crept into the connector
  • PossiblePoor earth
  • PossibleFailing sensor breaking down when hot

What you might notice

  • Slight loss of power, particularly on a turbocharged engine
  • Hesitation or rough running
  • Poor fuel economy
  • Check engine light on
  • Often nothing obvious while driving

What to check, in order

  1. Wiggle the connector and flex the harness with live data on screen. An intermittent fault that can be provoked by hand is nearly solved.
  2. Open the connector and look for corrosion, spread terminals or a pin pushed back out of its housing.
  3. Check the earth before replacing the sensor.
  4. Replace the sensor last. Intermittent faults are connections far more often than components, because a component that has failed usually stays failed.
