P0905

Gate Select Position Circuit Range/Performance

Severity
High
Driving
Drive only to a garage
System
Powertrain
Group
Transmission and control modules

The gate select position sensor is answering but its reading does not agree with what the module expects - the reported position does not match where the selector shaft actually ended up. Usually this is lost calibration or mechanical wear rather than a dead sensor.

Possible causes

  • CommonPosition calibration lost or never performed after a repair
  • CommonWorn selector mechanism inside the gearbox
  • CommonFailing or drifting sensor
  • PossibleActuator not completing its travel
  • PossibleLow hydraulic pressure on a hydraulically actuated system
  • PossibleHigh resistance in the wiring skewing the reading

What you might notice

  • Gear changes slow, harsh or failing
  • Some gears unavailable
  • Transmission dropping into neutral
  • Transmission warning message
  • Check engine light on

What to check, in order

  1. Run the position calibration procedure first. On these transmissions it is a routine step and resolves many of these codes without any parts.
  2. Watch the reported gate position through a full sweep and compare it with the specification.
  3. Check the hydraulic pressure if the system is hydraulically actuated - a slow actuator often means low pressure rather than a bad sensor.
  4. Check the wiring and connector for high resistance.
  5. Read all codes for gear shift position or actuator faults alongside.
