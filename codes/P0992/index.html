P0992

Transmission Fluid Pressure Sensor/Switch "F" Circuit

Severity
High
Driving
Drive only to a garage

A fault in the circuit for transmission fluid pressure sensor or switch "F". As with sensors "A" to "D", this reports whether pressure reached a particular hydraulic circuit - the module's way of confirming that the clutch it just commanded actually received pressure. Without it the module cannot verify its own shifts.

Possible causes

  • CommonFailed pressure sensor or switch
  • CommonCorroded transmission case connector, or fluid wicking into it
  • CommonInternal transmission harness failure
  • PossibleWiring damage between the transmission and the module
  • PossibleLow fluid level or genuinely low pressure
  • PossibleDebris blocking the pressure passage to the sensor

What you might notice

  • Transmission stuck in one gear
  • Harsh or delayed shifts
  • Slipping
  • Several transmission codes stored together
  • Check engine light on

What to check, in order

  1. Check the fluid level and condition first - low fluid produces genuinely low pressure and this code with a good sensor.
  2. Read all transmission codes together. Several pressure sensor codes at once point at the case connector, the internal harness or genuinely low line pressure.
  3. Inspect the case connector for fluid ingress and corroded pins.
  4. Watch the reported pressures on the live data screen against the specification for each gear.
  5. Eliminate the external wiring and the case connector before dropping the pan.
