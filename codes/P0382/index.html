P0382

Glow Plug/Heater Circuit "B"

Severity
Low
Driving
Drivable — book it in
System
Powertrain
Group
Ignition system or misfire

A fault in glow plug or heater circuit "B". Glow plugs preheat the combustion chambers of a diesel engine so it will start when cold. A fault here often goes unnoticed in warm weather and then becomes obvious on the first cold morning, when the engine cranks for a long time, starts roughly, and produces white smoke.

Possible causes

  • CommonOne or more failed glow plugs
  • CommonFailed glow plug relay or control module
  • CommonCorroded connections at the glow plugs
  • PossibleBlown fuse on the glow plug circuit
  • PossibleDamaged wiring in the glow plug harness

What you might notice

  • Hard starting when cold
  • White smoke on a cold start
  • Rough running for the first minute from cold
  • Glow plug light behaving oddly
  • No symptom at all in warm weather

What to check, in order

  1. Test in cold weather, or at least on a fully cold engine. In summer a diesel will often start acceptably with several dead glow plugs, which is why these faults go unnoticed.
  2. Measure each glow plug individually. They usually fail one at a time, and the code may be set by just one of them.
  3. Check the relay or glow plug control module and the fuse.
  4. Inspect the connections at the plugs for corrosion - they sit in a hot, dirty part of the engine.
  5. Be aware that seized glow plugs are common on high-mileage engines and can break off during removal. That turns a cheap job into an expensive one, so it is worth knowing before starting rather than halfway through.
