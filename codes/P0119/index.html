P0119

Engine Coolant Temperature Circuit Intermittent

Severity
Medium
Driving
Drivable — book it in
System
Powertrain
Group
Fuel and air metering

The coolant temperature signal is intermittent - dropping out and returning. This is characteristic of a connection problem rather than a failed sensor, and it can make the engine's fuelling lurch as the module reacts to a temperature that appears to jump.

Possible causes

  • CommonLoose or corroded connector
  • CommonChafed wiring shorting intermittently
  • PossibleFailing sensor breaking down when hot

What you might notice

  • Check engine light that comes and goes
  • Occasional rough running or fuelling lurch
  • Cooling fans cycling unexpectedly
  • Temperature gauge jumping

What to check, in order

  1. Inspect and reseat the connector, checking for corrosion and spread terminals.
  2. Watch the live temperature reading while gently flexing the harness with the engine running.
  3. Trace the wiring for chafing against the engine or brackets.
  4. Fix the connection and re-test before replacing the sensor.
