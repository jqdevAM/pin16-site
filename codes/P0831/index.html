P0831

Clutch Pedal Switch "A" Circuit Low

Severity
Medium
Driving
Drivable — book it in
System
Powertrain
Group
Transmission

The clutch pedal switch "A" signal is stuck low - typically shorted to earth, or the switch stuck in one state. The module cannot tell whether the clutch pedal is pressed, so starting, cruise control and stop-start may all behave unexpectedly.

Possible causes

  • CommonFailed clutch pedal switch "A"
  • CommonWiring shorted to earth, or the switch stuck in one state between the switch and the module
  • CommonSwitch out of adjustment so it never changes state
  • PossibleCorroded connector in the footwell
  • PossibleAftermarket wiring spliced into the circuit

What you might notice

  • Engine will not start with the clutch pressed
  • Engine starts without the clutch pressed
  • Cruise control unavailable
  • Stop-start not working
  • Check engine light on

What to check, in order

  1. Watch the switch "A" state while pressing and releasing the pedal. A reading that never changes confirms the fault.
  2. Unplug the switch and check whether the low reading persists at the harness. If it does, the wiring is at fault rather than the switch.
  3. Check the adjustment before condemning the switch - a switch the pedal never reaches reads exactly like a failed one.
  4. Compare against the other clutch switch, which should still behave normally.
  5. Ask whether an aftermarket alarm or immobiliser was fitted, since these are commonly wired into the clutch switch circuit.
