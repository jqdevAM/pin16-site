P2175

Throttle Actuator Control System - Low Airflow Detected

Severity
High
Driving
Drive only to a garage
System
Powertrain
Group
Fuel and air metering

The module has measured persistently less airflow than the throttle position should allow. Either something is restricting the intake, or the airflow sensor is under-reporting - and a neglected air filter is the cheapest of those to eliminate.

Possible causes

  • CommonAir leak in the intake system
  • CommonCarbon on the throttle plate, so actual airflow does not match the commanded position
  • CommonFaulty mass airflow sensor
  • CommonThrottle position sensor drift
  • PossibleBlocked air filter or restricted intake
  • PossibleVacuum hose disconnected or split

What you might notice

  • Reduced power mode
  • Hesitation or surging
  • Rough idle
  • Worse fuel economy
  • Check engine light on

What to check, in order

  1. Check for intake leaks and disconnected vacuum hoses. Air the module has not accounted for is the most common reason measured airflow disagrees with throttle position.
  2. Inspect the throttle body for carbon, which changes how much air a given plate angle admits.
  3. Compare the mass airflow reading against the throttle position on the live data screen through an acceleration.
  4. Check the air filter and intake ducting.
  5. Read all codes - throttle position or MAF codes alongside this one name the component.
