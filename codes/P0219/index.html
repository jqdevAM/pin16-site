P0219

Engine Overspeed Condition

Severity
Medium
Driving
Drivable — book it in
System
Powertrain
Group
Fuel and air metering - injector circuits

The engine has been turned faster than its rev limit. The module records this because over-revving can damage valves, pistons and bearings. In the great majority of cases the cause is a mis-selected gear on a manual car - selecting a much lower gear than intended and letting the clutch out spins the engine well past its limit before the driver can react.

Possible causes

  • CommonWrong gear selected while changing down
  • CommonEngine braking in too low a gear on a descent
  • PossibleWheels spinning on a slippery surface
  • PossibleTransmission fault causing an unintended downshift
  • PossibleFaulty engine speed sensor recording a false reading

What you might notice

  • Usually recorded after a specific incident the driver will remember
  • Check engine light on
  • Sometimes a noise or roughness that started at that moment
  • Often no symptom at all

What to check, in order

  1. Think back to when it happened. This code almost always follows a specific event, and if you remember a mis-shift, that is the explanation.
  2. Listen for any new noise, and check the oil level and condition. A serious over-rev can damage valves or bearings, and that would show as a noise or a misfire rather than as another code.
  3. Read all codes. Misfire or compression-related codes appearing at the same time would suggest actual damage.
  4. Check for transmission codes if you were not driving hard - an unintended downshift is the other plausible cause.
  5. If nothing sounds or feels different, clear the code and see whether it returns. A one-off incident will not.
