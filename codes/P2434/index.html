P2434

Secondary Air Injection System Air Flow/Pressure Sensor Circuit Intermittent/Erratic (Bank 1)

Severity
Low
Driving
Drivable — book it in
System
Powertrain
Group
Auxiliary emission controls

The secondary air flow or pressure sensor signal on bank 1 is erratic or dropping out. Because the system only runs briefly on a cold start, an intermittent fault here can take many days to catch - it can only appear during that one short window per journey.

Possible causes

  • CommonLoose, corroded or backed-out connector pin
  • CommonChafed or heat-damaged wiring near the exhaust
  • CommonWater ingress into the connector
  • PossibleFailing sensor
  • PossiblePartially blocked sensing hose

What you might notice

  • Check engine light appearing occasionally, often after a cold night
  • Failed emissions test
  • Usually no symptom the driver notices

What to check, in order

  1. Expect this to take several cold starts to reproduce. The system runs for about a minute a day, so patience is part of the diagnosis.
  2. Note whether it correlates with wet or cold weather, which points at the connector.
  3. Wiggle-test the harness with the live data screen showing the sensor reading.
  4. Inspect the connector for a backed-out pin and for water.
  5. Check the harness for heat damage where it runs near the exhaust.
