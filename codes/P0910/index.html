P0910

Gate Select Actuator Circuit/Open

Severity
High
Driving
Drive only to a garage
System
Powertrain
Group
Transmission and control modules

An open circuit or general electrical fault in the gate select actuator circuit - the motor or valve that moves the selector shaft across the gate. Without it the module cannot choose which pair of gears to engage, so gear changes will fail.

Possible causes

  • CommonFailed gate select actuator
  • CommonCorroded or damaged connector at the gearbox
  • CommonOpen circuit or broken wire in the harness
  • PossibleBlown fuse on the actuator supply
  • PossibleWater ingress at the gearbox connector
  • RareFailed control module driver

What you might notice

  • Transmission will not select gears
  • Car stuck in neutral
  • Transmission warning message
  • Check engine light on

What to check, in order

  1. Check the fuses on the actuator supply first.
  2. Inspect the connector at the gearbox for corrosion and water.
  3. Measure the actuator winding resistance against specification.
  4. Check continuity through the harness between the module and the actuator.
  5. Run the calibration procedure after any repair here.
