P0703

Brake Switch "B" Circuit

Severity
Medium
Driving
Drivable — book it in
System
Powertrain
Group
Transmission

A fault in the brake switch "B" circuit — the one the transmission module uses. The module needs to know when the brake pedal is pressed - to release the shift interlock, to disengage cruise control and the torque converter clutch, and to allow the car to be shifted out of park. A brake switch fault therefore shows itself in odd, seemingly unrelated ways.

Possible causes

  • CommonMisadjusted or failed brake pedal switch
  • CommonBroken or worn switch striker on the pedal bracket
  • PossibleWiring or connector fault at the switch
  • PossibleBlown brake light fuse
  • PossibleAftermarket alarm or cruise control spliced into the brake switch circuit

What you might notice

  • Cannot shift out of park
  • Brake lights permanently on, or not working
  • Cruise control will not engage
  • Torque converter clutch not releasing on braking
  • Check engine light on

What to check, in order

  1. Check whether the brake lights work, and whether they go off when the pedal is released. This one observation identifies most brake switch faults without any tools.
  2. Check the switch adjustment at the pedal. These switches are adjustable and go out of adjustment as the plastic striker wears.
  3. Watch the brake switch state on the live data screen while someone presses the pedal.
  4. Inspect the connector at the switch, which sits in the footwell where feet catch it.
  5. Ask whether an aftermarket alarm or cruise control was fitted - these are commonly spliced into this circuit.
