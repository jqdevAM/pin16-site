P0738

TCM Engine Speed Output Circuit Low

Severity
Medium
Driving
Drivable — book it in

The engine speed output circuit from the transmission module is stuck low - typically shorted to earth or open circuit. The module cannot deliver the engine speed signal to whatever consumes it, so that system loses its reading while the transmission itself carries on normally.

Possible causes

  • CommonWiring shorted to earth or open circuit between the transmission module and the receiving module
  • CommonCorroded connector at either end
  • PossibleChafed harness shorting against the body or engine
  • PossibleFailed receiving module loading the circuit
  • RareFailed transmission control module output driver

What you might notice

  • Rev counter dead or reading wrong
  • Check engine light on
  • Usually no effect on how the car drives

What to check, in order

  1. Disconnect the receiving module and see whether the low reading persists. If it clears, the receiving module is loading the circuit.
  2. Inspect both connectors for corrosion.
  3. Follow the wire looking for chafe damage.
  4. Check for aftermarket splices into this circuit.
  5. Confirm the transmission module has good power and earth before condemning it.
