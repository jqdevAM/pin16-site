P2772

Four Wheel Drive (4WD) Low Switch Circuit Range/Performance

Severity
Medium
Driving
Drivable — book it in
System
Powertrain
Group
Transmission

The four-wheel drive low range switch is answering but its reading is not plausible - two positions reading the same, or a position reported that the selector is not in. Worn contacts or a linkage not moving the switch fully are the usual reasons.

Possible causes

  • CommonFailed or worn switch
  • CommonWiring or connector fault
  • CommonCorroded connector - underbody switches take road spray and salt
  • PossibleSwitch out of adjustment or its linkage worn
  • PossibleDamaged clock spring, on steering-wheel-mounted controls

What you might notice

  • Drive mode indicator flickering or wrong
  • Low range engaging when not requested
  • Mode changes taking a long time
  • Check engine light on

What to check, in order

  1. Move the selector slowly through every position while watching the reported state, looking for a position that reads ambiguously.
  2. Check the switch adjustment and its linkage for wear.
  3. Confirm the actuator completes its travel.
  4. Inspect the connector for corrosion and for a partial short between adjacent pins.
