P0504

Brake Switch "A"/"B" Correlation

Severity
Medium
Driving
Drivable — book it in
System
Powertrain
Group
Vehicle speed control, idle control and auxiliary inputs

The two brake switch signals disagree. Vehicles use two independent brake switch circuits precisely so that a single failure can be detected, and this code is that detection working: one of the two is wrong, and the module cannot tell which.

Possible causes

  • CommonMisadjusted or failed brake pedal switch
  • CommonWorn or broken striker on the pedal bracket
  • CommonWiring or connector fault in the footwell
  • PossibleBlown brake light fuse
  • PossibleAftermarket alarm or cruise control spliced into the circuit
  • CommonOne of the two switch circuits failed while the other still works

What you might notice

  • Brake lights stuck on or not working
  • Cannot shift out of park
  • Cruise control will not engage or will not cancel
  • Stop-start system disabled
  • Check engine light on

What to check, in order

  1. Watch both brake switch states together on the live data screen while pressing the pedal. The one that does not change with the other is the fault.
  2. Check the switch adjustment - a switch the pedal only just reaches will change state later than its partner and set this code without either being broken.
  3. Look at the brake lights. Working brake lights that go off when the pedal is released eliminate most of this without any tools.
  4. Check the switch adjustment at the pedal - these are adjustable and drift as the plastic striker wears.
  5. Watch the brake switch state on the live data screen while someone presses the pedal.
  6. Inspect the connector in the footwell, where feet catch it.
  7. Ask whether an aftermarket alarm or cruise control was fitted.
