P2749

Intermediate Shaft Speed Sensor "C" Circuit

Severity
High
Driving
Drive only to a garage
System
Powertrain
Group
Transmission

A fault in the circuit for intermediate shaft speed sensor "C". The module compares the speeds of the shafts inside the transmission to verify which clutches are actually applied, so losing one of those readings means it cannot confirm what the transmission is doing.

Possible causes

  • CommonFailed speed sensor
  • CommonCorroded connector or wiring damage at the sensor
  • CommonMetal debris on the sensor tip, from internal wear
  • PossibleDamaged or contaminated reluctor ring
  • PossibleInternal transmission harness failure
  • PossibleSlipping clutch, so the speeds really are wrong

What you might notice

  • Transmission stuck in one gear
  • Harsh or erratic shifting
  • Slipping - engine speed rising without matching acceleration
  • Speedometer erratic on some vehicles
  • Check engine light on

What to check, in order

  1. Watch the speed readings on the live data screen while driving. Input, intermediate and output speeds should track each other in a fixed ratio in each gear.
  2. Inspect the sensor tip for a coating of metal filings. Debris here is not just the fault - it is evidence of internal wear that will need addressing.
  3. Check the connector and wiring for corrosion and chafing.
  4. Check the fluid level and condition first. It is the cheapest check and it explains more transmission complaints than every electrical fault combined.
  5. Where the sensor is internal, eliminate the external wiring and the case connector before dropping the pan.
