P0815

Upshift Switch Circuit

Severity
Low
Driving
No driving restriction
System
Powertrain
Group
Transmission

A fault in the upshift switch circuit - the paddle or lever switch that lets the driver request a manual shift up. The transmission will keep shifting automatically; what is lost is the driver's ability to ask for a gear.

Possible causes

  • CommonFailed or worn upshift switch
  • CommonWiring fault in the steering column or console
  • CommonDamaged clock spring, on steering-wheel-mounted paddles
  • PossibleCorroded or disturbed connector after column or console work
  • RareFailed control module input

What you might notice

  • Manual upshift requests ignored
  • Manual mode unavailable
  • Transmission stuck in automatic mode
  • Check engine light on

What to check, in order

  1. Watch the switch state on the live data screen while operating the paddle or lever.
  2. Check whether the other direction still works. One direction failing points at that switch; both failing points at the shared wiring or the clock spring.
  3. On steering-wheel paddles, suspect the clock spring - it is the part that wears, and horn or airbag faults alongside this confirm it.
  4. Inspect the connector if the column or console has been worked on recently.
  5. If an airbag warning light is on as well, treat the restraints side as the priority and do not work in the column with the battery connected.
