P0500

Vehicle Speed Sensor "A"

Severity
Medium
Driving
Drivable — book it in
System
Powertrain
Group
Vehicle speed control, idle control and auxiliary inputs

A fault in the vehicle speed sensor "A" circuit. Road speed is used by more systems than most people expect: the speedometer, the transmission's shift decisions, cruise control, power steering assistance on some cars, and stability systems. Losing it affects several of those at once.

Possible causes

  • CommonFailed vehicle speed sensor
  • CommonDamaged wiring or connector, often from road debris
  • CommonCorroded connector under the car
  • PossibleDamaged sensor drive or reluctor ring
  • PossibleFault in a wheel speed sensor feeding the ABS module, where speed is derived from it

What you might notice

  • Speedometer reading zero or erratically
  • Harsh, late or unpredictable gear changes
  • Cruise control refusing to engage
  • ABS or stability warning lights on as well
  • Warning light on

What to check, in order

  1. Watch the speed value on the live data screen while driving slowly. Zero or a jumping value confirms the fault.
  2. Inspect the sensor connector and wiring - these live in an exposed position and suffer from salt, water and stone damage.
  3. Check whether ABS or stability codes are also stored. On many vehicles road speed comes from the ABS module's wheel speed sensors, so the real fault may be there.
  4. Check the sensor's mounting and the condition of the ring it reads.
  5. Because the transmission uses this signal to decide shifts, do not ignore this code for long.
