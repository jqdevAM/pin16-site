P0158

O2 Sensor Circuit High Voltage (Bank 2 Sensor 2)

Severity
Low
Driving
Drivable — book it in
System
Powertrain
Group
Fuel and air metering

Oxygen sensor 2 on bank 2, downstream of the catalytic converter, is reporting persistently high voltage - a rich signal. As a monitoring sensor rather than a control sensor, this rarely changes how the car drives.

Possible causes

  • CommonSensor signal wire shorted to a power supply
  • CommonFailed oxygen sensor
  • PossibleGenuinely rich mixture reaching the converter
  • PossibleSensor contaminated by oil or coolant

What you might notice

  • Check engine light on
  • Usually no drivability change
  • Failed emissions test

What to check, in order

  1. Check for upstream fuel or misfire codes first - a genuinely rich mixture will show up here too.
  2. Inspect the wiring for damage that could short the signal to power.
  3. Check the connector for water and corrosion.
  4. Replace the sensor once wiring and upstream mixture faults are ruled out.
