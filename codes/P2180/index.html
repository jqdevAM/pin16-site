P2180

System Too Rich Off Idle (Bank 2)

Severity
Medium
Driving
Drivable — book it in
System
Powertrain
Group
Fuel and air metering

The mixture on bank 2 is too rich - too much fuel for the air available - and specifically when running just off idle - light throttle, low load. The module has run out of adjustment trying to correct it. The condition named in the code is the useful part: This is the range where a vacuum leak shows most clearly but has not yet been swamped by airflow, so a leak is the first thing to look for.

Possible causes

  • CommonLeaking or stuck-open fuel injector
  • CommonFuel pressure regulator letting pressure run high
  • CommonMass airflow sensor over-reporting airflow
  • CommonRestricted air filter starving the engine of air
  • PossibleFaulty coolant temperature sensor making the module think the engine is cold
  • PossibleFailing oxygen sensor reporting rich incorrectly

What you might notice

  • Black smoke from the exhaust
  • Strong fuel smell
  • Poor fuel economy
  • Rough idle
  • Spark plugs fouled with soot

What to check, in order

  1. Check the air filter. A heavily restricted filter is the cheapest possible cause and worth eliminating first.
  2. Watch fuel trim on the live data screen - strongly negative trim confirms the module is cutting fuel to compensate.
  3. Check the coolant temperature sensor reads plausibly. A sensor stuck reading cold makes the module enrich the mixture permanently.
  4. Test fuel pressure against specification.
  5. Inspect the spark plugs - uniform soot across all of them supports a whole-bank rich condition rather than one bad injector.
