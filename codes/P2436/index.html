P2436

Secondary Air Injection System Air Flow/Pressure Sensor Circuit Range/Performance (Bank 2)

Severity
Low
Driving
Drivable — book it in
System
Powertrain
Group
Auxiliary emission controls

The secondary air flow or pressure sensor on bank 2 is answering, but the value it reports is not plausible. Either the sensor is inaccurate, or the flow genuinely is wrong - and on an older engine the second is more likely, because the air passages carbon up.

Possible causes

  • CommonCarbon blocking the air passages, so the flow genuinely is wrong
  • CommonFailing or drifting sensor
  • CommonBlocked or perished sensing hose
  • PossibleWeak air pump
  • PossibleCheck valve failed or partially blocked
  • PossibleHigh resistance in the wiring skewing the reading

What you might notice

  • Check engine light on
  • Failed emissions test
  • Pump sounding different from usual on a cold start
  • Usually no symptom the driver notices

What to check, in order

  1. Test from a cold start and watch the sensor reading while the pump runs.
  2. Establish whether the flow is genuinely wrong before blaming the sensor. A blocked passage produces a perfectly accurate reading of a real problem.
  3. Check the sensing hose for blockage and perishing.
  4. Inspect the air passages into the exhaust manifold for carbon.
  5. Check the check valve on bank 2.
