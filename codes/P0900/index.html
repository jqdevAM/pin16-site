P0900

Clutch Actuator Circuit/Open

Severity
High
Driving
Drive only to a garage
System
Powertrain
Group
Transmission and control modules

An open circuit or general electrical fault in the clutch actuator circuit. On an automated manual or dual-clutch transmission there is no clutch pedal - a motor or hydraulic actuator does the work. If the module cannot drive that actuator, the clutch cannot be operated and the car will usually not move at all.

Possible causes

  • CommonFailed clutch actuator motor
  • CommonCorroded or damaged connector at the actuator
  • CommonOpen circuit or broken wire in the actuator harness
  • PossibleBlown fuse on the actuator supply
  • PossibleWater ingress at the bellhousing
  • RareFailed control module driver

What you might notice

  • Car will not move
  • Transmission will not select a gear
  • Transmission warning message
  • Clutch not disengaging
  • Check engine light on

What to check, in order

  1. Check the fuses on the actuator supply before anything else - it is the cheapest possibility and a common one.
  2. Inspect the connector at the actuator for corrosion and water. It sits near the bellhousing, which is hot and exposed.
  3. Measure the actuator winding resistance against specification.
  4. Check continuity through the harness between the module and the actuator.
  5. Check whether the vehicle needs the clutch adaptation procedure re-running after any repair here - on these transmissions that step is routine, not optional.
