P0437

Catalyst Temperature Sensor Low (Bank 2)

Severity
Medium
Driving
Drivable — book it in
System
Powertrain
Group
Auxiliary emission controls

The catalyst temperature signal on bank 2 is reading low - typically shorted to earth or an open circuit, depending on the sensor type.

Possible causes

  • CommonFailed catalyst temperature sensor
  • CommonCorroded connector - these sit in the hottest, dirtiest part of the vehicle
  • CommonWiring damaged by exhaust heat
  • PossibleSensor seized in the exhaust and damaged during a previous removal
  • PossibleLoss of reference voltage or earth
  • PossibleGenuinely wrong exhaust temperature

What you might notice

  • Check engine light on
  • Failed emissions test
  • Slight loss of power
  • Sulphurous or rotten-egg smell from the exhaust
  • Rattling from the converter if its internals have broken up
  • Often no symptom the driver notices

What to check, in order

  1. Compare the reported temperature with ambient on a vehicle that has stood overnight - they should be close.
  2. Inspect the connector for corrosion and heat damage. These connectors live in the worst environment on the vehicle.
  3. Check the wiring where it runs along the exhaust.
  4. Check the reference voltage and earth at the sensor.
  5. Expect the sensor to be seized in the exhaust boss - penetrating oil and patience beat force.
