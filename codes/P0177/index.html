P0177

Fuel Composition Sensor Circuit Range/Performance

Severity
Medium
Driving
Drivable — book it in
System
Powertrain
Group
Fuel and air metering

The fuel composition sensor is producing a signal within its valid range that does not agree with what the module expects - for instance a reading that never changes across a complete tank of different fuel, or one that swings more than the fuel in the tank possibly could.

Possible causes

  • CommonFailed fuel composition sensor
  • CommonCorroded or wet connector at the sensor
  • CommonWiring fault between the sensor and the module
  • PossibleWater or contamination in the fuel confusing the measurement
  • PossibleSensor coated with deposits after long service

What you might notice

  • Rough running or hesitation, worst after a fuel change
  • Hard starting in cold weather
  • Loss of power
  • Poor fuel economy
  • Check engine light on

What to check, in order

  1. Read the ethanol percentage on live data. A reading stuck at one value through a full tank change, or one that jumps around while driving, both condemn the sensor.
  2. Inspect the connector for corrosion and water. This sensor lives in the fuel line, often under the vehicle, and its connector suffers accordingly.
  3. Check the wiring for chafing along its run. Underbody harnesses take a beating from road debris and salt.
  4. Rule out contaminated fuel before replacing the sensor. Water reads as a composition fault and is much cheaper to find than to overlook.
  5. Track the reading across a full tank rather than judging it on one look. This code is about how the value behaves over time, so a single snapshot cannot confirm or clear it.
