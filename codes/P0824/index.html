P0824

Gear Lever Y Position Circuit Intermittent

Severity
Medium
Driving
Drivable — book it in
System
Powertrain
Group
Transmission

The gear lever Y position signal is dropping out intermittently. The selector works, then briefly does not, then works again. The car may refuse a gear change once and behave normally afterwards. As always with intermittent circuits, connections are more likely than components.

Possible causes

  • CommonLoose or corroded connector under the console
  • CommonSticky residue from a spilled drink, intermittent as the lever moves
  • CommonWorn sensor track inside the selector
  • PossibleChafed or fatigued wiring
  • PossibleBacked-out connector pin

What you might notice

  • Occasional refusal to select a gear
  • Gear display flickering
  • Fault appearing only in certain lever positions
  • Check engine light coming and going

What to check, in order

  1. Move the lever slowly through the gate while watching the Y reading. A dropout at one specific position points at a worn track inside the selector.
  2. Wiggle-test the connector under the console.
  3. Check for a spilled drink. Sticky residue produces exactly this intermittent behaviour.
  4. Inspect the connector for a backed-out pin.
  5. Note whether the fault correlates with temperature or with the console being knocked.
