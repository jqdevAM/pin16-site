P2542

Low Pressure Fuel System Sensor Circuit High

Severity
High
Driving
Drive only to a garage
System
Powertrain
Group
Vehicle speed control, idle control and auxiliary inputs

The low pressure fuel signal is reading high - typically shorted to voltage or with a lost earth, though a restriction downstream will genuinely raise pressure.

Possible causes

  • CommonBlocked or overdue fuel filter
  • CommonWorn or failing lift pump
  • CommonFailed pressure sensor
  • CommonCorroded connector or damaged wiring
  • PossibleRestricted or kinked fuel line
  • PossibleAir leak on the suction side of the fuel system

What you might notice

  • Loss of power under load
  • Hesitation at higher speeds
  • Hard starting
  • Cutting out
  • Rough running
  • Check engine light on

What to check, in order

  1. Check the fuel filter and when it was last changed. It is the cheapest thing here and a very common cause.
  2. Compare the reported low-side pressure with specification at idle and under load.
  3. Note when the symptom appears. A fault that only shows under load or at high speed is the classic signature of a supply that cannot keep up.
  4. Inspect the connector for corrosion and fuel contamination.
  5. Look for air leaks on the suction side - air drawn into the fuel makes pressure erratic and is easy to miss.
