P0644

Driver Display Serial Communication Circuit

Severity
Low
Driving
Drivable — book it in
System
Powertrain
Group
Computer output circuits and control module

A fault in the serial communication circuit to the driver display. The module cannot send information to the display, so messages, warnings and readouts may be missing or wrong. The engine is unaffected - but you may not be told about things you would want to know.

Possible causes

  • CommonWiring or connector fault on the output circuit
  • CommonCorroded connector at either end
  • PossibleFault in the module receiving the signal, loading the circuit
  • PossibleChafed harness shorting against the body or engine
  • PossibleAftermarket equipment spliced into the circuit
  • RareFailed control module output driver

What you might notice

  • Display blank, frozen or showing wrong information
  • Warning messages not appearing
  • Check engine light on
  • Vehicle otherwise driving normally

What to check, in order

  1. Check whether the display works at all during the ignition-on self-test.
  2. Inspect the connectors at the display and the module.
  3. Check the wiring for damage, particularly if the dashboard has been worked on.
  4. Treat this as a warning-system fault: it means genuine problems may go unannounced.
