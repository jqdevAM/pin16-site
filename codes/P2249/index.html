P2249

O2 Sensor Reference Voltage Circuit Low (Bank 2 Sensor 1)

Severity
Medium
Driving
Drivable — book it in
System
Powertrain
Group
Fuel and air metering - injector circuits

The reference voltage for the upstream oxygen sensor on bank 2 is reading low - typically shorted to earth, an open circuit, or a sensor loading it down. Without a correct reference the sensor's mixture readings are unusable and the module will ignore them.

Possible causes

  • CommonReference circuit shorted to earth
  • CommonFailed oxygen sensor pulling the reference down
  • CommonCorroded or water-ingressed connector
  • PossibleChafed harness against the exhaust or subframe
  • PossibleFailed control module reference supply

What you might notice

  • Check engine light on
  • Worse fuel economy
  • Failed emissions test
  • Rough running
  • Fuel trim stuck at a default value

What to check, in order

  1. Unplug the sensor and check whether the low reading persists at the harness. If it recovers, the sensor is the fault.
  2. Inspect the connector for water and corrosion.
  3. Check the harness for chafe damage where it runs near the exhaust.
  4. Check whether both banks have faulted, which would point at a shared reference.
  5. Clear the wiring before buying a sensor.
