P0826

Up and Down Shift Switch Circuit

Severity
Low
Driving
No driving restriction
System
Powertrain
Group
Transmission

A fault in the combined up-and-down shift switch circuit - the single circuit carrying both manual shift requests on vehicles that wire them together. Automatic shifting is unaffected; manual mode will not respond.

Possible causes

  • CommonFailed switch assembly
  • CommonWiring fault in the steering column or console
  • CommonDamaged clock spring on steering-wheel-mounted switches
  • PossibleCorroded or disturbed connector
  • RareFailed control module input

What you might notice

  • Manual mode not responding in either direction
  • Paddles or lever ignored
  • Check engine light on
  • Automatic shifting normal

What to check, in order

  1. Watch the switch state on the live data screen while operating it in both directions.
  2. Both directions failing together points at shared wiring or the clock spring rather than at a switch.
  3. Inspect the connector if the column or console has been worked on.
  4. Check for horn or airbag faults alongside, which would confirm a clock spring problem.
  5. Confirm automatic shifting is normal, in which case this can wait.
