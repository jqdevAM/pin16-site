P0769

Shift Solenoid "D" Intermittent

Severity
High
Driving
Drive only to a garage
System
Powertrain
Group
Transmission

The circuit for shift solenoid "D" is faulting intermittently. The transmission drives normally most of the time and then shifts badly or drops into limp mode, usually recovering after the engine is switched off and restarted. As with all intermittent circuit faults, look at connections before components.

Possible causes

  • CommonLoose or corroded pin at the transmission case connector
  • CommonFluid wicking into the connector
  • CommonInternal transmission harness beginning to fail
  • PossibleSolenoid dropping out when hot
  • PossibleChafed wiring making intermittent contact

What you might notice

  • Occasional limp mode that clears after a restart
  • Shift quality varying between journeys
  • Symptoms appearing only once the transmission is hot
  • Check engine light coming and going

What to check, in order

  1. Note whether it only happens hot. A fault that appears after twenty minutes and is gone the next morning points at the internal harness or the solenoid.
  2. Record the freeze frame data. The fluid temperature at the moment the code set is often the most useful clue available.
  3. Pull the case connector and inspect for fluid and corrosion.
  4. Wiggle-test the external harness with the live data screen showing solenoid status.
  5. Check the fluid level and condition.
  6. Do not commit to internal repairs until the external wiring and connector are cleared.
