P0878

Transmission Fluid Pressure Sensor/Switch "D" Circuit High

Severity
High
Driving
Drive only to a garage
System
Powertrain
Group
Transmission

The signal from transmission fluid pressure sensor or switch "D" is stuck high - usually a short to voltage or a lost earth, though genuinely excessive pressure will also produce it. Excessive line pressure makes shifts violent and puts shock loads through the whole driveline.

Possible causes

  • CommonWiring shorted to voltage, or a lost sensor earth
  • CommonCorroded transmission case connector, or fluid wicking into it
  • CommonFailed pressure sensor or switch
  • PossiblePressure control solenoid stuck, producing genuinely high pressure
  • PossibleInternal transmission harness failure
  • PossibleBlocked return passage holding pressure up

What you might notice

  • Very harsh, banging shifts
  • Harsh engagement into drive or reverse
  • Transmission stuck in one gear
  • Several transmission codes together
  • Check engine light on

What to check, in order

  1. Note whether the shifts are actually harsh. If they are, the pressure may genuinely be high and the sensor is telling the truth.
  2. Check the sensor earth before assuming a shorted signal wire - a lost earth commonly drives a signal high.
  3. Unplug at the case connector and check whether the high reading persists at the harness.
  4. Inspect the connector for fluid and corrosion.
  5. Read all codes - a pressure control solenoid code alongside this one identifies the real cause.
  6. Very harsh shifts put shock loads through driveshafts and mountings. Do not drive it like this longer than necessary.
