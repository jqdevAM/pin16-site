P0118

Engine Coolant Temperature Circuit High

Severity
Medium
Driving
Drivable — book it in
System
Powertrain
Group
Fuel and air metering
Important. Do not dismiss a high-temperature indication as necessarily electrical. Confirm the actual coolant temperature before continuing to drive.

The coolant temperature sensor circuit is reading high - outside its valid range. Note that the electrical direction and the temperature direction are opposite on these sensors: resistance falls as temperature rises, so a low circuit reading corresponds to the module seeing an implausibly hot engine, and a high reading to an implausibly cold one. Either way the module falls back to a substitute value.

Possible causes

  • CommonShort or open circuit in the sensor wiring
  • CommonFailed coolant temperature sensor
  • PossibleDisconnected or corroded connector
  • RareGenuine overheating, if the reading really is that hot

What you might notice

  • Check engine light on
  • Cooling fans running constantly or never
  • Poor fuel economy
  • Harder cold starting
  • Temperature gauge at an extreme

What to check, in order

  1. Read the actual temperature value on the live data screen rather than relying on the code. An implausible extreme confirms an electrical fault; a genuinely high reading needs treating as possible overheating.
  2. If the reading really does indicate overheating, stop and check coolant level and for leaks before driving further.
  3. Check the connector is seated and free of corrosion.
  4. Check the wiring for a short to ground or an open circuit.
  5. Replace the sensor once wiring is confirmed good.
