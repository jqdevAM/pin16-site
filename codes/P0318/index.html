P0318

Rough Road Sensor "A" Signal Circuit

Severity
Low
Driving
Drivable — book it in
System
Powertrain
Group
Ignition system or misfire

A fault in the rough road sensor "A" circuit. This sensor tells the module when the road surface is rough, so that the crankshaft speed variations caused by bumps are not counted as engine misfires. With it faulty, misfire detection becomes unreliable.

Possible causes

  • CommonRough road sensor or its wiring faulty
  • CommonSensor not fitted, or removed during previous work
  • CommonModule expecting the option because it is coded for a different specification
  • PossibleABS or wheel speed fault, where the rough road signal comes from that module
  • PossibleCorroded connector

What you might notice

  • Check engine light on
  • Misfire codes appearing on poor road surfaces
  • Misfire detection disabled
  • Often no symptom the driver notices

What to check, in order

  1. Check the connector and wiring at the sensor.
  2. Check for ABS or wheel speed codes - on many vehicles the rough road signal is derived there rather than from a dedicated sensor.
  3. Treat any misfire codes stored alongside with suspicion, particularly ones that appeared on a bad road.
  4. Confirm the engine actually feels like it is misfiring before chasing misfire codes.
