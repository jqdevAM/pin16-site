P0213

Cold Start Injector 1

Severity
Medium
Driving
Drivable — book it in
System
Powertrain
Group
Fuel and air metering - injector circuits

A fault with cold start injector 1. Some engines use an extra injector that runs only during a cold start, adding fuel to help the engine catch and run smoothly before it warms up. A fault here typically shows as hard starting on a cold morning and nothing at all once the engine is warm.

Possible causes

  • CommonFailed cold start injector
  • CommonCorroded connector or damaged wiring
  • CommonBlocked injector or fuel passage
  • PossibleFaulty thermo-time switch or temperature sensor controlling it
  • PossibleInjector leaking, causing a rich cold start and flooding

What you might notice

  • Hard starting when cold
  • Engine catching and stalling on a cold start
  • Rough running until warm
  • Strong fuel smell on a cold start
  • No symptom at all once warm

What to check, in order

  1. Test from a genuinely cold start. A warm engine will start perfectly with this fault, so testing at the garage on a hot engine proves nothing.
  2. Check the connector and wiring at the injector.
  3. Measure the injector winding resistance against specification.
  4. Check the temperature sensor or switch that decides when the injector operates.
  5. If the engine floods on a cold start rather than refusing to catch, suspect the injector leaking rather than failing to open.
