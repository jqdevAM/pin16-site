P2296

Fuel Pressure Regulator 2 Control Circuit High

Severity
High
Driving
Drive only to a garage
System
Powertrain
Group
Fuel and air metering - injector circuits

The fuel pressure regulator 2 control circuit is reading high - typically shorted to voltage or a lost earth. The module cannot control fuel pressure and will restrict the engine.

Possible causes

  • CommonFailed fuel pressure regulator
  • CommonCorroded connector or damaged wiring
  • CommonBlocked fuel filter or restricted supply
  • CommonDebris in the fuel jamming the regulator
  • PossibleFailing high-pressure fuel pump
  • RareFailed control module driver

What you might notice

  • Hard starting
  • Loss of power or limp mode
  • Rough running
  • Excessive smoke on a diesel
  • Poor fuel economy
  • Check engine light on

What to check, in order

  1. Disconnect the regulator and check whether the high reading persists at the harness.
  2. Check the fuel filter and when it was last changed. A restricted supply makes a healthy regulator look faulty, and it is the cheapest thing to eliminate.
  3. Compare commanded against actual fuel pressure on the live data screen.
  4. Inspect the connector and wiring at the regulator.
  5. Measure the regulator winding resistance against specification.
  6. Get a professional assessment before replacing a high-pressure pump - it is expensive and it is frequently blamed for supply problems upstream of it.
