P0989

Transmission Fluid Pressure Sensor/Switch "E" Circuit Low

Severity
High
Driving
Drive only to a garage
System
Powertrain
Group
Transmission and control modules

The signal from transmission fluid pressure sensor or switch "E" is stuck low - usually a short to earth or an open circuit, though genuinely low pressure produces it too. The module will not trust its own shifts without this reading and falls back to a failsafe.

Possible causes

  • CommonWiring shorted to earth or open between the sensor and the module
  • CommonCorroded transmission case connector, or fluid wicking into it
  • CommonFailed pressure sensor or switch
  • CommonGenuinely low line pressure from low fluid or a worn pump
  • PossibleInternal transmission harness failure
  • PossibleLoss of the sensor reference voltage

What you might notice

  • Transmission stuck in one gear
  • Slipping
  • Harsh engagement
  • Several transmission codes together
  • Check engine light on

What to check, in order

  1. Check the fluid level and condition before assuming an electrical fault. Genuinely low pressure is a real fault, not a sensor problem.
  2. Unplug at the case connector and check whether the low reading persists at the harness side.
  3. Inspect the connector for fluid ingress and corrosion.
  4. Check the sensor reference voltage where the sensor is a variable type rather than a switch.
  5. Read all codes together - multiple pressure codes point at pressure, not at sensors.
