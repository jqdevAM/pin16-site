P0896

Shift Time Too Long

Severity
High
Driving
Drive only to a garage
System
Powertrain
Group
Transmission

Shifts are taking longer than the module expects. A shift that drags means the clutch is slipping while it engages instead of gripping cleanly, and every one of those slow shifts wears the friction material. This is the more damaging of the two shift timing codes.

Possible causes

  • CommonLow or degraded transmission fluid
  • CommonLow line pressure
  • CommonWorn clutch pack or band
  • CommonFailing pressure control solenoid
  • PossibleSticking valve in the valve body
  • PossibleInternal seal leak bleeding pressure away

What you might notice

  • Slow, drawn-out shifts
  • Engine speed flaring between gears
  • Slipping under load
  • Burnt smell
  • Limp mode

What to check, in order

  1. Check the fluid level and condition first, and look for clutch material.
  2. A line pressure test tells you whether the hydraulics can deliver what the clutches need.
  3. Read all codes. Ratio codes alongside this one identify which clutch is slipping.
  4. Check the filter if it has never been changed.
  5. Do not put off this repair. Every slow shift wears the clutch further, and the fault gets worse at an accelerating rate.
