P0809

Clutch Position Sensor Circuit Intermittent

Severity
Medium
Driving
Drivable — book it in
System
Powertrain
Group
Transmission

The clutch position signal is dropping out intermittently. The car may drive normally for a while and then engage harshly or refuse to move, recovering later. Intermittent faults of this kind are nearly always connections or wiring rather than the sensor itself.

Possible causes

  • CommonLoose, corroded or backed-out connector pin
  • CommonChafed or fatigued wiring near the bellhousing
  • PossibleFailing sensor that drops out when hot
  • PossibleWater ingress into the connector
  • PossibleSticking actuator producing erratic movement

What you might notice

  • Occasional harsh engagement or judder
  • Transmission occasionally refusing to engage, then recovering
  • Fault linked to heat, bumps or wet weather
  • Check engine light coming and going

What to check, in order

  1. Note when it happens. Hot points at the sensor, bumps point at wiring, wet points at the connector - that observation is worth more than any single measurement.
  2. Wiggle-test the harness with the live data screen showing clutch position.
  3. Inspect the connector for a pin that has backed out of its housing.
  4. Run the clutch calibration procedure and see whether the fault returns.
  5. Do not replace the sensor on an intermittent code alone - confirm the dropout at the sensor.
