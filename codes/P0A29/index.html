P0A29

Battery Power Off Circuit High

Severity
High
Driving
Drive only to a garage
System
Powertrain
Group
Hybrid propulsion
Important. High-voltage hazard. Do not disconnect, cut or probe anything orange, and do not remove high-voltage covers. Diagnosis beyond the 12 volt supply is not DIY work on these systems.

The battery power off circuit on a hybrid or electric vehicle is reading high - typically shorted to voltage or a lost earth. The vehicle cannot confirm control of its high-voltage shutdown, so it will refuse to bring the high-voltage system up.

Possible causes

  • CommonWiring shorted to voltage or a lost earth in the shutdown circuit
  • CommonCorroded or damaged connector
  • CommonLow 12 volt battery voltage
  • PossibleFailed contactor control circuit
  • PossibleFailed battery management module

What you might notice

  • Vehicle will not power up or move
  • Will not charge
  • High-voltage system warning
  • Hybrid running on the engine alone

What to check, in order

  1. Check the 12 volt battery and its terminals before anything else.
  2. Check for disturbed high-voltage service plugs or covers.
  3. Read all codes for battery management faults alongside.
  4. Stop at the 12 volt system and the fuse box. Everything beyond that requires the manufacturer's high-voltage procedure.
