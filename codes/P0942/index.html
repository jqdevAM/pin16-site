P0942

Hydraulic Pressure Unit

Severity
High
Driving
Drive only to a garage
System
Powertrain
Group
Transmission and control modules

A general fault with the hydraulic pressure unit - the pump, accumulator and valve assembly that provides the pressure the clutch and selector actuators run on. Nothing in this transmission moves without it, so a fault here usually stops the car changing gear at all.

Possible causes

  • CommonLow hydraulic fluid level or a leak
  • CommonFailed accumulator no longer holding pressure
  • CommonWorn or failing hydraulic pump
  • CommonFailed pump relay or supply fault
  • PossibleBlocked filter in the hydraulic circuit
  • PossibleFaulty pressure sensor misreporting the unit's state

What you might notice

  • Transmission will not change gear
  • Car will not move
  • Pump running constantly or not running at all
  • Transmission warning message
  • Fluid leak near the transmission

What to check, in order

  1. Check the hydraulic fluid level and look for leaks first. It is visible from underneath and it is the most common cause.
  2. Listen for the pump. Silence points at the relay or supply; constant running points at a leak or a failed accumulator.
  3. Watch the pressure reading through a pump cycle on the live data screen.
  4. Check the pump relay and its fuse.
  5. Read all codes - the more specific pressure unit and pump codes narrow this down considerably.
