P0914

Gear Shift Position Circuit

Severity
High
Driving
Drive only to a garage
System
Powertrain
Group
Transmission and control modules

A fault in the gear shift position circuit. This is the other axis of the selector shaft - the fore and aft movement that actually engages a gear once the gate has been chosen. Without a believable position reading the module cannot confirm a gear is engaged, so it will not complete the shift.

Possible causes

  • CommonFailed gear shift position sensor
  • CommonCorroded or damaged connector at the gearbox
  • CommonWiring damage in the actuator harness
  • CommonSensor not calibrated after a repair
  • PossibleLoss of reference voltage or earth
  • PossibleWater ingress at the gearbox connector

What you might notice

  • Transmission will not engage a gear
  • Car stuck in neutral
  • Gear display blank or wrong
  • Transmission warning message
  • Check engine light on

What to check, in order

  1. Watch the gear shift position on the live data screen during an attempted change. A reading that does not move names the fault.
  2. Check the reference voltage and earth at the sensor.
  3. Inspect the connector at the gearbox for corrosion and water.
  4. Run the position calibration procedure - routine after any actuator work on these transmissions.
  5. Read all codes. Gate select and gear shift codes together usually point at a shared supply, earth or connector rather than at two failed sensors.
