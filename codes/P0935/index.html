P0935

Hydraulic Pressure Sensor Circuit High

Severity
High
Driving
Drive only to a garage
System
Powertrain
Group
Transmission and control modules

The hydraulic pressure signal is stuck high - typically shorted to voltage or a lost earth. Without a usable pressure reading the module will not attempt gear changes it cannot guarantee, so the car may refuse to move.

Possible causes

  • CommonWiring shorted to voltage or a lost earth between the sensor and the module
  • CommonCorroded or fluid-contaminated connector
  • CommonFailed pressure sensor
  • CommonGenuinely low pressure from a leak or failed pump
  • PossibleLoss of the reference voltage or the sensor earth

What you might notice

  • Car will not move or will not change gear
  • Pump running constantly or not at all
  • Transmission warning message
  • Check engine light on

What to check, in order

  1. Check the hydraulic fluid level and look for leaks before assuming an electrical fault. Genuinely low pressure is a real fault, not a sensor problem.
  2. Unplug the sensor and check whether the high reading persists at the harness.
  3. Check the reference voltage and earth.
  4. Inspect the connector for corrosion and fluid contamination.
  5. Read all codes for pump or pressure unit faults alongside.
