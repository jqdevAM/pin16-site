P2437

Secondary Air Injection System Air Flow/Pressure Sensor Circuit Low (Bank 2)

Severity
Low
Driving
Drivable — book it in
System
Powertrain
Group
Auxiliary emission controls

The secondary air flow or pressure sensor signal on bank 2 is stuck low - typically shorted to earth or an open circuit, though genuinely absent airflow will produce it too. The module cannot confirm the system worked.

Possible causes

  • CommonWiring shorted to earth or open between the sensor and the module
  • CommonCorroded or water-ingressed connector
  • CommonFailed sensor
  • CommonNo airflow at all - a failed pump or a completely blocked passage
  • PossibleLoss of the sensor reference voltage

What you might notice

  • Check engine light on
  • Failed emissions test
  • No pump noise on a cold start, in some cases
  • Usually no symptom the driver notices

What to check, in order

  1. Listen for the pump on a cold start. Silence means the low reading is correct and the fault is the pump or its supply, not the sensor.
  2. Unplug the sensor and check whether the low reading persists at the harness.
  3. Check the reference voltage and earth at the sensor.
  4. Inspect the connector for corrosion and water.
  5. Check the harness for heat damage near the exhaust.
