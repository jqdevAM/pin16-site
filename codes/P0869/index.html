P0869

Transmission Fluid Pressure High

Severity
High
Driving
Drive only to a garage
System
Powertrain
Group
Transmission

Transmission fluid pressure is higher than it should be. The clutches are being clamped harder than intended, so shifts arrive abruptly and the whole driveline takes a shock each time. It is less immediately destructive than low pressure, but it is hard on mountings, driveshafts and the transmission itself.

Possible causes

  • CommonFailed or stuck pressure control solenoid
  • CommonSticking valve in the valve body
  • CommonModule defaulting to maximum pressure because of another fault
  • PossibleBlocked return passage
  • PossibleFaulty pressure sensor reporting high
  • PossibleWrong fluid specification affecting valve behaviour

What you might notice

  • Very harsh, banging shifts
  • Harsh engagement into drive or reverse
  • A jolt through the car on every shift
  • Limp mode
  • Check engine light on

What to check, in order

  1. Read all codes first. Modules deliberately raise line pressure to maximum as a protective failsafe when something else has faulted, so this code is often a consequence rather than a cause.
  2. Check the fluid level, condition and specification.
  3. Watch commanded against actual pressure on the live data screen if the vehicle reports both.
  4. A pressure test confirms whether the pressure is genuinely high or the sensor is wrong.
  5. Drive gently until it is fixed. Every harsh shift is a shock load through the driveline.
