P0668

PCM/ECM/TCM Internal Temperature Sensor Circuit Low

Severity
Medium
Driving
Drivable — book it in
System
Powertrain
Group
Computer output circuits and control module

The module's internal temperature signal is stuck low - typically an internal circuit fault, or a genuinely very low reading. Since this sensor is inside the module, the wiring you can reach is not the issue; what can be checked is whether the module is genuinely being overheated.

Possible causes

  • CommonModule mounted where it gets little airflow, or a blocked cooling path
  • CommonA shorted output making the module work far harder than it should
  • CommonCorroded connector causing high resistance and local heating
  • CommonFailed internal temperature sensor
  • PossibleVery high ambient temperature combined with heavy load
  • PossibleGenuine internal module failure

What you might notice

  • Check engine light on
  • Limp mode in some cases
  • Fault appearing in hot weather
  • Often no symptom the driver notices

What to check, in order

  1. Check whether the module is actually hot. Feel its casing after a drive - a module too hot to hold comfortably is telling the truth.
  2. Read all codes. A shorted output stored alongside would explain genuine heating.
  3. Check the supply voltage and the connector before concluding the module has failed.
