P0837

Four Wheel Drive (4WD) Switch Circuit Range/Performance

Severity
Medium
Driving
Drivable — book it in
System
Powertrain
Group
Transmission

The four-wheel drive switch is answering but its reading is not plausible - two positions reading the same, or a position reported that the selector is not in. Usually this is worn switch contacts or a linkage that is not moving the switch fully rather than an outright failure.

Possible causes

  • CommonWorn switch contacts giving an ambiguous position
  • CommonSwitch out of adjustment or its linkage worn
  • CommonCorroded connector
  • PossibleActuator not completing its travel
  • PossibleWiring fault causing a partial short between circuits

What you might notice

  • Drive mode indicator flickering or wrong
  • Four-wheel drive engaging when not requested, or not engaging
  • Mode changes taking a long time
  • Check engine light on

What to check, in order

  1. Move the selector slowly through every position while watching the reported state. Look for a position that is briefly ambiguous - the classic sign of worn contacts.
  2. Check the switch adjustment and its linkage for wear.
  3. Inspect the connector for corrosion and for a partial short between adjacent pins.
  4. Confirm the actuator completes its travel.
  5. Check for transfer case codes alongside.
