P0785

Shift/Timing Solenoid

Severity
High
Driving
Drive only to a garage
System
Powertrain
Group
Transmission

A fault in the shift/timing solenoid circuit. This solenoid controls the timing of shifts - how quickly pressure builds as a gear engages - so a fault here tends to show as shifts that are harsh, slow, or overlapping rather than as a gear being missing entirely.

Possible causes

  • CommonFailed shift/timing solenoid
  • CommonDebris jamming the solenoid
  • CommonCorroded case connector or fluid wicking into it
  • CommonLow or degraded fluid
  • PossibleInternal transmission harness failure
  • PossibleWiring damage between the module and the transmission

What you might notice

  • Harsh or drawn-out shifts
  • Shifts overlapping or flaring
  • Limp mode
  • Check engine light on

What to check, in order

  1. Check the fluid level and condition first.
  2. Read all transmission codes together - several solenoid codes point at a shared cause.
  3. Inspect the case connector for fluid and corrosion.
  4. Measure the solenoid resistance against specification where accessible.
  5. Check whether an adaptation reset is specified after repair.
