P2298

O2 Sensor Out of Range During Deceleration (Bank 2 Sensor 1)

Severity
Medium
Driving
Drivable — book it in
System
Powertrain
Group
Fuel and air metering - injector circuits

The upstream oxygen sensor on bank 2 gave a reading outside the expected range while the car was decelerating. When you lift off, most engines cut fuel entirely, so the exhaust should read very lean for a moment - a sensor that does not show that is either slow, contaminated, or being fooled by an exhaust leak.

Possible causes

  • CommonAged or slow oxygen sensor
  • CommonExhaust leak upstream of the sensor
  • CommonSensor contaminated by oil, coolant or silicone
  • PossibleFuel injector leaking, so fuel is present when it should not be
  • PossibleWiring or connector fault
  • PossibleFuel pressure regulator leaking fuel into the intake

What you might notice

  • Check engine light on
  • Failed emissions test
  • Worse fuel economy
  • Hesitation on the overrun
  • Often no symptom the driver notices

What to check, in order

  1. Watch the sensor on the live data screen while lifting off at speed. It should swing sharply lean as fuel is cut - a lazy or absent swing is the fault.
  2. Check for exhaust leaks upstream of the sensor.
  3. Consider a leaking injector. Fuel arriving when the module has cut it produces exactly this, and it is a very different repair from a sensor.
  4. Compare the two banks. Only one affected points at that sensor or that side's exhaust.
  5. Check for silicone contamination if sealant was used recently.
