P2510

ECM/PCM Power Relay Sense Circuit Range/Performance

Severity
High
Driving
Drive only to a garage
System
Powertrain
Group
Vehicle speed control, idle control and auxiliary inputs

The engine module's power relay sense circuit is reporting something implausible - the relay's actual state does not match what was commanded, or the timing is wrong. Usually the relay contacts are sticking or slow, which is a mechanical failure inside the relay rather than a wiring problem.

Possible causes

  • CommonFailed power relay with worn or sticking contacts
  • CommonCorroded relay socket or fuse box connections
  • CommonWater ingress into the fuse box
  • PossibleWiring fault on the sense circuit
  • PossibleWeak battery making relay operation marginal
  • RareFailed module input

What you might notice

  • Intermittent limp mode
  • Engine occasionally dead at start-up
  • Battery draining after standing
  • Check engine light on

What to check, in order

  1. Swap the relay with an identical one from elsewhere in the box and see whether the fault follows it. It costs nothing and is the fastest test available.
  2. Inspect the relay socket for corrosion and heat damage - a discoloured socket means the relay has been running hot.
  3. Check the fuse box for water ingress, particularly if it is mounted low or in the engine bay.
  4. Check battery voltage - a weak battery makes a marginal relay behave like a failing one.
  5. If the battery goes flat overnight, investigate this rather than replacing the battery. A relay that will not release keeps the module powered all night.
