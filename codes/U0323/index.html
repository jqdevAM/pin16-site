U0323

Software Incompatibility with Instrument Panel Control Module

Severity
High
Driving
Short journeys only

The software in the instrument panel control module is not compatible with the rest of the vehicle. The instrument cluster is not just gauges - it is where nearly every warning lamp is displayed. With it incorrectly configured you may have no speedometer, no fuel gauge, and no warning if the engine overheats or loses oil pressure. The car can drive perfectly while doing something expensive that you cannot see.

Possible causes

  • CommonCluster replaced but never coded to this vehicle
  • CommonUsed cluster fitted from a different vehicle, market or specification
  • CommonSoftware update interrupted during programming
  • CommonWrong part number - clusters vary by market, units and option list
  • CommonMileage or configuration data not transferred during replacement

What you might notice

  • Gauges dead, stuck or reading nonsense
  • Warning lights all on, or all off
  • No speedometer
  • Display in the wrong units or language
  • Odometer blank or implausible

What to check, in order

  1. Recognise what has been lost. Without warning lamps you get no notice of overheating or low oil pressure, so keep journeys short and check the temperature gauge and oil level by other means until it is fixed.
  2. Driving without a working speedometer is an offence in most jurisdictions and an inspection failure in nearly all of them.
  3. Ask what was done. A cluster replacement is almost always the explanation.
  4. Check the part number against the vehicle's market and specification - units, language and option list all vary.
  5. Cluster coding includes the odometer reading and is regulated work in most jurisdictions. Do not treat it as a plug-in swap, and expect a dealer or a properly authorised specialist to want proof of ownership.
