P0787

Shift/Timing Solenoid Low

Severity
High
Driving
Drive only to a garage
System
Powertrain
Group
Transmission

The shift/timing solenoid circuit is reading low - typically shorted to earth or open circuit. The module cannot control shift timing and will fall back to a failsafe, which usually means harsh shifts or a single fixed gear.

Possible causes

  • CommonWiring shorted to earth or open circuit between the module and the solenoid
  • CommonCorroded case connector, or fluid wicking into it
  • CommonOpen or shorted solenoid winding
  • PossibleInternal transmission harness failure
  • RareFailed transmission control module driver

What you might notice

  • Transmission stuck in one gear
  • Harsh shifts
  • Several transmission codes stored together
  • Check engine light on

What to check, in order

  1. Disconnect at the case connector and check whether the low reading persists at the harness side. If it does, the fault is external to the transmission.
  2. Inspect the connector for fluid ingress and corroded pins.
  3. Measure the solenoid winding resistance against specification.
  4. Check the harness for chafe damage between the module and the transmission.
  5. Read all codes - several solenoid electrical codes at once point at the internal harness or connector.
