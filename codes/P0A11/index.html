P0A11

DC/DC Converter Enable Circuit/Open

Severity
High
Driving
Drive only to a garage
System
Powertrain
Group
Hybrid propulsion
Important. The 12 volt battery is not being charged. The vehicle will run until it is flat and then stop. Do not start a journey with this stored.

The circuit the module uses to switch the DC/DC converter on is open - a broken wire, a disconnected plug, or a failed input on the converter. This is the command side rather than the reporting side: the converter may be perfectly healthy and simply never told to run. Either way the 12 volt system is not being charged, and on a hybrid or electric vehicle nothing else charges it.

Possible causes

  • CommonBroken wire or disconnected plug on the enable circuit
  • CommonCorroded connector
  • CommonBlown fuse in the enable circuit
  • PossibleFailed DC/DC converter input stage
  • PossibleLow 12 volt battery voltage, so the module never completes its start-up
  • RareFailed driver inside the control module

What you might notice

  • 12 volt battery going flat, especially overnight
  • Vehicle refusing to power up
  • Several unrelated warning lights at once
  • Vehicle powering down while driving
  • Hybrid or electric drive system warning message

What to check, in order

  1. Measure the 12 volt system voltage with the vehicle powered up and ready. If it sits near battery voltage rather than around 14 volts, the converter is not running.
  2. Check the 12 volt battery first - it is the most common cause of scattered hybrid system codes.
  3. Check the enable circuit's fuse and its continuity from the module to the converter.
  4. Read all codes. A converter status code stored alongside this one usually means the converter is fine and the command never reaches it.
  5. Stop at the 12 volt system and the fuse box.
