P2757

Torque Converter Clutch Pressure Control Solenoid Control Circuit Performance or Stuck Off

Severity
Medium
Driving
Drivable — book it in
System
Powertrain
Group
Transmission

The torque converter clutch pressure solenoid is electrically sound but not applying the clutch as commanded. This produces the classic converter shudder: a vibration at light throttle around cruising speed that feels very like a light misfire.

Possible causes

  • CommonFailed torque converter clutch pressure control solenoid
  • CommonLow or degraded transmission fluid
  • CommonDebris in the fluid jamming the solenoid or a valve
  • CommonCorroded case connector, or fluid wicking into it
  • CommonWorn torque converter clutch lining
  • PossibleInternal transmission harness failure

What you might notice

  • Shudder or vibration at a light steady throttle
  • Higher engine speed at a steady cruise than usual
  • Worse fuel consumption
  • Transmission running hot
  • Stalling when coming to a stop
  • Check engine light on

What to check, in order

  1. Rule out an engine misfire. A light misfire at cruise feels almost identical from the driver's seat and the fix is completely different.
  2. Ask what fluid was fitted at the last service. Fluid of the wrong specification is a frequent cause of converter shudder.
  3. Check the fluid level and condition first. It is the cheapest check and it explains more transmission complaints than every electrical fault combined.
