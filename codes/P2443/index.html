P2443

Secondary Air Injection System Switching Valve Stuck Closed (Bank 2)

Severity
Low
Driving
Drivable — book it in
System
Powertrain
Group
Auxiliary emission controls

The secondary air injection switching valve on bank 2 is stuck closed. The pump may be running perfectly, but its air cannot reach the exhaust, so the system does nothing. Unlike a valve stuck open, this causes no running problem - it simply means higher emissions for the first minute of each journey.

Possible causes

  • CommonCarbon blocking the valve
  • CommonFailed valve solenoid or diaphragm
  • CommonNo vacuum reaching a vacuum-operated valve - split hose or failed solenoid
  • PossibleCorroded valve seized shut
  • PossibleWiring or connector fault, so the valve is never commanded

What you might notice

  • Check engine light on
  • Failed emissions test
  • Pump running on a cold start but no change in exhaust note
  • Usually no symptom the driver notices

What to check, in order

  1. Test from a genuinely cold start.
  2. Confirm the valve is being commanded electrically before condemning it - a valve that is never told to open behaves exactly like one that is stuck.
  3. On a vacuum-operated valve, apply vacuum directly with a hand pump and watch it move. If it opens and holds vacuum, the valve is fine and the fault is upstream.
  4. Remove and inspect the valve for carbon and corrosion.
  5. Check the air passages into the exhaust manifold while the valve is off.
