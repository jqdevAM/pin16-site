U Network and communication

The vehicle's data buses and the modules on them. These codes usually indicate a module that has stopped responding or a wiring fault on the bus, rather than a mechanical failure.

  • U00xxNetwork electrical98 codes
  • U01xxNetwork communication100 codes
  • U02xxNetwork communication36 codes
  • U03xxNetwork software32 codes
  • U04xxNetwork data32 codes

Read this code from your own car

Plug an ELM327 adapter into the OBD-II port under the dashboard. Pin16 reads every stored, pending and permanent code, explains each one like this page does — offline, with no account — and shows what the car recorded when the fault was set.
