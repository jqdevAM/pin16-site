P0723

Output Speed Sensor Circuit Intermittent

Severity
High
Driving
Drive only to a garage
System
Powertrain
Group
Transmission

The signal from the output speed sensor is dropping out intermittently - present, then briefly absent, then back. The module usually reacts by dropping into limp mode for the rest of the journey and recovering after a restart, which is why this fault often presents as a transmission that misbehaves once and then seems fine.

Possible causes

  • CommonLoose, corroded or backed-out connector pin
  • CommonChafed or fatigued wiring making contact until it moves
  • CommonMetal debris intermittently bridging the sensor tip
  • PossibleFailing sensor that drops out when hot
  • PossibleDamaged reluctor ring with one bad tooth
  • PossibleInternal harness beginning to fail

What you might notice

  • Transmission drops into limp mode occasionally and recovers after a restart
  • Momentary harsh shift or flare
  • Speedometer needle dropping briefly
  • Fault linked to bumps, heat or wet weather

What to check, in order

  1. Note the conditions. Over bumps means wiring, when hot means the sensor, in the wet means the connector - this single observation is the most useful diagnostic step available for an intermittent fault.
  2. Wiggle-test the harness and connector with the live data screen showing the speed reading.
  3. Inspect the connector for a backed-out pin and for corrosion.
  4. Inspect the sensor tip for debris.
  5. Check the fluid level and condition.
  6. Resist replacing the sensor on the strength of an intermittent code alone - confirm the dropout at the sensor first.
