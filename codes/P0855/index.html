P0855

Drive Switch Input Circuit High

Severity
High
Driving
Drivable — book it in
System
Powertrain
Group
Transmission

The signal from the drive switch is stuck high - typically shorted to voltage or an open circuit. The module gets the same answer whatever the selector is doing, so it cannot tell which position has been chosen. Starting and the shift interlock are the first things affected.

Possible causes

  • CommonWiring shorted to voltage or an open circuit between the switch and the module
  • CommonFailed switch or transmission range sensor
  • CommonCorroded or water-filled connector at the transmission
  • PossibleSwitch out of adjustment so it never changes state
  • PossibleChafed harness under the vehicle

What you might notice

  • Engine will not crank at all, or cranks in any position
  • Gear display wrong
  • Reversing lamps wrong
  • Transmission behaving as though the wrong gear were selected
  • Check engine light on

What to check, in order

  1. Watch the switch state through the full selector travel. A reading that never changes confirms the fault.
  2. Unplug at the transmission and check whether the high reading persists at the harness.
  3. Check the switch or range sensor adjustment before condemning it.
  4. Inspect the connector for corrosion and water - it sits low and takes constant road spray.
  5. If the engine cranks with a gear selected, stop using the vehicle until this is repaired.
