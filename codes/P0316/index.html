P0316

Engine Misfire Detected on Startup (First 1000 Revolutions)

Severity
Medium
Driving
Drivable — book it in
System
Powertrain
Group
Ignition system or misfire

A misfire was detected during the first moments after starting - within roughly the first thousand engine revolutions. Faults that only appear on a cold start often disappear once the engine warms, which makes them easy to dismiss, but the module logs them because they point at something real.

Possible causes

  • CommonWorn spark plugs that only fail to fire when cold
  • CommonWeak ignition coil breaking down under cold-start load
  • PossibleLeaking fuel injector flooding a cylinder overnight
  • PossibleLow fuel pressure at start-up from a failing pump check valve
  • RareValve or piston ring wear reducing cold compression

What you might notice

  • Rough running for the first few seconds after a cold start
  • Engine smooths out once warm
  • Check engine light that may come and go

What to check, in order

  1. Note whether the roughness only happens on a cold start. That pattern is the main clue and worth recording before you change anything.
  2. Inspect the spark plugs - cold-start-only misfires often come from plugs near the end of their life.
  3. Check for a fuel smell at start-up, which suggests an injector leaking down overnight.
  4. If the engine has high mileage and cranks unevenly, a compression test is worth doing.
