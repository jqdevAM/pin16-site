P0884

TCM Power Input Signal Intermittent

Severity
High
Driving
Drive only to a garage
System
Powertrain
Group
Transmission

The transmission module's supply voltage is dropping out or fluctuating intermittently. The transmission may behave perfectly for days and then shift harshly or drop into limp mode. Intermittent supply faults are usually a connection - a terminal, an earth or a relay - rather than a failed component.

Possible causes

  • CommonLoose or corroded battery terminal
  • CommonCorroded earth point
  • CommonFailing relay with worn contacts
  • PossibleChafed or fatigued supply wiring
  • PossibleFailing alternator with an intermittent output
  • PossibleBattery on the point of failing

What you might notice

  • Occasional limp mode that clears after a restart
  • Shift quality varying between journeys
  • Momentary flicker of the dashboard lights
  • Check engine light coming and going

What to check, in order

  1. Wiggle-test the battery terminals and the main earth straps with the engine running while watching voltage on the live data screen.
  2. Have the battery load tested. A battery on the way out produces intermittent faults across the whole vehicle before it fails outright.
  3. Swap the supply relay for an identical one elsewhere in the box and see whether the fault follows it.
  4. Perform a voltage drop test on the supply and earth circuits.
  5. Record the freeze frame data. Knowing what the voltage was when the code set often identifies the problem straight away.
