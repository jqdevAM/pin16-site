P0888

TCM Power Relay Sense Circuit

Severity
High
Driving
Drive only to a garage
System
Powertrain
Group
Transmission

A fault in the power relay sense circuit. This is the wire the module uses to check whether the relay actually did what it was told - a feedback line rather than a control line. The module commanded the relay and cannot confirm the result, so it will not trust its own power supply.

Possible causes

  • CommonFailed power relay with worn or stuck contacts
  • CommonCorroded relay socket
  • CommonWiring fault on the sense circuit
  • PossibleWater ingress into the fuse box
  • RareFailed module input

What you might notice

  • Transmission in limp mode
  • Intermittent loss of transmission function
  • Battery draining after standing
  • Check engine light on

What to check, in order

  1. Swap the relay with an identical one and see whether the fault follows.
  2. Inspect the relay socket for corrosion and heat damage.
  3. Check continuity on the sense wire between the relay and the module.
  4. Check the fuse box for water.
  5. Read the other relay codes stored. Control and sense codes together usually mean the relay itself.
