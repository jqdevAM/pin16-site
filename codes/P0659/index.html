P0659

Actuator Supply Voltage "A" Circuit High

Severity
High
Driving
Drive only to a garage
System
Powertrain
Group
Computer output circuits and control module

Actuator supply voltage "A" is reading high - the supply to a group of actuators is above where it should be, usually because it has been shorted to a higher voltage or has lost its earth reference. Actuators on that supply may operate when they should not.

Possible causes

  • CommonSupply circuit shorted to battery voltage or another supply
  • CommonChafed harness bridging the supply to a heavier circuit
  • CommonCorrosion in a connector bridging adjacent pins
  • PossibleOvercharging from a charging system fault
  • PossibleFailed control module supply driver

What you might notice

  • Several actuator codes at once
  • Actuators operating when they should not
  • Limp mode
  • Check engine light on

What to check, in order

  1. Check charging voltage first. If the whole vehicle is being overcharged, this code is a symptom of that and the charging fault is the one to fix.
  2. Look for a short to a supply wire on the actuator supply circuit.
  3. Inspect connectors for corrosion bridging adjacent pins.
  4. Check the harness for chafe damage where the supply runs alongside heavier circuits.
  5. Fix the supply before diagnosing the actuator codes stored alongside.
