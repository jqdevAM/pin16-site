P0517

Battery Temperature Sensor Circuit High

Severity
Medium
Driving
Drivable — book it in
System
Powertrain
Group
Vehicle speed control, idle control and auxiliary inputs

The battery temperature sensor signal is stuck high - typically open circuit or shorted to voltage. The module has no usable battery temperature and will fall back to a default charging voltage, which is safe but not optimal for battery life.

Possible causes

  • CommonWiring open circuit or shorted to voltage between the sensor and the module
  • CommonFailed sensor
  • CommonCorroded or acid-damaged connector
  • CommonSensor disconnected during battery replacement
  • PossibleLoss of reference voltage or earth

What you might notice

  • Battery warning light on
  • Temperature reading implausible
  • Check engine light on
  • Usually no symptom the driver notices

What to check, in order

  1. Check the sensor is connected. After a battery change this is the answer more often than not.
  2. Unplug the sensor and check whether the high reading persists at the harness.
  3. Inspect the connector for corrosion and acid damage.
  4. Measure the sensor resistance against specification at a known temperature.
  5. Check charging voltage against specification while you are there.
