P0829

5-6 Shift

Severity
High
Driving
Drive only to a garage
System
Powertrain
Group
Transmission

The 5-6 shift did not complete as commanded - it did not happen, took too long, or flared. Naming the specific shift is useful: it points at the particular clutch, band and solenoid that shift depends on rather than at the transmission as a whole.

Possible causes

  • CommonLow or degraded transmission fluid
  • CommonFailed or sticking shift solenoid for the 5-6 shift
  • CommonWorn clutch pack or band used by sixth gear
  • CommonSticking valve in the valve body
  • PossibleLow line pressure
  • PossibleInternal seal leak in that clutch circuit

What you might notice

  • Engine speed flaring during the 5-6 shift
  • Transmission not reaching sixth gear
  • Harsh bang on the 5-6 shift
  • Higher motorway revs and worse fuel economy
  • Check engine light on

What to check, in order

  1. Check the fluid level and condition first.
  2. Confirm what actually happens on the 5-6 shift. Flaring means the clutch is not gripping; a bang means it is arriving too hard; nothing at all points at the solenoid or valve.
  3. Read all codes. A solenoid code alongside this one usually names the cause directly.
  4. Check for a gear 6 ratio code, which would confirm the clutch is slipping rather than the shift not being commanded.
  5. A pressure test tells you whether the hydraulics can deliver what that clutch needs.
