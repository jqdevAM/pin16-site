P2153

Fuel Injector Group "C" Supply Voltage Circuit Low

Severity
High
Driving
Drive only to a garage
System
Powertrain
Group
Fuel and air metering

The supply voltage circuit for fuel injector group "C" is reading low - typically a short to earth, an open circuit, or one injector loading the supply. The injectors in that group will not open properly, so those cylinders will misfire or not fire at all.

Possible causes

  • CommonSupply circuit shorted to earth
  • CommonOne injector shorted internally
  • CommonBlown fuse or failing relay
  • CommonHigh resistance in the supply wiring or a corroded connector
  • PossibleChafed harness against the engine
  • PossibleWeak battery or charging fault

What you might notice

  • Severe misfire on several cylinders
  • Loss of power
  • Engine will not start
  • Check engine light flashing

What to check, in order

  1. Check battery and charging voltage first, then the fuses and relays.
  2. Disconnect the injectors in group "C" one at a time while watching the supply voltage. Recovery identifies the shorted injector.
  3. Measure each injector winding resistance against specification.
  4. Perform a voltage drop test on the supply circuit.
  5. If the check engine light is flashing, stop driving - a severe misfire destroys the catalytic converter quickly.
