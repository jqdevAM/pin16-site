P0320

Ignition/Distributor Engine Speed Input Circuit

Severity
High
Driving
Drive only to a garage
System
Powertrain
Group
Ignition system or misfire

A fault in the ignition or distributor engine speed input circuit. On a distributor-equipped engine this is how the module knows engine speed and firing position - the equivalent of the crankshaft sensor on a distributorless engine.

Possible causes

  • CommonFailed pickup or sensor inside the distributor
  • CommonWorn distributor shaft or bearing
  • CommonCorroded connector or damaged wiring
  • PossibleFailed ignition module
  • PossibleMoisture inside the distributor cap

What you might notice

  • Engine will not start
  • Cutting out while driving
  • Rough running or misfire
  • Rev counter erratic
  • Check engine light on

What to check, in order

  1. Check inside the distributor cap for moisture, cracks and corroded contacts - the cheapest thing to eliminate.
  2. Check the connector and wiring at the distributor.
  3. Watch engine speed on the live data screen while cranking.
  4. Check the distributor shaft for play. A worn shaft moves the pickup out of position and produces an erratic signal that no new sensor will fix.
