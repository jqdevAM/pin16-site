P0931

Gear Shift Lock Solenoid Control Circuit High

Severity
High
Driving
Drivable — book it in
System
Powertrain
Group
Transmission and control modules

The gear shift lock solenoid circuit is reading high - typically shorted to voltage or a lost earth. The module cannot control the park lock, so the lever may be trapped in park or may not be locked at all.

Possible causes

  • CommonWiring shorted to voltage or a lost earth between the module and the solenoid
  • CommonCorroded or damaged connector under the console
  • CommonOpen or shorted solenoid winding
  • PossibleBlown fuse on the circuit
  • RareFailed control module driver

What you might notice

  • Cannot move the selector out of park
  • Lever moves out of park without the brake pedal
  • Check engine light on

What to check, in order

  1. Disconnect the solenoid and check whether the high reading persists at the harness.
  2. Measure the solenoid winding resistance against specification.
  3. Inspect the connector under the console.
  4. Check the fuse and the brake switch circuit.
  5. If the lever is trapped, use the manual override release described in the handbook rather than forcing it - forcing the lever breaks the detent mechanism and turns an electrical fault into a mechanical one.
