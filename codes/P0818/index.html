P0818

Driveline Disconnect Switch Input Circuit

Severity
Medium
Driving
Drivable — book it in
System
Powertrain
Group
Transmission

A fault in the driveline disconnect switch input circuit. This switch tells the module whether the driveline is connected - on four-wheel drive and part-time systems, whether the front axle or transfer case is engaged. Without a believable reading the module cannot know how the vehicle is driven, so it may refuse to change drive modes or may default to one.

Possible causes

  • CommonFailed or misadjusted disconnect switch
  • CommonCorroded connector - these switches live underneath in road spray and salt
  • CommonWiring damage under the vehicle
  • PossibleActuator not moving fully, so the switch never sees the engaged position
  • PossibleWorn or damaged linkage

What you might notice

  • Four-wheel drive will not engage or disengage
  • Drive mode indicator wrong or flashing
  • Stability or traction control behaving oddly
  • Check engine light on

What to check, in order

  1. Watch the switch state on the live data screen while changing drive mode.
  2. Inspect the switch and its connector underneath. Corrosion here is the most common cause by a wide margin.
  3. Check the actuator actually completes its travel. A switch reporting correctly on a mechanism that has not fully engaged is not a switch fault.
  4. Check the wiring under the vehicle for chafe and impact damage.
  5. Check the transfer case fluid level while you are underneath.
