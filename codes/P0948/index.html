P0948

Hydraulic Pump Relay Circuit High

Severity
High
Driving
Drive only to a garage
System
Powertrain
Group
Transmission and control modules

The hydraulic pump relay circuit is reading high - typically shorted to voltage, or the relay not releasing. The module cannot control the pump, so the transmission will either lose hydraulic pressure or keep the pump running when it should be off.

Possible causes

  • CommonWiring shorted to voltage, or the relay not releasing between the module and the relay
  • CommonFailed relay
  • CommonCorroded relay socket
  • PossibleWater ingress into the fuse box
  • RareFailed control module driver

What you might notice

  • Pump not running, or running continuously
  • Transmission will not change gear
  • Battery flat after standing
  • Check engine light on

What to check, in order

  1. Check whether the high reading persists with the relay removed. That separates the relay from the wiring.
  2. Swap the relay with an identical one and see whether the fault follows.
  3. Inspect the socket for corrosion and heat damage.
  4. Check the fuse box for water.
  5. A pump running continuously will wear out and will flatten the battery. Do not leave the car standing with this fault.
