P0749

Pressure Control Solenoid "A" Intermittent

Severity
High
Driving
Drive only to a garage
System
Powertrain
Group
Transmission

The circuit for pressure control solenoid "A" is faulting intermittently. The transmission may drive normally for long periods and then shift harshly or drop into limp mode without obvious cause, recovering after a restart. Intermittent electrical faults here are usually connections rather than components.

Possible causes

  • CommonLoose or corroded pin at the transmission case connector
  • CommonFluid wicking into the connector
  • CommonInternal transmission harness beginning to fail
  • PossibleSolenoid dropping out when hot
  • PossibleChafed wiring making intermittent contact

What you might notice

  • Occasional harsh shift or limp mode that clears after a restart
  • Symptoms appearing only when the transmission is hot
  • Check engine light coming and going
  • Shift quality varying from journey to journey

What to check, in order

  1. Note whether it only happens hot. A fault that appears after twenty minutes of driving and clears overnight points strongly at the internal harness or the solenoid itself.
  2. Pull the case connector and inspect for fluid and corrosion.
  3. Wiggle-test the external harness with the engine running and the live data screen showing solenoid status.
  4. Check the fluid level and condition.
  5. Record the freeze frame data if the tool supports it. Knowing the fluid temperature when the fault set is often what identifies a heat-related failure.
