P0381

Glow Plug/Heater Indicator Circuit

Severity
Low
Driving
No driving restriction
System
Powertrain
Group
Ignition system or misfire

A fault in the glow plug indicator circuit - the lamp that tells the driver the glow plugs are heating and it is time to start. The glow plugs themselves may be working perfectly; what has failed is the indication.

Possible causes

  • CommonBlown indicator bulb
  • CommonWiring or connector fault to the instrument cluster
  • PossibleFailed instrument cluster lamp driver
  • PossibleFailed glow plug control module output

What you might notice

  • Glow plug lamp not lighting
  • Glow plug lamp permanently on
  • Check engine light on
  • Engine starting normally

What to check, in order

  1. Check whether the lamp lights during the ignition-on bulb check.
  2. Confirm the engine still starts normally from cold, which tells you the glow plugs themselves are working.
  3. Check the wiring between the glow plug module and the cluster.
  4. Treat this as an indication fault - but do not ignore it, because it means a genuine glow plug failure would go unannounced.
