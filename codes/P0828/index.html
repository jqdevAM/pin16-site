P0828

Up and Down Shift Switch Circuit High

Severity
Low
Driving
No driving restriction
System
Powertrain
Group
Transmission

The up-and-down shift switch circuit is stuck high - typically shorted to voltage or open circuit, or a switch that never closes. The module either sees a shift being requested constantly, or never sees one at all. On some vehicles a stuck-closed switch causes the transmission to keep trying to shift, which is more noticeable than simply losing manual mode.

Possible causes

  • CommonSwitch stuck or failed
  • CommonWiring shorted to voltage or open circuit, or a switch that never closes in the column or console
  • PossibleDamaged clock spring
  • PossibleDrink or dirt in a console-mounted switch
  • PossibleCorroded connector

What you might notice

  • Transmission repeatedly trying to shift on its own
  • Manual mode unavailable
  • Paddle or lever unresponsive
  • Check engine light on

What to check, in order

  1. Watch the switch state on the live data screen without touching anything. A reading that already shows the switch operated identifies a stuck switch immediately.
  2. Unplug the switch and see whether the high reading persists at the harness.
  3. Inspect a console switch for spilled drink, which is the most common cause of a stuck console switch.
  4. Check the clock spring on steering-wheel-mounted switches.
  5. Confirm automatic shifting is unaffected before deciding how urgent this is.
