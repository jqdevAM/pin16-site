P0640

Intake Air Heater Control Circuit

Severity
Low
Driving
Drivable — book it in
System
Powertrain
Group
Computer output circuits and control module

A fault in the intake air heater control circuit. On a diesel, a heater element warms the incoming air so the engine starts and runs cleanly when cold. A fault here goes unnoticed in warm weather and becomes obvious on the first cold morning.

Possible causes

  • CommonFailed relay
  • CommonCorroded relay socket or fuse box connections
  • CommonWiring fault between the module and the relay
  • CommonWater ingress into the fuse box
  • PossibleBlown fuse
  • RareFailed control module driver
  • CommonFailed intake air heater element
  • CommonCorroded high-current connections at the heater

What you might notice

  • Hard starting when cold
  • White smoke on a cold start
  • Rough running for the first minute
  • No symptom at all in warm weather

What to check, in order

  1. Test on a genuinely cold engine - in warm weather a diesel will usually start acceptably without it.
  2. Check the fuse or fusible link and the relay. These circuits carry very high current and their connections suffer for it.
  3. Inspect the heater terminals for corrosion and heat damage.
  4. Measure the heater element resistance against specification.
