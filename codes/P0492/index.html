P0492

Secondary Air Injection System Insufficient Flow (Bank 2)

Severity
Low
Driving
Drivable — book it in
System
Powertrain
Group
Auxiliary emission controls

Not enough secondary air is reaching the exhaust on bank 2. Because the module names one bank rather than the whole system, this points at something specific to that side - a blocked passage, a stuck valve or a failed check valve on bank 2 - rather than at the pump, which usually feeds both.

Possible causes

  • CommonCarbon blocking the air passage into the bank 2 exhaust manifold
  • CommonCheck valve on bank 2 failed or blocked
  • CommonSwitching valve for bank 2 stuck closed
  • PossibleSplit or disconnected hose on bank 2
  • PossibleWeak air pump - though this usually affects both banks
  • PossibleFaulty flow sensor on bank 2

What you might notice

  • Check engine light on
  • Failed emissions test
  • Rough running for the first minute from cold
  • Often no symptom the driver notices

What to check, in order

  1. Test from a genuinely cold start.
  2. Note that only one bank has faulted. That rules out the pump as a sole cause and directs everything at the bank 2 plumbing.
  3. Check the hose and check valve on bank 2 first.
  4. Inspect the air passage into the bank 2 exhaust manifold for carbon.
  5. Compare the two banks. Whatever is different between them is the fault.
