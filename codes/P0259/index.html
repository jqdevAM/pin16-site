P0259

Injection Pump Fuel Metering Control "B" High (Cam/Rotor/Injector)

Severity
High
Driving
Drive only to a garage
System
Powertrain
Group
Fuel and air metering - injector circuits

Injection pump fuel metering control "B" is reading high - typically shorted to voltage or with a lost earth. The module cannot meter fuel properly and will restrict the engine.

Possible causes

  • CommonFailed fuel metering control solenoid or valve
  • CommonWiring or connector fault at the injection pump
  • CommonWorn injection pump
  • CommonLow fuel pressure, a blocked filter, or air in the fuel system
  • PossibleContaminated or misfuelled diesel
  • RareFailed control module driver

What you might notice

  • Reduced power or limp mode
  • Excessive smoke
  • Hard starting or no start
  • Rough running
  • Engine cutting out
  • Check engine light on

What to check, in order

  1. Disconnect at the pump and check whether the reading persists at the harness.
  2. Check the fuel filter and when it was last changed. A blocked filter starves the pump and produces metering faults with a perfectly good pump - and it is by far the cheapest thing to eliminate.
  3. Check the connector and wiring at the injection pump.
  4. Look for air in the fuel system, and for leaks on the suction side that would let it in.
  5. Ask about recent fuelling. Contaminated or misfuelled diesel damages high-pressure pumps, and the damage spreads to the injectors.
  6. Get a professional assessment before replacing the pump - it is one of the most expensive parts on a diesel and it is often blamed for supply problems upstream of it.
