P0762

Shift Solenoid "C" Stuck On

Severity
High
Driving
Drive only to a garage
System
Powertrain
Group
Transmission
Important. A solenoid stuck on can lock the transmission up. If the car stalls when stopping, or feels like it is binding when you move off, have it recovered rather than driven.

Shift solenoid "C" is stuck on - applying when it should be released. Because the gear it controls stays engaged, the transmission can end up trying to hold two gears at once, which it will not tolerate: expect very harsh behaviour, a refusal to shift, or a stall as the car slows.

Possible causes

  • CommonDebris jamming the solenoid open
  • CommonFailed solenoid stuck in the applied position
  • CommonSticking valve in the valve body
  • PossibleDegraded, varnished fluid
  • PossibleWiring fault holding the solenoid energised

What you might notice

  • Very harsh shifts or a bang on engagement
  • Transmission will not shift out of one gear
  • Engine stalls when coming to a stop
  • Binding or dragging feeling when moving off
  • Check engine light on

What to check, in order

  1. If the engine stalls as you come to a stop, treat it as urgent - losing the engine means losing power steering and brake assistance at a junction.
  2. Check whether the solenoid circuit is being held energised electrically. A shorted wire mimics a jammed solenoid exactly and is far cheaper to repair.
  3. Check the fluid level and condition, and look for debris.
  4. Read all codes. Several stuck solenoids point at contaminated fluid affecting the whole valve body.
  5. Do not drive it far in this state. Two gears trying to engage at once puts very high loads through the transmission internals.
