P0952

Auto Shift Manual Control Circuit Low

Severity
Low
Driving
No driving restriction
System
Powertrain
Group
Transmission and control modules

The auto shift manual control circuit is stuck low - typically shorted to earth, or a switch stuck closed. The module either sees a shift request constantly or never sees one at all, so manual mode will not work correctly.

Possible causes

  • CommonSwitch stuck or failed
  • CommonWiring shorted to earth, or a switch stuck closed in the console or column
  • PossibleDamaged clock spring
  • PossibleDrink or dirt in a console control
  • PossibleCorroded connector

What you might notice

  • Transmission repeatedly attempting to change gear
  • Manual mode unavailable
  • Control unresponsive
  • Check engine light on

What to check, in order

  1. Watch the switch state without touching the control.
  2. Unplug the switch and check whether the low reading persists at the harness.
  3. Inspect a console control for spilled drink.
  4. Check the clock spring on steering-wheel-mounted controls.
  5. Confirm automatic shifting is unaffected.
