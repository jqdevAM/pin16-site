P0902

Clutch Actuator Circuit Low

Severity
High
Driving
Drive only to a garage
System
Powertrain
Group
Transmission and control modules

The clutch actuator circuit is reading low - typically shorted to earth or open circuit. The module cannot drive the clutch actuator, so the clutch cannot be operated and the car will usually refuse to move.

Possible causes

  • CommonWiring shorted to earth or open circuit between the module and the actuator
  • CommonCorroded or water-ingressed connector at the actuator
  • CommonOpen or shorted actuator winding
  • PossibleChafed harness near the bellhousing
  • RareFailed control module driver

What you might notice

  • Car will not move
  • Transmission will not engage a gear
  • Transmission warning message
  • Check engine light on

What to check, in order

  1. Disconnect the actuator and check whether the low reading persists at the harness. If it does, the fault is in the wiring rather than the actuator.
  2. Inspect the connector for corrosion and water.
  3. Measure the actuator winding resistance against specification.
  4. Follow the harness around the bellhousing looking for heat and chafe damage.
  5. Run the clutch adaptation procedure after any repair here.
