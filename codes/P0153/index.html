P0153

O2 Sensor Circuit Slow Response (Bank 2 Sensor 1)

Severity
Medium
Driving
Drivable — book it in
System
Powertrain
Group
Fuel and air metering

The upstream oxygen sensor on bank 2 is responding too slowly. A healthy upstream sensor switches between rich and lean many times a second; as the sensor ages its response slows, and the module measures this directly. This is the classic sign of a worn-out but not yet dead oxygen sensor.

Possible causes

  • CommonOxygen sensor worn out with age and mileage
  • CommonSensor contaminated by oil, coolant or fuel additives
  • PossibleExhaust leak near the sensor slowing its response
  • PossibleSilicone sealant used on the engine poisoning the sensor

What you might notice

  • Check engine light on
  • Gradually worsening fuel economy
  • Slightly rough running
  • Failed emissions test

What to check, in order

  1. Check the sensor's age and mileage. Oxygen sensors are wear items, and slow response is how they usually fail.
  2. Watch the sensor's switching rate on the live data screen with the engine warm and at a steady speed. Sluggish, lazy oscillation confirms the diagnosis.
  3. Check for exhaust leaks near the sensor before condemning it.
  4. Look for signs of oil or coolant consumption, which shorten sensor life considerably.
  5. This is one of the cases where replacing the sensor is usually the correct fix rather than a guess.
