P0A25

Generator Torque Sensor Circuit High

Severity
Medium
Driving
Drive only to a garage
System
Powertrain
Group
Hybrid propulsion

The generator torque sensor signal is stuck high - typically shorted to voltage or a lost earth. The module has no usable torque reading, so it will restrict the hybrid drive rather than operate it blind.

Possible causes

  • CommonWiring shorted to voltage or a lost earth between the sensor and the module
  • CommonCorroded or damaged connector at the transaxle
  • CommonFailed sensor
  • PossibleLoss of the reference voltage or earth
  • PossibleLow 12 volt battery voltage

What you might notice

  • Reduced power
  • Hybrid system warning message
  • Vehicle refusing electric drive
  • Engine running constantly

What to check, in order

  1. Check the 12 volt battery and its terminals.
  2. Unplug the sensor and check whether the high reading persists at the harness.
  3. Check the reference voltage and earth - a lost earth commonly drives a signal high.
  4. Inspect the connector at the transaxle for corrosion.
  5. Do not disturb anything orange while working around the transaxle.
