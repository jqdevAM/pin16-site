P0919

Gear Shift Position Control Error

Severity
High
Driving
Drive only to a garage
System
Powertrain
Group
Transmission and control modules

The module commanded the selector shaft into a gear and it did not get there. The circuits are not being blamed - this is the mechanism physically failing to engage. Because the shaft can end up between positions, the car may be left unable to select any gear at all.

Possible causes

  • CommonLow hydraulic pressure - leak, failed pump or accumulator
  • CommonFailing gear shift actuator
  • CommonCalibration lost or never performed after a repair
  • CommonWorn synchromesh or selector forks inside the gearbox
  • PossibleFaulty position sensor misreporting the shaft position
  • PossibleClutch not fully releasing, so the gear cannot be engaged

What you might notice

  • Transmission will not engage a gear
  • Car stuck in neutral or stuck in one gear
  • Grinding during a gear change
  • Gear changes taking a long time
  • Car will not move

What to check, in order

  1. Read all codes first. Hydraulic pressure or clutch codes alongside this one usually explain it.
  2. Check whether the clutch is releasing fully. A gear cannot be engaged against a clutch that is still driving, and that presents as a shift failure rather than a clutch fault.
  3. Run the calibration procedure. If the code returns immediately, the fault is mechanical or hydraulic.
  4. Check hydraulic pressure and look for leaks.
  5. Grinding during engagement points at gearbox wear rather than at the control system.
  6. A gearbox that cannot select a gear may leave you stranded. Do not rely on it for a journey until it is fixed.
