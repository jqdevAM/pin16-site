P2625

Injector Control Pressure Regulator Circuit High

Severity
High
Driving
Drive only to a garage
System
Powertrain
Group
Computer output circuits and control module

The injector control pressure regulator circuit is reading high - typically shorted to voltage or a lost earth. The module cannot control injection pressure and will restrict the engine.

Possible causes

  • CommonFailed injector control pressure regulator
  • CommonCorroded or oil-contaminated connector
  • CommonWiring damage in a hot part of the engine bay
  • CommonLow engine oil level or oil of the wrong grade
  • PossibleDebris in the oil jamming the regulator
  • RareFailed control module driver

What you might notice

  • Hard starting or no start
  • Rough running and misfire
  • Loss of power
  • White smoke on start-up
  • Check engine light on

What to check, in order

  1. Disconnect the regulator and check whether the high reading persists at the harness.
  2. Check the engine oil level, condition and grade first. These systems fire the injectors using engine oil at very high pressure, so oil faults cause more problems here than wiring does.
  3. Inspect the connector for oil contamination - a leaking seal fills its own plug.
  4. Measure the regulator winding resistance against specification.
  5. Watch commanded against actual injector control pressure on the live data screen.
  6. Read all codes - pressure codes alongside this one usually mean the pressure is genuinely wrong.
