P0658

Actuator Supply Voltage "A" Circuit Low

Severity
High
Driving
Drive only to a garage
System
Powertrain
Group
Computer output circuits and control module

Actuator supply voltage "A" is reading low - the supply the module provides to a group of actuators has been dragged down, usually by a short to earth or by one failed actuator loading it. Everything on that supply will be working weakly or not at all.

Possible causes

  • CommonOne actuator failed internally and is loading the supply
  • CommonSupply circuit shorted to earth
  • CommonWater in a connector bridging the supply to earth
  • PossibleHigh resistance in the supply wiring or a corroded connector
  • PossibleWeak battery or charging fault dragging the whole system down
  • PossibleFailed control module supply driver

What you might notice

  • Several actuator codes at once
  • Limp mode or reduced power
  • Actuators operating weakly or intermittently
  • Check engine light on

What to check, in order

  1. Check battery and charging voltage first. A weak supply to the whole vehicle shows up here before it shows up anywhere else.
  2. Disconnect actuators on supply "A" one at a time while watching the supply voltage.
  3. Look for water in connectors bridging the supply to earth.
  4. Perform a voltage drop test on the supply circuit to find high resistance.
  5. Fix the supply before diagnosing the individual actuator codes stored alongside.
