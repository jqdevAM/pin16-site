P0510

Closed Throttle Position Switch

Severity
Medium
Driving
Drivable — book it in
System
Powertrain
Group
Vehicle speed control, idle control and auxiliary inputs

A fault in the closed throttle position switch - the switch that tells the module the accelerator is fully released. Without it the module cannot be certain when to go to idle control, so idle speed, overrun fuel cut and cruise control cancellation can all behave oddly.

Possible causes

  • CommonFailed or misadjusted closed throttle switch
  • CommonCarbon build-up preventing the throttle closing fully
  • CommonCorroded connector or damaged wiring
  • PossibleThrottle cable or linkage out of adjustment
  • RareFailed control module input

What you might notice

  • Idle too high
  • Engine not returning to idle promptly when you lift off
  • Poor fuel economy on the overrun
  • Cruise control not cancelling as expected
  • Check engine light on

What to check, in order

  1. Watch the switch state on the live data screen while pressing and releasing the accelerator.
  2. Check the throttle actually closes fully - carbon around the throttle plate stops it reaching the stop, and the switch is then telling the truth.
  3. Check the switch adjustment before condemning it.
  4. Inspect the connector and wiring at the throttle body.
  5. Check the throttle cable adjustment on a cable-operated throttle.
