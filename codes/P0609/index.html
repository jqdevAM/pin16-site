P0609

Control Module VSS Output "B"

Severity
Medium
Driving
Drivable — book it in
System
Powertrain
Group
Computer output circuits and control module

A fault in vehicle speed signal output "B" from the control module. The module sends road speed to other systems - the instrument cluster, cruise control, the radio's volume compensation, sometimes the power steering. A fault here does not affect the engine, but whatever depends on that signal will lose it.

Possible causes

  • CommonWiring or connector fault on the output circuit
  • CommonCorroded connector at either end
  • PossibleFault in the module receiving the signal, loading the circuit
  • PossibleChafed harness shorting against the body or engine
  • PossibleAftermarket equipment spliced into the circuit
  • RareFailed control module output driver

What you might notice

  • Speedometer dead or reading wrongly
  • Cruise control unavailable
  • Speed-dependent features not working
  • Check engine light on
  • Often no effect on how the car drives

What to check, in order

  1. Establish what actually uses this signal on the vehicle, then check whether it works.
  2. Inspect the connectors at both ends of the circuit.
  3. Check the wiring for chafe damage.
  4. Ask whether aftermarket equipment - a tracker, a speed limiter, an aftermarket stereo - has been spliced into this wire.
