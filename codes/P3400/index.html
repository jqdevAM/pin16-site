P3400

Cylinder Deactivation System (Bank 1)

Severity
Medium
Driving
Drivable — book it in
System
Powertrain
Group
Auxiliary emission controls
Important. If this system is misbehaving, treat the engine oil as part of the diagnosis rather than as maintenance you will get to later. Most expensive failures here start with oil that was left in too long.

A general fault in the cylinder deactivation system on bank 1. This system shuts down some of the cylinders at light load - cruising on a level road, for instance - by holding their valves closed so they neither draw in air nor burn fuel. It saves fuel and is meant to be almost imperceptible. Because it is actuated by engine oil pressure through solenoids and special lifters, its health depends far more on oil condition than on electronics.

Possible causes

  • CommonLow, old or wrong-viscosity engine oil
  • CommonFailed or collapsed deactivation lifter
  • CommonFailed control solenoid or oil control valve
  • CommonBlocked oil passages in the lifter valley from neglected servicing
  • PossibleWiring or connector fault at the solenoids
  • PossibleLow oil pressure from a worn pump or a clogged screen

What you might notice

  • Misfire or shudder as the system switches in and out
  • Ticking or tapping from the top of the engine
  • Worse fuel economy
  • Reduced power
  • Check engine light on

What to check, in order

  1. Check the oil level and condition first, and check what viscosity was last used. On these engines the correct oil specification is not a recommendation - the deactivation hardware is hydraulically actuated and will not work reliably on the wrong oil.
  2. Ask when the oil was last changed and by whom. Extended or missed oil changes are the single biggest cause of failures in this system.
  3. Listen for a tick from the top of the engine, which points at a lifter rather than a solenoid.
  4. Read all codes. Misfire codes on specific cylinders alongside this one identify which ones are affected.
  5. Check the oil pressure against specification if the noise or misfire is present at idle.
