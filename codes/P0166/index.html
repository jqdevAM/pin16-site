P0166

O2 Sensor Circuit No Activity Detected (Bank 2 Sensor 3)

Severity
Low
Driving
Drivable — book it in

No activity is being seen from oxygen sensor 3 on bank 2, downstream of the converter. The signal is flat rather than incorrect, which points at an open circuit, a dead sensor, or a failed heater keeping the sensor below operating temperature.

Possible causes

  • CommonOpen circuit - broken wire or unplugged connector
  • CommonFailed sensor heater
  • CommonFailed oxygen sensor
  • PossibleBlown fuse on the heater circuit
  • PossibleCorrosion in the connector from road salt

What you might notice

  • Check engine light on
  • No drivability change
  • Failed emissions test

What to check, in order

  1. Check the connector and the heater circuit fuse first.
  2. Inspect the wiring under the car - these leads are exposed to salt and stone damage.
  3. Look for a heater code stored alongside this one, which would identify the cause.
  4. Replace the sensor once wiring and fuse are eliminated.
