P0763

Shift Solenoid "C" Electrical

Severity
High
Driving
Drive only to a garage
System
Powertrain
Group
Transmission

An electrical fault in the circuit for shift solenoid "C" - an open winding, a short to earth or voltage, or resistance outside specification. The module cannot drive the solenoid, so it will drop into a failsafe and hold one gear.

Possible causes

  • CommonOpen or shorted solenoid winding
  • CommonCorroded transmission case connector, or fluid wicking into it
  • CommonInternal transmission harness failure
  • PossibleWiring damage between the module and the transmission
  • RareFailed transmission control module driver

What you might notice

  • Transmission stuck in one gear
  • No shifting
  • Harsh engagement
  • Several solenoid codes stored at once
  • Check engine light on

What to check, in order

  1. Read all transmission codes first. Several solenoid electrical codes together point at the case connector or the internal harness, not at several failed solenoids.
  2. Pull the transmission case connector and look for fluid inside it. Fluid tracking up the harness corrodes the pins and can eventually reach and destroy the control module.
  3. Measure the solenoid resistance at the case connector against specification.
  4. Check the harness between the module and the transmission for chafe damage.
  5. Check the fluid level and condition while the vehicle is raised.
  6. Do not order a control module until the connector, harness and solenoid have all been measured.
