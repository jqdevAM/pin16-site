P0100

Mass or Volume Air Flow Circuit

Severity
Medium
Driving
Drivable — book it in
System
Powertrain
Group
Fuel and air metering

A fault in the mass airflow sensor circuit. This sensor measures how much air is entering the engine, and the module uses that figure as the basis for how much fuel to inject. When it is unreliable, fuelling is unreliable, so faults here tend to affect how the car drives rather than just emissions.

Possible causes

  • CommonMass airflow sensor element dirty or contaminated
  • CommonDamaged wiring or connector at the sensor
  • CommonAir leak between the sensor and the throttle body
  • PossibleFailed mass airflow sensor
  • PossibleBadly fitted or aftermarket air filter letting dust through

What you might notice

  • Hesitation or stumble under acceleration
  • Rough idle
  • Worse fuel economy
  • Check engine light on
  • Occasionally stalling

What to check, in order

  1. Inspect the intake ducting between the sensor and the throttle body for splits and loose clamps. Air entering after the sensor is never measured, which produces the same symptoms as a faulty sensor.
  2. Look at the sensor element. If it is visibly dirty, clean it with dedicated mass airflow sensor cleaner only - the hot-wire element is delicate and other solvents damage it.
  3. Check the air filter. A poorly fitting or oiled aftermarket filter is a common way sensors get contaminated.
  4. Inspect the connector and wiring for damage.
  5. Compare the airflow reading on the live data screen against expected values at idle before replacing the sensor.
