P2438

Secondary Air Injection System Air Flow/Pressure Sensor Circuit High (Bank 2)

Severity
Low
Driving
Drivable — book it in
System
Powertrain
Group
Auxiliary emission controls

The secondary air flow or pressure sensor signal on bank 2 is stuck high - typically shorted to voltage or with a lost earth, though a genuinely blocked passage can also raise pressure beyond what the module expects.

Possible causes

  • CommonWiring shorted to voltage, or a lost sensor earth
  • CommonCorroded connector bridging pins
  • CommonFailed sensor
  • CommonBlocked passage raising pressure against the pump
  • PossibleChafed harness against a supply wire

What you might notice

  • Check engine light on
  • Failed emissions test
  • Pump sounding strained on a cold start
  • Usually no symptom the driver notices

What to check, in order

  1. Listen to the pump on a cold start. A pump straining against a blockage sounds different from one running freely, and that supports a genuine pressure reading rather than an electrical fault.
  2. Check the sensor earth before assuming a shorted signal wire.
  3. Unplug the sensor and check whether the high reading persists at the harness.
  4. Inspect the air passages into the exhaust manifold for carbon.
  5. Inspect the connector for corrosion bridging pins.
