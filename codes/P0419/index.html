P0419

Secondary Air Injection System Control "B" Circuit

Severity
Low
Driving
Drivable — book it in
System
Powertrain
Group
Auxiliary emission controls

A fault in secondary air injection control circuit "B" - typically the circuit that switches the air pump itself, through its relay. Without it the pump will not run, so the system does nothing on a cold start.

Possible causes

  • CommonFailed air pump relay
  • CommonCorroded relay socket or fuse box connections
  • CommonWiring fault between the module and the relay
  • CommonBlown fuse on the pump circuit
  • PossibleSeized pump drawing excessive current and taking out the circuit
  • RareFailed control module driver

What you might notice

  • No pump noise on a cold start
  • Check engine light on
  • Failed emissions test
  • Blown fuse, sometimes repeatedly
  • Usually no symptom the driver notices

What to check, in order

  1. Check the fuse and swap the relay with an identical one elsewhere in the box. Both cost nothing and account for a large share of these faults.
  2. Listen on a cold start to confirm whether the pump runs at all.
  3. Inspect the relay socket for corrosion and heat damage.
  4. If a fuse keeps blowing, suspect a seized pump. A pump that has ingested water draws far more current than the circuit is designed for.
  5. Check the wiring between the module and the relay.
