P0A20

Motor Torque Sensor Circuit High

Severity
Medium
Driving
Drive only to a garage
System
Powertrain
Group
Hybrid propulsion
Important. High-voltage hazard nearby. The transaxle on a hybrid contains high-voltage components and orange cabling. Reading codes and checking connectors on low-voltage circuits is safe; do not disturb anything orange.

The drive motor torque sensor signal is stuck high - typically a short to voltage or a lost earth. The module has no usable torque reading from the motor, so it will restrict the drive rather than operate it blind.

Possible causes

  • CommonWiring a short to voltage or a lost earth between the sensor and the module
  • CommonCorroded or damaged connector at the transaxle
  • CommonFailed sensor
  • CommonLoss of the reference voltage or earth
  • PossibleLow 12 volt battery voltage

What you might notice

  • Reduced power
  • Hybrid or electric drive system warning message
  • Vehicle refusing electric drive
  • Engine running constantly on a hybrid

What to check, in order

  1. Check the 12 volt battery first - it is the most common cause of scattered hybrid system codes.
  2. Unplug the sensor and check whether the high reading persists at the harness.
  3. Check the reference voltage and earth - a lost earth commonly drives a signal high.
  4. Inspect the connector at the transaxle for corrosion.
  5. Do not disturb anything orange while working around the transaxle.
