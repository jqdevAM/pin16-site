P0895

Shift Time Too Short

Severity
Medium
Driving
Drivable — book it in
System
Powertrain
Group
Transmission

Shifts are completing faster than the module expects. A shift that happens too quickly means the clutch is being clamped abruptly rather than being brought in smoothly, which is felt as a bang and puts a shock load through the driveline. Usually pressure is too high, or the module is compensating for something.

Possible causes

  • CommonLine pressure too high
  • CommonPressure control solenoid stuck or failed
  • CommonModule defaulting to maximum pressure because of another stored fault
  • CommonAdaptation values wrong after a repair, or never reset
  • PossibleSticking valve in the valve body
  • PossibleWrong fluid specification

What you might notice

  • Harsh, banging shifts
  • A jolt through the car on every gear change
  • Harsh engagement into drive or reverse
  • Check engine light on

What to check, in order

  1. Read all codes first. A module that has raised pressure to protect itself does so because of another fault, so this code is often a consequence rather than a cause.
  2. Check whether an adaptation reset is specified. Adaptation values left over from a previous state are a common cause of harsh shifts after a repair.
  3. Check the fluid level, condition and specification.
  4. Watch commanded against actual line pressure on the live data screen if available.
  5. Drive gently until it is fixed - harsh shifts load mountings and driveshafts.
