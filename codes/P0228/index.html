P0228

Throttle/Pedal Position Sensor/Switch "C" Circuit High

Severity
High
Driving
Drive only to a garage
System
Powertrain
Group
Fuel and air metering - injector circuits

Throttle or pedal position sensor "C" is reporting a high signal - outside its valid range rather than merely inaccurate. A working sensor cannot physically produce a value outside the range, so this points at wiring or a failed sensor.

Possible causes

  • CommonShort or open circuit in the sensor wiring
  • CommonFailed sensor
  • PossibleDisconnected or corroded connector
  • PossibleLoss of the sensor's reference voltage or ground

What you might notice

  • Reduced power mode, often severe
  • Engine will not rev, or will not start
  • Warning light on
  • Unstable or very high idle

What to check, in order

  1. Do not drive far with this fault. Reduced power can engage abruptly.
  2. Check the connector is seated and free of corrosion and water.
  3. Check the sensor's reference voltage and ground at the connector. Losing either produces a stuck-at-limit reading on a healthy sensor.
  4. Check continuity of the signal wire back to the module - an open circuit and a short give the two opposite versions of this code.
  5. Replace the sensor only once reference, ground and wiring are confirmed good.
