P0889

TCM Power Relay Sense Circuit Range/Performance

Severity
High
Driving
Drive only to a garage
System
Powertrain
Group
Transmission

The power relay sense circuit is reporting something implausible - the relay's actual state does not match what the module commanded, or the timing is wrong. Usually the relay contacts are sticking or slow, which is a mechanical failure inside the relay rather than a wiring problem.

Possible causes

  • CommonRelay contacts sticking or welded
  • CommonWorn relay slow to switch
  • CommonCorroded relay socket adding resistance
  • PossibleHigh resistance in the sense wiring
  • RareFailed module input

What you might notice

  • Intermittent limp mode
  • Transmission occasionally dead at start-up
  • Battery draining after standing
  • Check engine light on

What to check, in order

  1. Swap the relay first. Sticking contacts are the most common cause and swapping is the fastest way to confirm it.
  2. Inspect the socket for corrosion and heat damage - a discoloured socket means the relay has been running hot.
  3. Check for high resistance on the sense wire.
  4. Check the fuse box for water ingress.
  5. If the battery drains overnight, this circuit is worth investigating as the cause.
