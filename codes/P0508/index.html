P0508

Idle Air Control System Circuit Low

Severity
Medium
Driving
Drivable — book it in
System
Powertrain
Group
Vehicle speed control, idle control and auxiliary inputs

The idle air control circuit is reading low - outside its valid electrical range. Unlike the idle-speed codes, this one points at the wiring or the valve itself rather than at an air leak or a restriction.

Possible causes

  • CommonOpen or shorted wiring to the idle air control valve
  • CommonFailed idle air control valve
  • PossibleCorroded or disconnected connector
  • RareModule driver fault

What you might notice

  • Idle stuck high or low
  • Stalling
  • Warning light on

What to check, in order

  1. Check the connector at the valve is seated and free of corrosion.
  2. Check continuity of the wiring back to the module - an open circuit and a short give the two versions of this code.
  3. Measure the valve's resistance against specification.
  4. Replace the valve once the wiring is confirmed good.
