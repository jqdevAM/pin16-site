P0944

Hydraulic Pressure Unit Loss of Pressure

Severity
High
Driving
Drive only to a garage
System
Powertrain
Group
Transmission and control modules

The hydraulic pressure unit has lost pressure. Without pressure the clutch and selector actuators cannot move, so the transmission cannot change gear and the car will usually not move at all. The most common cause is the simplest: fluid has leaked out.

Possible causes

  • CommonExternal hydraulic leak
  • CommonLow fluid level
  • CommonFailed hydraulic pump
  • CommonFailed accumulator
  • PossiblePump relay or supply fault, so the pump never runs
  • PossibleBlocked filter starving the pump

What you might notice

  • Car will not move
  • Transmission will not select a gear
  • Pump not running, or running without building pressure
  • Transmission warning message
  • Fluid puddle under the car

What to check, in order

  1. Look underneath for a leak and check the fluid level. This finds the cause most of the time and costs nothing.
  2. Listen for the pump when the ignition is switched on. Silence points at the relay, the fuse or the pump itself.
  3. Check the pump relay and fuse.
  4. Watch the pressure reading while the pump runs - a pump that runs without building pressure is worn or starved.
  5. Do not keep switching the ignition on and off to try to build pressure. If the pump is running dry it will destroy itself.
