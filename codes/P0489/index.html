P0489

Exhaust Gas Recirculation Control Circuit Low

Severity
Medium
Driving
Drivable — book it in
System
Powertrain
Group
Auxiliary emission controls

The voltage on the EGR control circuit is low - the circuit is shorted to earth or open. The module drives the EGR valve and monitors what the circuit does in response; a reading pinned low means the circuit is not behaving like a working actuator, so the module has disabled EGR control and set this code.

Possible causes

  • CommonWiring fault in the EGR control circuit - an earthed or broken wire
  • CommonCorroded or water-damaged connector at the EGR valve
  • PossibleFailed EGR solenoid or valve motor
  • PossibleChafed harness shorting against the engine or bodywork
  • RareFaulty control module output driver

What you might notice

  • Check engine light on
  • EGR disabled
  • Failed emissions test
  • Usually no symptom the driver notices

What to check, in order

  1. Unplug the EGR valve and measure the circuit at the harness side. If the low reading persists with the valve disconnected, the fault is in the wiring, not the valve.
  2. Inspect the connector for corrosion, spread pins and melted plastic.
  3. Measure the actuator winding resistance against specification.
  4. Follow the harness to the valve looking for chafe points near hot or moving parts.
  5. Clear the code and see whether it returns immediately or only after driving. An immediate return points at a hard short; an intermittent return points at chafed insulation or a loose pin.
