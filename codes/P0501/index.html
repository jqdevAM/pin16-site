P0501

Vehicle Speed Sensor "A" Range/Performance

Severity
Medium
Driving
Drivable — book it in
System
Powertrain
Group
Vehicle speed control, idle control and auxiliary inputs

The vehicle speed signal from sensor "A" is present but implausible - it does not agree with what the module expects from engine speed, gear and load. The sensor is producing something, just not something believable.

Possible causes

  • CommonFailing vehicle speed sensor producing a weak or noisy signal
  • CommonDamaged reluctor ring or sensor gap out of specification
  • PossibleWiring interference or a poor ground
  • PossibleIncorrect tyre size or final drive ratio after modification
  • PossibleTransmission fault causing a genuine speed mismatch

What you might notice

  • Speedometer reading inaccurately
  • Odd gear change behaviour
  • Cruise control cutting out
  • Warning light on

What to check, in order

  1. Compare the indicated speed against a known-good reference, such as a phone GPS, at a steady speed.
  2. Ask whether wheels, tyres or the final drive have been changed - a significant size change makes a healthy sensor read wrongly.
  3. Inspect the sensor tip and the ring it reads for damage or debris.
  4. Check the sensor's ground and look for chafed wiring picking up interference.
