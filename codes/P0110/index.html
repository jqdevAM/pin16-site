P0110

Intake Air Temperature Sensor 1 Circuit

Severity
Low
Driving
Drivable — book it in
System
Powertrain
Group
Fuel and air metering

A fault in the intake air temperature sensor circuit. The module uses air temperature to fine-tune fuelling and ignition timing, since cold air is denser than warm air. A fault here has a smaller effect than an airflow fault, but it still degrades fuelling accuracy.

Possible causes

  • CommonDamaged wiring or connector at the sensor
  • CommonFailed temperature sensor
  • PossibleSensor contaminated where it is built into the airflow sensor

What you might notice

  • Check engine light on
  • Slightly worse fuel economy
  • Occasionally harder starting when cold
  • Often no noticeable symptom

What to check, in order

  1. Check the intake air temperature reading on the live data screen with the engine cold. It should be close to ambient temperature - an obviously implausible value confirms the fault.
  2. Inspect the connector and wiring.
  3. On many vehicles this sensor is built into the mass airflow sensor, so check whether it can be replaced separately.
