P0102

Mass or Volume Air Flow Circuit Low Input

Severity
Medium
Driving
Drivable — book it in
System
Powertrain
Group
Fuel and air metering

The mass airflow sensor signal is too low. The module has a valid range for this sensor and the reading sits outside it, which points at a wiring problem, a leak, or a failed sensor rather than a subtle calibration drift.

Possible causes

  • CommonDamaged wiring or poor connection at the sensor
  • CommonDirty or contaminated sensor element
  • CommonAir leak in the intake ducting
  • PossibleFailed mass airflow sensor
  • PossibleBlocked air filter

What you might notice

  • Poor acceleration
  • Rough idle or stalling
  • Check engine light on
  • Noticeably worse fuel economy

What to check, in order

  1. Inspect the connector and wiring at the sensor, including for chafing where the harness passes the engine.
  2. Check the intake ducting for splits, particularly on the underside where cracks are easy to miss.
  3. Clean the sensor element with the correct cleaner.
  4. Check the air filter.
  5. Compare live airflow readings against specification before replacing the sensor.
