P0125

Insufficient Coolant Temperature for Closed Loop Fuel Control

Severity
Medium
Driving
Drivable — book it in
System
Powertrain
Group
Fuel and air metering

The engine is not reaching normal operating temperature quickly enough for the module to switch to closed loop fuel control. Until it is warm the module runs a deliberately rich cold-start mixture and ignores the oxygen sensors, so an engine that never warms up properly runs rich permanently. By far the most common cause is a thermostat stuck open, and that is a cheap part.

Possible causes

  • CommonThermostat stuck open, so the coolant never reaches operating temperature
  • CommonCoolant temperature sensor reading lower than the truth
  • PossibleThermostat missing, or one of the wrong temperature rating fitted
  • PossibleLow coolant level
  • PossibleWiring or connector fault at the coolant temperature sensor
  • PossibleGenuinely extreme cold combined with a lot of short journeys

What you might notice

  • Poor fuel economy - often noticeably worse than usual
  • Weak or lukewarm cabin heating
  • Temperature gauge slow to rise, or never reaching the middle
  • Failed emissions test
  • Check engine light on

What to check, in order

  1. Notice whether the cabin heater blows properly hot. Weak heat plus slow warm-up is the classic thermostat-stuck-open picture, and it costs nothing to check on the drive in.
  2. Watch the coolant temperature on live data through a warm-up. It should climb steadily and settle around the thermostat's rating; one that creeps up and stalls short of it, or falls again at motorway speed, is a stuck-open thermostat.
  3. Check the sensor before the thermostat if the gauge and the live data disagree. A sensor reading low makes a healthy engine look cold to the module.
  4. Check the coolant level. Low coolant can leave the sensor reading air rather than liquid.
  5. Do not ignore this because the car drives normally. It runs rich the whole time, which costs fuel, dilutes the oil with unburnt petrol and shortens the catalytic converter's life.
