P0881

TCM Power Input Signal Range/Performance

Severity
High
Driving
Drive only to a garage
System
Powertrain
Group
Transmission

The transmission module's supply voltage is present but outside the range it expects, or unstable. It may sag under load, spike, or sit persistently low or high. Solenoid control depends on a predictable voltage, so shift quality suffers before anything actually fails.

Possible causes

  • CommonWeak or failing battery
  • CommonFailing alternator or voltage regulator
  • CommonCorroded earth or high resistance in the supply wiring
  • CommonLoose or corroded battery terminals
  • PossibleFailing relay with worn contacts
  • PossibleHeavy aftermarket electrical load

What you might notice

  • Harsh or unpredictable shifting
  • Limp mode, often intermittently
  • Lights dimming or flickering
  • Battery warning light
  • Check engine light on

What to check, in order

  1. Measure battery voltage with the engine off and again with it running. Outside roughly 12.4 to 12.7 volts at rest, or outside the charging range running, and you have found the fault.
  2. Perform a voltage drop test on the module's supply and earth. High resistance in a corroded connection produces exactly this without anything looking wrong.
  3. Check the alternator output under load - lights and heated screen on.
  4. Clean and tighten the battery terminals and earth straps.
  5. Ask about aftermarket electrical equipment, particularly amplifiers and winches, which can drag the supply down.
