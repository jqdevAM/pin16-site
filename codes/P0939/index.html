P0939

Hydraulic Oil Temperature Sensor Circuit Low

Severity
Medium
Driving
Drivable — book it in
System
Powertrain
Group
Transmission and control modules

The hydraulic oil temperature signal is stuck low, which reads as implausibly hot on a typical sensor whose resistance falls as temperature rises. Electrically this is usually shorted to earth. The module treats the reading as unusable and drives the actuators conservatively, so gear changes may feel slow or harsh.

Possible causes

  • CommonWiring shorted to earth between the sensor and the module
  • CommonFailed temperature sensor
  • CommonCorroded or fluid-contaminated connector
  • PossibleLoss of the sensor earth
  • RareGenuinely extreme fluid temperature - rare, but check before assuming a sensor fault

What you might notice

  • Slow or harsh gear changes
  • Temperature reading implausible
  • Transmission warning message
  • Check engine light on

What to check, in order

  1. Read the temperature on a cold vehicle. A reading nowhere near ambient confirms the circuit rather than a genuine temperature.
  2. Unplug the sensor and check whether the low reading persists at the harness.
  3. Check the sensor earth before assuming a shorted signal wire.
  4. Inspect the connector for corrosion and fluid.
  5. Check the fluid level and condition.
