P0936

Hydraulic Pressure Sensor Circuit Intermittent

Severity
High
Driving
Drive only to a garage
System
Powertrain
Group
Transmission and control modules

The hydraulic pressure signal is dropping out intermittently. Gear changes work most of the time and occasionally fail. As with all intermittent circuit faults, connections are more likely than components - but a genuinely fluctuating pressure will produce the same code.

Possible causes

  • CommonLoose or corroded connector pin at the hydraulic unit
  • CommonChafed or fatigued wiring
  • CommonGenuinely fluctuating pressure from a small leak
  • PossibleSensor dropping out when hot
  • PossibleFluid contamination in the connector

What you might notice

  • Occasional failure to change gear
  • Pump cycling irregularly
  • Transmission warning message coming and going
  • Check engine light intermittent

What to check, in order

  1. Watch the pressure reading through several pump cycles. Genuine fluctuation looks different from an electrical dropout - the reading falls smoothly rather than jumping.
  2. Check the fluid level and look for a slow leak.
  3. Wiggle-test the harness at the hydraulic unit.
  4. Inspect the connector for a backed-out pin and for fluid contamination.
  5. Record the freeze frame data if the tool supports it.
