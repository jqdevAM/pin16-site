P0A07

Motor Electronics Coolant Pump Control Circuit High

Severity
High
Driving
Drive only to a garage
Important. The inverter's cooling cannot be relied on while this is stored. Keep the load light until it is fixed. High-voltage hazard nearby. The transaxle on a hybrid contains high-voltage components and orange cabling. Reading codes and checking connectors on low-voltage circuits is safe; do not disturb anything orange.

The motor electronics coolant pump control circuit is reading high - typically a short to voltage or a lost earth. The module cannot control the pump that cools the inverter, so it must assume that loop is not being cooled and will restrict power accordingly.

Possible causes

  • CommonWiring a short to voltage or a lost earth between the module and the pump
  • CommonCorroded or water-damaged connector at the pump
  • CommonFailed pump with a shorted or open winding
  • PossibleBlown fuse or failed relay
  • RareFailed driver inside the control module

What you might notice

  • Severely reduced power under sustained load
  • Hybrid or electric drive system warning message
  • Inverter or drive electronics overheating warning
  • Slower charging, or charging refused

What to check, in order

  1. Check the fuse and the pump's supply before condemning the pump.
  2. Check the 12 volt battery first - it is the most common cause of scattered hybrid system codes.
  3. Unplug the pump and check whether the high reading persists at the harness. That separates a failed pump from a wiring fault.
  4. Check the coolant level in the motor electronics reservoir, which is not the engine one.
  5. Do not disturb anything orange.
