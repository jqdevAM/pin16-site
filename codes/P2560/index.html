P2560

Engine Coolant Level Low

Severity
High
Driving
Short journeys only
System
Powertrain
Group
Vehicle speed control, idle control and auxiliary inputs
Important. Low coolant is a warning you get before the damage, not after. An engine that overheats warps cylinder heads and destroys head gaskets, and the difference between a hose and a rebuild is often whether this warning was acted on.

The engine coolant level is low. Coolant does not get used up in normal running, so if it is low it has gone somewhere - a leak, a failed hose, a water pump seal, or into the engine itself through a head gasket. Low coolant leads to overheating, and overheating is one of the most expensive things that can happen to an engine.

Possible causes

  • CommonCoolant genuinely low, from a leak or from being burnt
  • CommonFailed coolant level sensor or float
  • CommonCorroded connector at the expansion tank
  • PossibleWiring damage
  • PossibleLoss of reference voltage or earth
  • PossibleSensor coated with deposits from old or wrong-specification coolant

What you might notice

  • Coolant level warning
  • Engine running hot
  • Heater blowing cool
  • Sweet smell of coolant
  • Visible coolant loss or a puddle under the car
  • Check engine light on

What to check, in order

  1. Check the level when the engine is cold and top it up with the correct coolant specification. Never open a hot cooling system.
  2. Find where it went. Topping up without finding the leak means doing it again, and next time possibly not in time.
  3. Look underneath, around the hoses and water pump, and in the passenger footwell - a heater matrix leak shows as a damp carpet and a persistently misted screen rather than a puddle.
  4. Watch the temperature gauge closely until this is resolved, and stop if it climbs.
  5. If coolant disappears with no visible leak, have a head gasket test done. That is the expensive possibility and it is worth confirming or ruling out early.
  6. Use the coolant specification the manufacturer names. Mixing types can cause them to gel and block the radiator.
