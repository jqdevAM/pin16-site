C

Chassis

Mechanical systems outside the powertrain: braking, ABS, traction and stability control, steering, suspension and wheel speed sensing.

No open-source descriptions exist for this system, so its codes are read from their structure.

Read this code from your own car

Plug an ELM327 adapter into the OBD-II port under the dashboard. Pin16 reads every stored, pending and permanent code, explains each one like this page does — offline, with no account — and shows what the car recorded when the fault was set.
