P0833

Clutch Pedal Switch "B" Circuit

Severity
Medium
Driving
Drivable — book it in
System
Powertrain
Group
Transmission

A fault in the clutch pedal switch "B" circuit. The module uses this switch to know when the clutch pedal is pressed - to permit starting, to cancel cruise control, and to adjust fuelling during a gear change. Vehicles often have two of these switches for redundancy, so the fault may show only as a warning rather than as a change in behaviour.

Possible causes

  • CommonFailed or misadjusted clutch pedal switch "B"
  • CommonWorn or broken switch striker on the pedal
  • CommonWiring or connector fault in the footwell
  • PossibleWorn clutch pedal bushings changing the pedal travel
  • PossibleAftermarket alarm or immobiliser spliced into the circuit

What you might notice

  • Engine will not start, or starts without the clutch pressed
  • Cruise control will not engage
  • Stop-start system disabled
  • Check engine light on

What to check, in order

  1. Watch the switch "B" state on the live data screen while pressing the pedal fully.
  2. Compare it with the other clutch switch. One reading correctly and the other not immediately identifies which switch is at fault.
  3. Check the switch adjustment and the striker on the pedal. Adjustment is the most common fix here.
  4. Inspect the connector in the footwell, where feet catch it.
  5. Check the pedal bushings for wear, which changes how far the pedal travels.
