P0920

Gear Shift Forward Actuator Circuit/Open

Severity
High
Driving
Drive only to a garage
System
Powertrain
Group
Transmission and control modules

An open circuit or general electrical fault in the gear shift forward actuator circuit - the actuator that pushes the selector shaft in the forward direction to engage a gear. Without it the gears on that side of the gate cannot be engaged.

Possible causes

  • CommonFailed forward actuator or its solenoid
  • CommonCorroded or damaged connector at the gearbox
  • CommonOpen circuit or broken wire in the harness
  • PossibleBlown fuse on the actuator supply
  • PossibleWater ingress at the gearbox connector
  • RareFailed control module driver

What you might notice

  • Some gears unavailable
  • Transmission will not engage in the forward direction
  • Car stuck in neutral
  • Transmission warning message
  • Check engine light on

What to check, in order

  1. Note which gears are still available. If only the gears on one side of the gate work, that confirms one actuator rather than a shared fault.
  2. Check the fuses on the actuator supply.
  3. Inspect the connector at the gearbox for corrosion and water.
  4. Measure the actuator or solenoid winding resistance against specification.
  5. Check continuity through the harness.
  6. Run the calibration procedure after any repair.
