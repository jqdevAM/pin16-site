P0892

TCM Power Relay Sense Circuit Intermittent

Severity
High
Driving
Drive only to a garage
System
Powertrain
Group
Transmission

The power relay sense circuit is faulting intermittently. The transmission works, then briefly does not, then works again. Relay and socket problems behave exactly like this as the contacts wear, and so do corroded connections in a damp fuse box.

Possible causes

  • CommonWorn relay with intermittent contacts
  • CommonCorroded relay socket
  • CommonWater ingress into the fuse box
  • PossibleLoose or chafed sense wiring
  • PossibleWeak battery causing marginal relay operation

What you might notice

  • Occasional limp mode that clears after a restart
  • Transmission occasionally dead at start-up
  • Fault linked to wet weather
  • Check engine light coming and going

What to check, in order

  1. Note whether it happens in the wet. Water in the fuse box is a very common cause of intermittent relay faults and is easily missed.
  2. Swap the relay and see whether the fault follows it.
  3. Inspect the socket for corrosion.
  4. Check battery voltage and state of charge - a weak battery makes a marginal relay behave intermittently.
  5. Wiggle-test the fuse box connections with the engine running.
