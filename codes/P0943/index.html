P0943

Hydraulic Pressure Unit Cycling Period Too Short

Severity
High
Driving
Drive only to a garage
System
Powertrain
Group
Transmission and control modules
Important. The pump in these systems is designed to run occasionally, not continuously. Constant cycling shortens its life considerably, so this is worth fixing before the pump becomes part of the repair.

The hydraulic pump is cycling too often - it charges the accumulator, and the pressure falls again far sooner than it should. Pressure is escaping somewhere. This is one of the more useful codes in this range because it points almost directly at a leak or a failed accumulator rather than at the pump itself.

Possible causes

  • CommonExternal hydraulic leak - pipes, seals or the actuator
  • CommonFailed accumulator, unable to store pressure
  • CommonInternal leak past a worn valve or seal
  • CommonLow fluid level
  • PossibleFaulty pressure sensor causing unnecessary pump cycles
  • PossibleWorn pump unable to maintain pressure

What you might notice

  • Pump running frequently, including with the car standing still
  • Slow or failed gear changes
  • Transmission warning message
  • Fluid leak visible under the car
  • Pump noise more noticeable than usual

What to check, in order

  1. Look underneath for a leak. Frequent pump cycling with a falling fluid level is a leak until proven otherwise.
  2. Watch the pressure reading after the pump stops. Pressure that falls away quickly with the car stationary confirms it is escaping rather than being used.
  3. Suspect the accumulator if there is no external leak and the fluid level is correct - a failed accumulator loses its ability to store pressure and the pump has to make up the difference constantly.
  4. Check the fluid level and condition.
  5. Do not ignore this. A pump running constantly wears out quickly and overheats the fluid, so a cheap repair becomes an expensive one if left.
