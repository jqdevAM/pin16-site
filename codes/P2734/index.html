P2734

Pressure Control Solenoid "F" Electrical

Severity
High
Driving
Drive only to a garage
System
Powertrain
Group
Transmission

An electrical fault in the circuit for pressure control solenoid "F" - an open winding, a short, or resistance outside specification. The module cannot drive the solenoid, so it falls back to a failsafe pressure and usually holds one gear.

Possible causes

  • CommonDebris in the fluid jamming the solenoid
  • CommonFailed solenoid
  • CommonCorroded transmission case connector, or fluid wicking into it
  • CommonLow or degraded fluid
  • PossibleInternal transmission harness failure
  • PossibleSticking valve in the valve body bore

What you might notice

  • Very harsh or violent shifts
  • Slipping under load
  • Transmission stuck in one gear
  • Delayed engagement into drive or reverse
  • Check engine light on

What to check, in order

  1. Read all transmission codes together. Several solenoid codes at once point at the case connector, the internal harness or contaminated fluid - not at several failed solenoids.
  2. Pull the transmission case connector and look for fluid inside it. Fluid wicking up the harness corrodes the pins and can reach and destroy the control module itself.
  3. Check the fluid level and condition first. It is the cheapest check and it explains more transmission complaints than every electrical fault combined.
