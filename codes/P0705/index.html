P0705

Transmission Range Sensor Circuit Malfunction (PRNDL Input)

Severity
High
Driving
Drive only to a garage
System
Powertrain
Group
Transmission
Important. If the engine will start in drive or reverse, do not use the vehicle until this is repaired. The starter interlock exists to stop the car lurching forward when someone turns the key.

A fault in the transmission range sensor circuit - the sensor that tells the module which position the gear selector is in. If the module cannot tell whether you have selected park, reverse, neutral or drive, it cannot shift sensibly and it cannot let the engine start safely, so it falls back to a failsafe.

Possible causes

  • CommonFailed or misadjusted range sensor (also called the neutral safety or inhibitor switch)
  • CommonWorn or damaged selector linkage, so the sensor is not being moved to the right position
  • CommonCorroded connector at the sensor - it sits low on the transmission
  • PossibleWiring damage between the sensor and the module
  • PossibleWater or salt ingress into the sensor

What you might notice

  • Engine will not start, or starts in gears other than park and neutral
  • Gear position display wrong or blank
  • Reversing lights not working, or on in the wrong gear
  • Transmission stuck in one gear
  • Harsh engagement when selecting drive or reverse

What to check, in order

  1. Watch the selected gear on the live data screen while moving the selector through every position. A position that reads wrong, or two positions that read the same, identifies the fault immediately.
  2. Check the sensor adjustment. Many of these sensors are adjustable and their alignment drifts - adjustment is often the whole repair.
  3. Check the selector linkage for wear and free play. The sensor can be perfect while the linkage fails to move it far enough.
  4. Inspect the connector for corrosion. It sits low on the transmission, in the path of road spray and salt.
  5. Treat an engine that starts in gear as a safety fault. It means the interlock is not working, and the car can move as it starts.
