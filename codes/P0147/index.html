P0147

O2 Sensor Heater Circuit (Bank 1 Sensor 3)

Severity
Low
Driving
Drivable — book it in
System
Powertrain
Group
Fuel and air metering

The heater circuit in oxygen sensor 3 on bank 1, downstream of the converter, has a fault. The heater brings the sensor up to working temperature after start-up. Without it the sensor cannot monitor the converter properly, though the engine will still run normally since this sensor does not control fuelling.

Possible causes

  • CommonFailed heater element inside the sensor
  • CommonBlown fuse on the heater circuit
  • PossibleDamaged wiring or corroded connector under the car

What you might notice

  • Check engine light on
  • No drivability change
  • Failed emissions test
  • Converter monitoring never completes

What to check, in order

  1. Check the heater circuit fuse first.
  2. Measure heater resistance at the connector against specification.
  3. Inspect the under-car wiring for salt corrosion and stone damage.
  4. If several heater codes are stored together, check the shared fuse before replacing sensors.
