P0862

Gear Shift Module Communication Circuit High

Severity
High
Driving
Drive only to a garage
System
Powertrain
Group
Transmission

The communication circuit to the gear shift module is stuck high - typically shorted to voltage or a lost earth. Nothing can be exchanged with the module, so the transmission falls back to a failsafe and usually holds one gear.

Possible causes

  • CommonBus wiring shorted to voltage or a lost earth
  • CommonCorroded or water-ingressed connector at the module
  • CommonLow battery voltage
  • PossibleChafed harness shorting against the body or engine
  • PossibleFailed module dragging the circuit high
  • PossibleAftermarket wiring spliced into the bus

What you might notice

  • Transmission stuck in one gear
  • Scan tool cannot communicate with the module
  • Several warning lights at once
  • Check engine light on

What to check, in order

  1. Check battery voltage and state of charge first.
  2. Disconnect the gear shift module and see whether the high reading clears. If it does, the module is dragging the circuit and is the fault.
  3. Inspect the connector for corrosion and water.
  4. Follow the harness looking for chafe damage where it passes near hot or moving parts.
  5. Check the fuses and earths for the module.
  6. Ask whether aftermarket electrical work has been spliced into the bus.
