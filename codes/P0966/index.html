P0966

Pressure Control Solenoid "B" Control Circuit Low

Severity
High
Driving
Drive only to a garage
System
Powertrain
Group
Transmission and control modules

The control circuit for pressure control solenoid "B" is reading low - typically shorted to earth or open circuit. The module cannot drive the solenoid, so line pressure cannot be controlled and the transmission falls back to a failsafe, usually holding one gear with harsh engagement.

Possible causes

  • CommonWiring shorted to earth or open circuit between the module and the solenoid
  • CommonCorroded transmission case connector, or fluid wicking into it
  • CommonShorted or open solenoid winding
  • CommonInternal transmission harness failure
  • PossibleChafed harness between the module and the transmission
  • RareFailed control module driver

What you might notice

  • Transmission stuck in one gear
  • Harsh engagement
  • No shifting
  • Several solenoid codes stored together
  • Check engine light on

What to check, in order

  1. Read all codes first - several solenoid codes at once point at a shared cause.
  2. Disconnect at the case connector and check whether the low reading persists at the harness side. That establishes whether the fault is inside or outside the transmission.
  3. Pull the case connector and look for fluid ingress and corroded pins.
  4. Measure the solenoid winding resistance against specification.
  5. Check the fluid level and condition.
