P2223

NOx Sensor Heater Sense Circuit Low Input (Bank 2)

Severity
Medium
Driving
Drive only to a garage
System
Powertrain
Group
Fuel and air metering - injector circuits
Important. A NOx sensor cannot measure anything until its heater brings it up to working temperature - several hundred degrees. So a heater fault disables the sensor completely even though the sensing element may be perfect.

The NOx sensor heater sense circuit on bank 2 is reading low - typically shorted to earth or an open circuit.

Possible causes

  • CommonFailed heater element inside the NOx sensor
  • CommonCorroded connector at the sensor
  • CommonWiring damaged by exhaust heat
  • PossibleBlown fuse on the heater circuit
  • PossibleWater reaching a hot sensor and cracking the element
  • RareFailed control module driver

What you might notice

  • Check engine light on
  • Reduced power or limp mode
  • Emissions system warning
  • Restart countdown on some vehicles
  • Failed emissions test
  • Often no symptom the driver notices at first

What to check, in order

  1. Check the fuse for the heater circuit before anything else.
  2. Inspect the connector for corrosion and heat damage.
  3. Measure the heater element resistance against specification.
  4. Check the wiring where it runs along the exhaust.
  5. Note that the heater is usually integral to the sensor. If the element has failed, the sensor is the repair - so eliminate the wiring and fuse first, because the sensor is expensive.
