P2158

Vehicle Speed Sensor "B"

Severity
Medium
Driving
Drivable — book it in
System
Powertrain
Group
Fuel and air metering

A fault in the vehicle speed sensor "B" circuit. Vehicles often derive road speed from more than one source so that a single failure can be caught, and this is the second of them.

Possible causes

  • CommonFailed vehicle speed sensor
  • CommonCorroded connector or damaged wiring under the vehicle
  • CommonMetal debris on the sensor tip
  • PossibleDamaged reluctor ring
  • PossibleNon-standard wheel or tyre sizes changing the calculation
  • PossibleWheel speed sensor fault where the signal is derived from the ABS module

What you might notice

  • Speedometer dead or erratic
  • Cruise control unavailable
  • Harsh or erratic shifting
  • ABS or stability warning lights
  • Check engine light on

What to check, in order

  1. Compare the reported speed with a known-good reference on a steady drive.
  2. Inspect the connector and wiring under the vehicle for corrosion and impact damage.
  3. Inspect the sensor tip for metal debris.
  4. Check for ABS or wheel speed sensor codes - on many vehicles road speed is derived there.
  5. Ask whether non-standard wheels or tyres have been fitted.
