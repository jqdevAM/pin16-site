P0180

Fuel Temperature Sensor A Circuit

Severity
Low
Driving
Drivable — book it in
System
Powertrain
Group
Fuel and air metering

A fault in the circuit of fuel temperature sensor "A". The module uses fuel temperature to correct for the fact that warm fuel is less dense than cold fuel, so the same injector opening time delivers a different amount of energy. The correction is small, which is why the symptoms usually are too.

Possible causes

  • CommonFailed fuel temperature sensor
  • CommonCorroded or wet connector
  • CommonWiring fault between the sensor and the module
  • PossiblePoor earth at the sensor

What you might notice

  • Slightly rough running or hesitation
  • Hard starting in very hot or very cold conditions
  • Poor fuel economy
  • Check engine light on
  • Often nothing noticeable at all

What to check, in order

  1. Compare the fuel temperature reading against ambient with the vehicle standing overnight. They should be close; a large difference on a cold vehicle condemns the sensor.
  2. Inspect the connector for corrosion and water ingress. These sensors are frequently mounted low down or in the tank, where connectors suffer.
  3. Check the wiring and the earth before replacing the sensor.
  4. Do not expect dramatic symptoms. This sensor trims fuelling rather than determining it, so the code is often the only sign - which is not a reason to ignore it, because the trim it applies is wrong until it is fixed.
