P0137

O2 Sensor Circuit Low Voltage (Bank 1 Sensor 2)

Severity
Low
Driving
Drivable — book it in
System
Powertrain
Group
Fuel and air metering

Oxygen sensor 2 on bank 1 - a downstream sensor after the catalytic converter - is reporting persistently low voltage, meaning a lean signal. Because this sensor monitors the converter rather than controlling fuel, the effect on drivability is usually minimal, but it stops converter monitoring working.

Possible causes

  • CommonExhaust leak near the sensor letting fresh air in
  • CommonSensor signal wire shorted to ground
  • PossibleFailed oxygen sensor
  • PossibleCorroded connector under the car

What you might notice

  • Check engine light on
  • Usually no drivability change
  • Failed emissions test

What to check, in order

  1. Check for exhaust leaks around and ahead of the sensor - joints, flex sections and the converter seams.
  2. Inspect the wiring under the car for chafing, stone damage and corrosion.
  3. Check the connector for water and corrosion.
  4. Replace the sensor only after leaks and wiring have been eliminated.
