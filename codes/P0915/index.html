P0915

Gear Shift Position Circuit Range/Performance

Severity
High
Driving
Drive only to a garage
System
Powertrain
Group
Transmission and control modules

The gear shift position sensor is answering but its reading does not agree with what the module expects - the shaft is not ending up where the module believes it commanded. Usually lost calibration or mechanical wear rather than a dead sensor.

Possible causes

  • CommonPosition calibration lost or never performed
  • CommonWorn selector mechanism inside the gearbox
  • CommonFailing or drifting sensor
  • PossibleActuator not completing its travel
  • PossibleLow hydraulic pressure
  • PossibleWorn synchromesh making engagement slow

What you might notice

  • Slow, harsh or failed gear changes
  • Some gears unavailable
  • Transmission dropping into neutral
  • Grinding on engagement
  • Transmission warning message

What to check, in order

  1. Run the position calibration procedure first.
  2. Watch reported against commanded position through a full sweep.
  3. Check hydraulic pressure on a hydraulically actuated system.
  4. Consider gearbox wear. Worn synchromesh makes engagement slow enough for the module to call it a fault, and no amount of electrical work will fix that.
  5. Read all codes for actuator or hydraulic faults alongside.
