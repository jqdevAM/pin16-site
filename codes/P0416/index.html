P0416

Secondary Air Injection System Switching Valve "B" Circuit Open

Severity
Low
Driving
Drivable — book it in
System
Powertrain
Group
Auxiliary emission controls

The circuit for secondary air injection switching valve "B" is open - a broken wire, a disconnected plug, or a failed valve winding. The module cannot operate the valve at all.

Possible causes

  • CommonOpen winding in the valve solenoid
  • CommonBroken wire or disconnected connector
  • CommonCorroded connector at the valve
  • PossibleBlown fuse on the circuit
  • RareFailed control module driver

What you might notice

  • Check engine light on
  • Failed emissions test
  • Secondary air system inoperative
  • Usually no symptom the driver notices

What to check, in order

  1. Check the connector is actually plugged in. This valve sits in a part of the engine bay that gets disturbed during other work.
  2. Measure the valve winding resistance - an open circuit reading confirms a failed valve.
  3. Check continuity through the wiring between the module and the valve.
  4. Check the fuse for the circuit.
  5. Inspect the harness for heat damage near the exhaust.
