P0654

Engine RPM Output Circuit

Severity
Low
Driving
Drivable — book it in
System
Powertrain
Group
Computer output circuits and control module

A fault in the engine speed output circuit - the wire by which the module tells other systems how fast the engine is turning. The engine itself is unaffected; what fails is whatever was relying on being told.

Possible causes

  • CommonWiring or connector fault on the output circuit
  • CommonCorroded connector at either end
  • PossibleFault in the module receiving the signal, loading the circuit
  • PossibleChafed harness shorting against the body or engine
  • PossibleAftermarket equipment spliced into the circuit
  • RareFailed control module output driver

What you might notice

  • Rev counter dead or reading wrongly
  • Check engine light on
  • Usually no effect on how the car drives

What to check, in order

  1. Check whether the rev counter works. If it does, the fault is in the circuit rather than in what it feeds.
  2. Inspect the connectors at both ends.
  3. Check the wiring for chafe damage and for aftermarket splices - rev limiters, shift lights and performance gauges are commonly wired into this circuit.
