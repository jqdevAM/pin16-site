P0923

Gear Shift Forward Actuator Circuit High

Severity
High
Driving
Drive only to a garage
System
Powertrain
Group
Transmission and control modules

The gear shift forward actuator circuit is reading high - typically shorted to voltage or a lost earth. The module cannot drive that actuator, so gears requiring movement in the forward direction cannot be engaged.

Possible causes

  • CommonWiring shorted to voltage or a lost earth between the module and the actuator
  • CommonCorroded or water-ingressed connector at the gearbox
  • CommonOpen or shorted actuator winding
  • PossibleChafed harness at the gearbox
  • RareFailed control module driver

What you might notice

  • Some gears unavailable
  • Car stuck in neutral
  • Transmission warning message
  • Check engine light on

What to check, in order

  1. Disconnect the actuator and check whether the high reading persists at the harness.
  2. Inspect the connector for corrosion and water.
  3. Measure the actuator winding against specification.
  4. Note which gears still work - that tells you whether one actuator or a shared circuit is at fault.
  5. Run the calibration procedure after any repair.
