P0410

Secondary Air Injection System

Severity
Low
Driving
Drivable — book it in
System
Powertrain
Group
Auxiliary emission controls
Important. These systems are fitted mainly to petrol engines. If you are looking at a diesel, check you have identified the right system before ordering parts.

A general fault in the secondary air injection system. This system pumps fresh air into the exhaust for a minute or two after a cold start. The extra oxygen lets unburnt fuel finish burning in the exhaust, which cuts emissions during the dirtiest part of a journey and warms the catalytic converter up faster. It only runs briefly and only when cold, which is why a fault here usually changes nothing the driver can feel.

Possible causes

  • CommonFailed air pump - very often seized after ingesting water
  • CommonFailed one-way check valve letting exhaust gas back into the pump
  • CommonBlocked or corroded air passages into the exhaust manifold
  • CommonFailed switching or control valve
  • PossibleSplit, perished or disconnected hoses
  • PossibleBlown fuse or failed relay on the pump circuit

What you might notice

  • Check engine light on
  • Failed emissions test
  • Loud noise from the engine bay for a minute or so after a cold start, or the absence of a noise that used to happen
  • Rough running for the first minute from cold
  • Frequently no symptom at all

What to check, in order

  1. Test from a genuinely cold start. The system only runs for a minute or two after starting a cold engine, so a warm restart will not reproduce the fault however far you drive.
  2. Listen on a cold start. A working pump makes an obvious whirring or buzzing noise for a minute; silence when there used to be noise is a clear finding.
  3. Check the fuse and relay for the pump before anything mechanical.
  4. Inspect the hoses between the pump, the valve and the exhaust for splits and disconnection.
  5. Check the one-way check valve. If it has failed, exhaust gas and moisture have been reaching the pump, and the pump is probably the next thing to fail.
  6. Check the air passages into the exhaust manifold for carbon blockage on a high-mileage engine.
