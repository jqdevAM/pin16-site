P0836

Four Wheel Drive (4WD) Switch Circuit

Severity
Medium
Driving
Drivable — book it in
System
Powertrain
Group
Transmission

A fault in the four-wheel drive switch circuit - the switch or selector by which the driver requests four-wheel drive. The module cannot tell what has been selected, so it will usually stay in whatever mode it considers safe, typically two-wheel drive.

Possible causes

  • CommonFailed 4WD selector switch
  • CommonWiring or connector fault at the switch
  • CommonCorroded connector under the vehicle or in the console
  • PossibleWorn switch contacts from repeated use
  • RareFailed control module input

What you might notice

  • Four-wheel drive will not engage
  • Mode indicator not changing or flashing
  • Stuck in one drive mode
  • Check engine light on

What to check, in order

  1. Watch the switch state on the live data screen while operating the selector through every position.
  2. Inspect the switch connector. Console switches suffer from spilled drinks; underbody connectors suffer from salt.
  3. Check the wiring between the switch and the module.
  4. Check the transfer case fluid level while underneath.
  5. Check for transfer case codes stored alongside, which would move the diagnosis away from the switch.
