U0166

Lost Communication With Auxiliary Heater Control Module

Severity
Low
Driving
No driving restriction
System
Network and communication
Group
Network communication
Important. One exception to this being a comfort-only fault: on many vehicles the same module runs the windscreen demister and the air conditioning compressor that dries the air. If you cannot clear a misted or frozen screen, that is a visibility problem and worth fixing before winter driving.

The control module has stopped receiving messages from the auxiliary heater control module. Heating, ventilation or air conditioning will not respond, and the system usually falls back to a fixed default setting. Nothing about the way the car drives is affected.

Possible causes

  • CommonLow battery voltage or a discharged battery, dropping modules off the bus
  • CommonBlown fuse or corroded earth on the supply to the auxiliary heater control module
  • CommonCorroded or water-ingressed connector - blocked drains soak footwell electrics
  • PossibleDamaged, chafed or corroded bus wiring
  • PossibleFailed module
  • PossibleConnector disturbed during dashboard or heater work

What you might notice

  • Climate control unresponsive or stuck on one setting
  • Blower not working, or running at one fixed speed
  • Temperature not adjustable
  • Display blank
  • Heated steering wheel inoperative

What to check, in order

  1. Check battery voltage and state of charge before anything else. A weak or failing battery drops modules off the bus and sets lost-communication codes with no wiring fault at all - this is the single most common false lead in this whole family.
  2. Check the fuses and earths for the auxiliary heater control module.
  3. Look for water ingress. Blocked bulkhead and sunroof drains soak the footwell, and climate modules are often mounted right there.
  4. Ask whether the dashboard or heater has been worked on recently. A partially seated connector is a common cause.
  5. Note how many U-codes are stored. Several at once points at battery, earth or bus wiring.
