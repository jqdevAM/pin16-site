P2788

Auto Shift Manual Adaptive Learning at Limit

Severity
High
Driving
Drive only to a garage
System
Powertrain
Group
Transmission

The automated manual transmission's adaptive learning has reached the end of its adjustment range. These transmissions continuously learn where the clutch bites and how long each engagement takes, compensating for wear as it happens. Reaching the limit means there is no compensation left - which on a high-mileage vehicle usually means the clutch is simply worn out.

Possible causes

  • CommonClutch worn out, beyond what adaptation can compensate for
  • CommonClutch contaminated with oil from a leaking seal
  • CommonActuator worn or not achieving full travel
  • CommonAdaptation never reset after a clutch replacement
  • PossibleLow hydraulic pressure limiting clamping force
  • PossibleFaulty position sensor misreporting where the clutch is

What you might notice

  • Judder or slipping when moving off
  • Harsh or slow gear changes
  • Loss of power under acceleration
  • Burning smell
  • Transmission warning message

What to check, in order

  1. Ask whether a clutch has been fitted recently without resetting the adaptation. If so, resetting it is the repair and nothing needs replacing.
  2. Assess clutch wear. On a high-mileage vehicle this code usually means the clutch has reached the end of its life, and no amount of electrical diagnosis changes that.
  3. Look for oil contamination. A leaking crankshaft rear seal or gearbox input seal soaks the clutch, and replacing the clutch without fixing the leak repeats the failure.
  4. Run the adaptation procedure and see whether it completes. If it immediately returns to the limit, the mechanical condition is the answer.
  5. Do not keep driving a clutch that is slipping - it destroys itself quickly and can damage the flywheel with it.
