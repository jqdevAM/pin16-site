P2098

Post Catalyst Fuel Trim System Too Lean (Bank 2)

Severity
Medium
Driving
Drivable — book it in
System
Powertrain
Group
Fuel, air metering and auxiliary emission controls

The mixture measured after the catalytic converter on bank 2 is too lean - too much air for the fuel being delivered - and the module has run out of adjustment trying to correct it. The most common cause is not fuelling at all: an exhaust leak between the converter and the sensor lets air in, and the sensor honestly reports a lean mixture that does not exist.

Possible causes

  • CommonExhaust leak between the converter and the downstream sensor
  • CommonFailing downstream oxygen sensor
  • CommonVacuum leak
  • PossibleFailing catalytic converter
  • PossibleFuel delivery problem - weak pump, blocked filter, dirty injectors
  • PossibleUpstream oxygen sensor drifting

What you might notice

  • Check engine light on
  • Failed emissions test
  • Worse fuel economy
  • Rough running in some conditions
  • Often no symptom the driver notices

What to check, in order

  1. Check for exhaust leaks first, particularly at the joints and welds around the converter and the sensor. This is the most common cause and it is far cheaper than anything else on the list.
  2. Read all codes. Upstream fuel trim codes alongside this one mean the mixture really is wrong and the problem is upstream, not after the converter.
  3. Watch both the upstream and downstream sensor readings on the live data screen.
  4. Do not replace the catalytic converter on this code alone. It is the most expensive item here and rarely the cause.
  5. Check for vacuum leaks, which produce a lean condition throughout.
