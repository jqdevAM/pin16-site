P0956

Auto Shift Manual Mode Circuit Range/Performance

Severity
Low
Driving
No driving restriction
System
Powertrain
Group
Transmission and control modules

The auto shift manual mode circuit is answering but its reading is implausible - manual mode reported when the selector is not there, or a mode state that does not match the selector position. Usually a worn or contaminated selector rather than a wiring fault.

Possible causes

  • CommonWorn switch contacts giving an ambiguous reading
  • CommonDrink or dirt in the selector assembly
  • CommonWorn selector gate mechanism
  • PossibleWiring fault causing a partial short between circuits
  • RareFailed control module input

What you might notice

  • Manual mode engaging or disengaging on its own
  • Mode indicator disagreeing with the selector
  • Shifts ignored or unexpected
  • Check engine light on

What to check, in order

  1. Move the selector slowly between modes while watching the reported state. Look for a position that reads ambiguously - the classic sign of worn contacts.
  2. Inspect the selector for spilled drink.
  3. Check the gate mechanism for wear and free play.
  4. Inspect the connector for a partial short between adjacent pins.
  5. Check for other selector codes stored alongside.
