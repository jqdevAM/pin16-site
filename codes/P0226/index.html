P0226

Throttle/Pedal Position Sensor/Switch "C" Circuit Range/Performance

Severity
High
Driving
Drive only to a garage
System
Powertrain
Group
Fuel and air metering - injector circuits

Throttle or pedal position sensor "C" is producing a signal that is within its electrical range but does not agree with what the module expects from the other inputs. The sensor is not obviously broken - it is disagreeing, which is often harder to find and just as likely to trigger reduced power.

Possible causes

  • CommonSensor drifting out of calibration with wear
  • CommonCarbon build-up on the throttle plate
  • PossiblePoor connection adding resistance to the signal
  • PossibleVacuum leak making actual airflow disagree with throttle angle
  • PossibleThrottle body mechanically worn or sticking

What you might notice

  • Hesitation or surging under light throttle
  • Reduced power mode
  • Unstable idle
  • Warning light on

What to check, in order

  1. Watch pedal position against throttle position on the live data screen through a slow full sweep of the pedal. They should track each other smoothly.
  2. Look for carbon build-up around the throttle plate, which stops it closing fully and makes the reported angle disagree with reality.
  3. Check for vacuum leaks - unmetered air makes a correct throttle angle look wrong.
  4. Inspect the connector for corrosion adding resistance.
  5. Check for other stored codes; a correlation code alongside this one narrows it to the sensor rather than the air path.
