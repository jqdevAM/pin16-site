P0883

TCM Power Input Signal High

Severity
High
Driving
Drive only to a garage
System
Powertrain
Group
Transmission

The transmission module's supply voltage is high - typically an overcharging alternator or a faulty voltage regulator. Solenoids will not be driven accurately at the wrong voltage, so the module protects itself and the transmission by dropping into a failsafe.

Possible causes

  • CommonAn overcharging alternator or a faulty voltage regulator
  • CommonCorroded or loose battery terminals
  • CommonCorroded earth point
  • CommonFailing alternator
  • PossibleDamaged supply wiring
  • PossibleFailing supply relay

What you might notice

  • Harsh shifting or limp mode
  • Battery or charging warning light
  • Lights dimming or unusually bright
  • Slow cranking
  • Check engine light on

What to check, in order

  1. Measure battery and charging voltage before touching anything else.
  2. An overcharging alternator boils the battery and damages electronics across the whole vehicle. If voltage is high, treat it as urgent rather than as a transmission problem.
  3. Perform a voltage drop test on the supply and earth circuits.
  4. Clean and tighten the battery terminals and earth straps.
  5. Check the supply fuses and relay.
  6. Fix the electrical supply before diagnosing anything else the transmission is doing. Almost every other symptom will follow it.
