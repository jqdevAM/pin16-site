P2795

Gear Shift Direction Circuit High

Severity
High
Driving
Drive only to a garage
System
Powertrain
Group
Transmission

A fault in the gear shift direction circuit - a high reading, usually a short to voltage or a lost earth. Inside an automated manual gearbox the selector shaft moves on two axes, exactly as a driver's hand does: this one moves the selector shaft fore and aft to engage a gear. Without control of it, gear changes will fail.

Possible causes

  • CommonFailed direction control solenoid or actuator
  • CommonCorroded or damaged connector at the gearbox
  • CommonWiring damage in the actuator harness
  • PossibleLow hydraulic pressure on a hydraulically actuated system
  • PossibleSticking selector mechanism
  • RareFailed control module driver

What you might notice

  • Transmission will not select gears
  • Car stuck in neutral
  • Gear changes failing in one direction only
  • Transmission warning message
  • Check engine light on

What to check, in order

  1. Note which gears are still available. If only movement in one direction fails, that confirms one circuit rather than a shared fault.
  2. Inspect the connector at the gearbox for corrosion and water.
  3. Measure the solenoid or actuator winding resistance against specification.
  4. Check hydraulic pressure on a hydraulically actuated system - a slow actuator is more often a pressure problem than a failed motor.
  5. Run the position calibration procedure after any repair - it is routine on these transmissions, not a last resort.
