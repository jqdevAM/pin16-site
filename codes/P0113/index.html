P0113

Intake Air Temperature Sensor 1 Circuit High

Severity
Low
Driving
Drivable — book it in
System
Powertrain
Group
Fuel and air metering

The intake air temperature sensor circuit is reading high - outside the valid range rather than merely inaccurate. This normally means a wiring fault or a failed sensor, since a working sensor cannot physically report a value outside the range.

Possible causes

  • CommonOpen or shorted sensor wiring
  • CommonFailed temperature sensor
  • PossibleCorroded or disconnected connector

What you might notice

  • Check engine light on
  • Slightly worse fuel economy
  • Occasionally harder cold starting

What to check, in order

  1. Check the connector is seated and free of corrosion.
  2. Check continuity of the sensor wiring back to the module - an open circuit and a short produce the two opposite versions of this code.
  3. Compare the reading against ambient temperature on the live data screen.
  4. Replace the sensor once the wiring is confirmed good.
