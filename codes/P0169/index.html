P0169

Incorrect Fuel Composition

Severity
Medium
Driving
Short journeys only
System
Powertrain
Group
Fuel and air metering
Important. If the wrong fuel has just been put in, do not start the engine. Almost all of the damage from misfuelling happens when the fuel is pumped through the system, not when it sits in the tank.

The fuel in the tank is not what the module expects. On a flexible fuel vehicle the module measures how much ethanol the fuel contains and adjusts fuelling and timing to suit, because ethanol needs a richer mixture than petrol. This code means that measurement is outside the range the vehicle can adapt to - either the fuel really is wrong, or the sensor measuring it is.

Possible causes

  • CommonFuel filled that the vehicle is not designed to run on
  • CommonFuel composition sensor fault or drift
  • CommonSensor not yet relearned after a fuel change - the module needs a few miles to adapt
  • PossibleWater or other contamination in the fuel
  • PossibleWiring or connector fault at the composition sensor

What you might notice

  • Rough running, hesitation or misfire
  • Hard starting, especially in cold weather
  • Loss of power
  • Poor fuel economy
  • Check engine light on

What to check, in order

  1. Establish what was last put in the tank, and where. This code very often follows a fill at an unfamiliar pump, and the answer is in the receipt rather than in the wiring.
  2. Read the ethanol percentage on live data and compare it against what should have been filled. A vehicle running on ordinary petrol should read close to the local blend.
  3. Drive a few miles after a fuel change before condemning anything. The module needs distance to relearn, and the code can appear simply because it has not finished.
  4. If genuinely wrong fuel has been added, have the tank drained rather than diluting it. Running an engine on fuel it was not built for causes damage that costs far more than a drain.
  5. Check for water contamination. Water gives readings that look like an ethanol fault and damages the injection system in its own right.
