P0657

Actuator Supply Voltage "A" Circuit/Open

Severity
High
Driving
Drive only to a garage
System
Powertrain
Group
Computer output circuits and control module

An open circuit or general fault in actuator supply voltage "A". This is the supply the module provides to a group of actuators - solenoids, valves, relays - rather than to sensors. When it is lost, everything on that supply stops working at once, so expect several actuator codes together rather than one.

Possible causes

  • CommonBlown fuse on the actuator supply
  • CommonWiring or connector fault on the supply circuit
  • CommonOne actuator failed internally and shorted the supply
  • PossibleCorroded or water-ingressed connector
  • PossibleFailed relay feeding the supply
  • PossibleFailed control module supply driver

What you might notice

  • Several actuator codes at once
  • Limp mode or reduced power
  • Engine running badly or not starting
  • Multiple systems inoperative together
  • Check engine light on

What to check, in order

  1. Check the fuses first. A blown fuse on a shared supply is the cheapest possible cause and it explains every code stored alongside.
  2. List which actuators have faulted - they share supply "A", and that list tells you which circuit to trace.
  3. Disconnect actuators on that supply one at a time while watching the supply voltage. Recovery identifies the one that is shorting it.
  4. Inspect connectors for water and corrosion.
  5. Do not diagnose the individual actuator codes until the supply is restored.
