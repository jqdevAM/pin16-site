P0595

Cruise Control Servo Control Circuit Low

Severity
Low
Driving
No driving restriction
System
Powertrain
Group
Vehicle speed control, idle control and auxiliary inputs

The cruise control servo circuit is reading low - typically shorted to earth or an open circuit. The module cannot drive the servo, so cruise control will be unavailable.

Possible causes

  • CommonFailed vacuum or vent solenoid
  • CommonSplit, perished or disconnected vacuum hose
  • CommonCorroded connector or damaged wiring
  • PossibleFailed vacuum supply - a leaking non-return valve or a weak vacuum pump
  • PossibleFailed cruise control servo
  • RareFailed control module driver

What you might notice

  • Cruise control will not hold a speed
  • Cruise control drifting or surging
  • Cruise control cancelling on its own
  • Cruise control will not engage
  • Check engine light on

What to check, in order

  1. Disconnect the servo and check whether the low reading persists at the harness.
  2. Check the vacuum hoses first. On a vacuum-operated cruise system a perished or disconnected hose is the most common cause and the cheapest to fix.
  3. Measure the solenoid winding resistance against specification.
  4. Inspect the connector for corrosion.
  5. Check the vacuum supply and its non-return valve.
  6. Confirm ordinary driving is unaffected before deciding how urgent this is.
