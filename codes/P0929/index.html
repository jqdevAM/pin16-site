P0929

Gear Shift Lock Solenoid Control Circuit Range/Performance

Severity
High
Driving
Drivable — book it in
System
Powertrain
Group
Transmission and control modules

The gear shift lock solenoid circuit is electrically sound but the lock is not behaving as commanded - slow to release, not releasing, or not locking. Often the solenoid is weak or the mechanism is sticking rather than the circuit being at fault.

Possible causes

  • CommonWeak or sticking shift lock solenoid
  • CommonMechanism sticky with spilled drink or dirt
  • CommonBrake switch marginal or misadjusted
  • PossibleWorn selector detent mechanism
  • PossibleLow battery voltage weakening the solenoid

What you might notice

  • Selector needing several attempts to leave park
  • Lock releasing without the brake pedal
  • Lever feeling sticky in the gate
  • Check engine light on

What to check, in order

  1. Check the brake switch adjustment and confirm the brake lights work.
  2. Check battery voltage - a weak battery makes a marginal solenoid behave exactly like this.
  3. Inspect the selector assembly for spilled drink, which is the most common mechanical cause.
  4. Test whether the lever will leave park without the brake, and park with the handbrake fully applied if it will.
  5. Locate the manual override release in the handbook before you need it.
