Computer output circuits and control module

The control module itself and the output circuits it drives - relays, solenoids and actuators - plus internal memory and processor faults.

No open-source descriptions exist for this system, so its codes are read from their structure.

Manufacturer-specific. Check a source specific to your make and model year. The subsystem digit is still a reliable hint.
