P2257

Secondary Air Injection System Control "A" Circuit Low

Severity
Low
Driving
Drivable — book it in
System
Powertrain
Group
Fuel and air metering - injector circuits

Secondary air injection control circuit "A" is reading low - typically shorted to earth or open circuit. The module cannot switch the pump on, so the system does nothing after a cold start.

Possible causes

  • CommonWiring shorted to earth or open circuit between the module and the relay
  • CommonFailed relay
  • CommonCorroded relay socket
  • PossibleBlown fuse
  • RareFailed control module driver

What you might notice

  • No pump noise on a cold start
  • Check engine light on
  • Failed emissions test
  • Usually no symptom the driver notices

What to check, in order

  1. Check whether the low reading persists with the relay removed. That separates the relay from the wiring.
  2. Swap the relay with an identical one and see whether the fault follows.
  3. Check the fuse for the pump circuit.
  4. Inspect the relay socket for corrosion.
  5. Check the wiring between the module and the relay.
