P0893

Multiple Gears Engaged

Severity
Critical
Driving
Stop driving
System
Powertrain
Group
Transmission
Important. A transmission that engages two gears at once can lock the driven wheels while the car is moving. Have it recovered rather than driven, even a short distance.

The transmission has engaged more than one gear at once. An automatic gearbox cannot hold two gears simultaneously - the gearsets fight each other, and at worst the transmission locks up. If that happens while the car is moving, the driven wheels can lock, which on a wet or loose surface means losing control.

Possible causes

  • CommonShift solenoid stuck on, applying a clutch that should be released
  • CommonSticking valve in the valve body
  • CommonDebris from internal wear jamming the hydraulics
  • PossibleInternal seal failure applying a clutch unintentionally
  • PossibleWiring fault holding a solenoid energised
  • RareControl module fault commanding two gears

What you might notice

  • Severe jolting or banging from the transmission
  • Engine bogging down or stalling
  • Transmission locking up momentarily
  • Loss of drive
  • Grinding or binding noises

What to check, in order

  1. Stop driving. This is one of the few transmission faults that can cause a loss of control rather than just a breakdown, and it is worth the cost of recovery.
  2. Do not clear the code and carry on to see whether it returns. It can lock up at any speed.
  3. Check the fluid level and condition once the vehicle is somewhere safe - debris in the fluid is the most common cause.
  4. Read all codes. A stuck solenoid code alongside this one names the likely cause.
  5. Check whether a solenoid circuit is being held energised electrically before assuming a mechanical jam.
  6. Expect the pan to come off. Whatever jammed the hydraulics is still in there.
