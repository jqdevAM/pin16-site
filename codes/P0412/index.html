P0412

Secondary Air Injection System Switching Valve "A" Circuit

Severity
Low
Driving
Drivable — book it in
System
Powertrain
Group
Auxiliary emission controls

A fault in the circuit for secondary air injection switching valve "A". The switching valve is what routes the pump's air into the exhaust, and closes to keep exhaust gas out again once the system has finished its cold-start run. A circuit fault here means the module cannot operate the valve.

Possible causes

  • CommonFailed switching valve solenoid
  • CommonCorroded connector at the valve
  • CommonWiring damage between the module and the valve
  • PossibleBlown fuse on the circuit
  • PossibleVacuum hose failure on a vacuum-operated valve
  • RareFailed control module driver

What you might notice

  • Check engine light on
  • Failed emissions test
  • Secondary air system inoperative
  • Usually no symptom the driver notices

What to check, in order

  1. Inspect the connector at the valve. It sits in a hot area near the exhaust and its connector suffers for it.
  2. Measure the valve solenoid resistance against specification.
  3. On a vacuum-operated valve, check the vacuum hose is intact and that vacuum reaches the valve when commanded.
  4. Check the fuse for the circuit.
  5. Check the harness for heat and chafe damage where it runs near the exhaust.
