P0313

Misfire Detected with Low Fuel

Severity
Medium
Driving
Drivable — book it in
System
Powertrain
Group
Ignition system or misfire

A misfire was detected while the fuel level was low. The control module reports this separately because running out of fuel causes a misfire that is not a mechanical fault - the engine is simply being starved. It is the module telling you it noticed the tank was nearly empty.

Possible causes

  • CommonFuel tank ran very low or empty
  • PossibleFaulty fuel level sensor reporting a low level incorrectly
  • PossibleWeak fuel pump unable to draw from a low tank

What you might notice

  • Engine stumbling or cutting out when the tank is nearly empty
  • Misfire that disappears after refuelling

What to check, in order

  1. Refuel, clear the code and drive normally. If it does not return, that was the cause.
  2. If the code returns with a full tank, check the fuel level sensor reading against the actual level.
  3. Persistent misfire with plenty of fuel in the tank points at the fuel pump or a blocked pickup.
