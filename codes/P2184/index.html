P2184

Engine Coolant Temperature Sensor 2 Circuit Low

Severity
Medium
Driving
Drivable — book it in
System
Powertrain
Group
Fuel and air metering

The coolant temperature sensor 2 signal is reading low. On a typical sensor whose resistance falls as temperature rises, that reads as implausibly hot; electrically it is usually a short to earth.

Possible causes

  • CommonFailed coolant temperature sensor
  • CommonCorroded connector or damaged wiring
  • CommonLow coolant level, leaving the sensor uncovered
  • CommonAir lock in the cooling system after a previous repair
  • PossibleLoss of reference voltage or earth
  • PossibleThermostat fault, so the temperature genuinely is wrong

What you might notice

  • Temperature gauge wrong or erratic
  • Cooling fans running constantly or not at all
  • Poor fuel economy
  • Hard starting
  • Heater blowing cool
  • Check engine light on

What to check, in order

  1. Compare the reported temperature with the other coolant sensor and with ambient on a vehicle that has stood overnight. All should agree closely when cold.
  2. Check the coolant level and look for leaks. A low level uncovers the sensor and produces these codes with a good sensor.
  3. Inspect the connector for corrosion.
  4. Check the reference voltage and earth at the sensor.
  5. Watch the temperature climb from cold on a drive - it should rise smoothly and settle.
