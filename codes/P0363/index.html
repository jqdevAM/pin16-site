P0363

Misfire Detected - Fueling Disabled

Severity
High
Driving
Short journeys only
System
Powertrain
Group
Ignition system or misfire
Important. The rough running is the protection, not the fault. The module has shut a cylinder down to save the catalytic converter, and it will keep it shut down until the underlying misfire is repaired.

The module has detected a misfire severe enough that it has stopped injecting fuel into the affected cylinder. That is a deliberate protective action: unburnt fuel reaching the catalytic converter overheats and destroys it, so the module would rather run on fewer cylinders than pay for a converter. The engine will feel rough and down on power, and that is the protection working.

Possible causes

  • CommonFailed ignition coil or worn spark plug on that cylinder
  • CommonFailed or blocked fuel injector
  • CommonLoss of compression - burnt valve, failed head gasket, worn rings
  • PossibleVacuum leak affecting that cylinder
  • PossibleWiring fault on the coil or injector circuit
  • PossibleCamshaft or timing problem

What you might notice

  • Engine running very roughly
  • Significant loss of power
  • Check engine light flashing
  • Engine shaking at idle
  • Smell of unburnt fuel

What to check, in order

  1. Read all codes. A misfire code names the cylinder, and that is where to start.
  2. Do not clear this and carry on. The module disabled that cylinder for a reason, and re-enabling it by clearing codes puts the converter back at risk.
  3. Check the ignition coil and spark plug for the named cylinder first - the cheapest and most likely causes.
  4. If ignition and fuel both check out, test compression on that cylinder. A mechanical problem is the remaining explanation.
  5. Have the vehicle recovered rather than driven any distance. Running on fewer cylinders loads the engine mountings and the remaining cylinders unevenly.
