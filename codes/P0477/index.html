P0477

Exhaust Pressure Control Valve Low

Severity
Medium
Driving
Drivable — book it in
System
Powertrain
Group
Auxiliary emission controls

The exhaust pressure control valve circuit is reading low - typically shorted to earth or an open circuit.

Possible causes

  • CommonCarbon or soot seizing the valve mechanism
  • CommonFailed control solenoid or actuator
  • CommonSplit, perished or disconnected vacuum hose
  • CommonCorroded connector or damaged wiring
  • PossibleBlown fuse on the circuit
  • RareFailed control module driver

What you might notice

  • Reduced power or limp mode
  • Slow to warm up in cold weather
  • Poor cabin heating
  • Excessive smoke
  • Particulate filter regeneration problems
  • Check engine light on

What to check, in order

  1. Check the vacuum hoses first on a vacuum-operated system.
  2. Check the valve mechanism moves freely - soot seizing it is common on a diesel used mainly for short journeys.
  3. Measure the solenoid winding resistance against specification.
  4. Inspect the connector for corrosion and heat damage.
  5. Read all codes - particulate filter codes alongside this one matter more.
