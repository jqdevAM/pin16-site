P0737

TCM Engine Speed Output Circuit

Severity
Medium
Driving
Drivable — book it in
System
Powertrain
Group
Transmission

A fault in the circuit the transmission module uses to send engine speed information onward - to the instrument cluster, the engine module or another system. The transmission itself is generally unaffected; what fails is something downstream that was relying on that signal.

Possible causes

  • CommonWiring or connector fault on the output circuit
  • CommonCorroded connector at the transmission module
  • PossibleFault in the module receiving the signal, loading the circuit
  • PossibleFailed transmission control module output driver
  • PossibleAftermarket equipment spliced into the circuit

What you might notice

  • Rev counter reading wrong or dead
  • Check engine light on
  • Often no drivability symptom at all

What to check, in order

  1. Establish what actually uses this signal on the vehicle, then check whether that thing works. If the rev counter is fine, the fault is in the circuit rather than in what it feeds.
  2. Inspect the connectors at both ends of the circuit.
  3. Check the wiring for chafing and shorts.
  4. Ask whether aftermarket equipment - a rev limiter, an alarm, a performance gauge - has been spliced into this wire.
  5. Check for other transmission codes. This one rarely matters on its own.
