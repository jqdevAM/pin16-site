P0440

Evaporative Emission System

Severity
Low
Driving
No driving restriction
System
Powertrain
Group
Auxiliary emission controls

A general fault has been detected in the evaporative emissions system, without the module narrowing it to a specific leak size or component. It captures fuel vapour from the tank and feeds it to the engine to be burnt; a fault here affects emissions rather than how the car drives.

Possible causes

  • CommonLoose or faulty fuel filler cap
  • CommonLeaking or disconnected EVAP hose
  • PossibleFaulty purge or vent valve
  • PossibleDamaged charcoal canister
  • PossibleFaulty EVAP pressure sensor

What you might notice

  • Check engine light on with no drivability change
  • Possible faint fuel smell
  • Failed emissions test

What to check, in order

  1. Check the fuel cap first - cheapest and most likely.
  2. Look for other EVAP codes stored alongside this one. A more specific code will point at the component.
  3. Inspect visible EVAP hoses and the canister for obvious damage.
  4. If nothing is obvious, a workshop smoke test is the efficient next step.
