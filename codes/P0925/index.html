P0925

Gear Shift Reverse Actuator Circuit Range/Performance

Severity
High
Driving
Drive only to a garage
System
Powertrain
Group
Transmission and control modules

The gear shift reverse actuator circuit is electrically sound but the actuator is not performing as expected - slow, weak, or not reaching its position. The cause is mechanical or hydraulic rather than electrical.

Possible causes

  • CommonLow hydraulic pressure on a hydraulically actuated system
  • CommonWorn or failing actuator
  • CommonSticking selector mechanism
  • CommonCalibration lost or never performed
  • PossibleWorn synchromesh making engagement slow
  • PossibleHigh resistance in the wiring

What you might notice

  • Slow gear changes in the reverse direction
  • Some gears unavailable
  • Grinding on engagement
  • Transmission warning message
  • Check engine light on

What to check, in order

  1. Check hydraulic pressure first on a hydraulically actuated system.
  2. Run the calibration procedure.
  3. Watch commanded against actual position through a full sweep.
  4. Check the hydraulic fluid level and condition.
  5. Consider gearbox wear if engagement grinds rather than simply being slow.
