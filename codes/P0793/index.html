P0793

Intermediate Shaft Speed Sensor "A" Circuit No Signal

Severity
High
Driving
Drive only to a garage
System
Powertrain
Group
Transmission

The intermediate shaft speed sensor "A" is producing no signal at all. The module is receiving nothing where it expects a speed reading, so it cannot confirm what the transmission is doing and will fall back to a failsafe - typically one fixed gear, with harsh engagement.

Possible causes

  • CommonFailed speed sensor
  • CommonOpen circuit in the wiring or a disconnected connector
  • CommonSensor tip packed with metal debris
  • PossibleDamaged, sheared or missing reluctor ring
  • PossibleInternal transmission harness failure
  • PossibleSensor not refitted or its connector left off after previous work

What you might notice

  • Transmission stuck in one gear
  • Very harsh shifts or no shifting
  • Speedometer dead on vehicles that take speed from this sensor
  • Cruise control unavailable
  • Check engine light on

What to check, in order

  1. Ask whether the transmission or a nearby component has been worked on recently. A connector left unplugged is the most common cause of an outright missing signal.
  2. Check for continuity through the sensor circuit at the module connector.
  3. Measure the sensor resistance against specification, and inspect its tip for debris.
  4. Check the reluctor ring the sensor reads, if it can be seen. A sheared ring produces exactly this with a good sensor.
  5. Check the fluid level and condition.
