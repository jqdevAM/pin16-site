P2112

Throttle Actuator Control System - Stuck Closed

Severity
Critical
Driving
Drive only to a garage
System
Powertrain
Group
Fuel and air metering
Important. Forced idle or a stuck-closed throttle means the vehicle cannot accelerate. Do not attempt to join fast-moving traffic - it will not respond.

The throttle plate is stuck closed. The module commanded it open and it did not move, so the engine can only idle - the car will barely move, if at all. Carbon seizing the plate is again the usual reason.

Possible causes

  • CommonCarbon seizing the throttle plate in its bore
  • CommonFailed throttle actuator motor
  • CommonCorroded connector or wiring fault, so the motor is never driven
  • PossibleForeign object jamming the plate
  • PossibleDamaged throttle body

What you might notice

  • Car will not accelerate
  • Engine idles but will not rev
  • Reduced power warning
  • Engine may stall

What to check, in order

  1. The car may be unable to move under its own power. Arrange recovery rather than trying to coax it along.
  2. Remove and inspect the throttle body for carbon and for anything jamming the plate.
  3. Check the motor is being driven electrically before assuming a mechanical jam.
  4. Run the throttle relearn procedure after any cleaning or replacement.
