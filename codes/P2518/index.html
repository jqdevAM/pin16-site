P2518

A/C Refrigerant Pressure Sensor "B" Circuit High

Severity
Low
Driving
No driving restriction
System
Powertrain
Group
Vehicle speed control, idle control and auxiliary inputs

The refrigerant pressure signal from sensor "B" is reading high - typically shorted to voltage or with a lost earth, though genuinely excessive pressure from a blocked condenser will also do it.

Possible causes

  • CommonLow refrigerant charge, usually from a slow leak
  • CommonFailed refrigerant pressure sensor
  • CommonCorroded connector or damaged wiring
  • PossibleBlocked condenser restricting flow
  • PossibleLoss of reference voltage or earth
  • PossibleFailed compressor

What you might notice

  • Air conditioning not cooling
  • Compressor not engaging
  • Cooling fans running constantly
  • Air conditioning cutting in and out
  • Check engine light on

What to check, in order

  1. Have the refrigerant charge checked. The pressure sensor exists to protect the compressor, and a low charge makes it do exactly what this code describes - the sensor is often right.
  2. Inspect the connector for corrosion and water.
  3. Check the reference voltage and earth at the sensor.
  4. Check the condenser is clear of leaves, insects and road debris.
  5. If refrigerant has been lost, find the leak rather than simply recharging. A system topped up without repair will be empty again within months.
