P0439

Catalyst Heater Control Circuit (Bank 2)

Severity
Low
Driving
Drivable — book it in
System
Powertrain
Group
Auxiliary emission controls

A fault in the catalyst heater control circuit on bank 2. This heater brings the converter up to working temperature quickly after a cold start, when a petrol engine produces most of its emissions. The engine runs normally without it; what suffers is the first minute of every journey.

Possible causes

  • CommonFailed catalyst heater element
  • CommonBlown fuse or failed relay
  • CommonCorroded high-current connections
  • PossibleWiring damage
  • RareFailed control module driver

What you might notice

  • Check engine light on
  • Failed emissions test
  • No drivability symptom

What to check, in order

  1. Check the fuse and relay for the circuit.
  2. Inspect the heater connections for corrosion and heat damage.
  3. Measure the heater element resistance against specification.
  4. Check the wiring between the module and the heater.
