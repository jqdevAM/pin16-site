P0A12

DC/DC Converter Enable Circuit Low

Severity
High
Driving
Drive only to a garage
System
Powertrain
Group
Hybrid propulsion
Important. The 12 volt system may not be charging. If the battery is going down, have the vehicle recovered rather than driving it until it stops.

The DC/DC converter enable circuit is reading low - typically a short to earth. The module cannot control when the converter runs, so it cannot guarantee the 12 volt system is being charged. Depending on where the fault is, the converter may be stuck off, or stuck on when it should be resting.

Possible causes

  • CommonWiring a short to earth on the enable circuit
  • CommonCorroded or water-damaged connector
  • CommonFailed DC/DC converter input stage
  • PossibleBlown fuse
  • RareFailed driver inside the control module

What you might notice

  • 12 volt battery going flat, or conversely being overcharged
  • Vehicle refusing to power up
  • Multiple unrelated warning lights
  • Vehicle powering down unexpectedly

What to check, in order

  1. Measure the 12 volt system voltage powered up and again after shutdown. It should be around 14 volts running and should stop being driven once the vehicle is off.
  2. Check the 12 volt battery first - it is the most common cause of scattered hybrid system codes.
  3. Unplug the converter's control connector and check whether the low reading persists at the harness.
  4. Check the enable circuit fuse and the wiring back to the module.
  5. Stop at the 12 volt system. Anything further needs the manufacturer's high-voltage procedure.
