P0908

Gate Select Position Circuit Intermittent

Severity
High
Driving
Drive only to a garage
System
Powertrain
Group
Transmission and control modules

The gate select position signal is dropping out intermittently. Gear changes work most of the time and then occasionally fail, often leaving the car in neutral until it is restarted. Intermittent faults here are usually connections rather than the sensor.

Possible causes

  • CommonLoose, corroded or backed-out connector pin at the gearbox
  • CommonChafed or fatigued wiring
  • CommonWater ingress into the connector
  • PossibleSensor dropping out when hot
  • PossibleWorn sensor track losing signal at one position

What you might notice

  • Occasional refusal to change gear
  • Car dropping into neutral and recovering after a restart
  • Fault linked to heat, bumps or wet weather
  • Check engine light coming and going

What to check, in order

  1. Note when it happens. Hot points at the sensor, bumps point at wiring, wet points at the connector - that observation is worth more than any single measurement.
  2. Wiggle-test the harness at the gearbox with the live data screen showing gate position.
  3. Inspect the connector for a pin that has backed out of its housing.
  4. Run the position calibration and see whether the fault returns.
  5. Do not replace the sensor on an intermittent code alone.
