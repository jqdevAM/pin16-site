P2724

Pressure Control Solenoid "E" Stuck On

Severity
High
Driving
Drive only to a garage
System
Powertrain
Group
Transmission

Pressure control solenoid "E" is stuck on - applying pressure when it has been told not to. Line pressure will be higher than intended, so shifts become abrupt and the transmission may refuse to shift at all. Debris in the fluid holding the solenoid open is the usual reason.

Possible causes

  • CommonDebris in the fluid jamming the solenoid
  • CommonFailed solenoid
  • CommonCorroded transmission case connector, or fluid wicking into it
  • CommonLow or degraded fluid
  • PossibleInternal transmission harness failure
  • PossibleSticking valve in the valve body bore

What you might notice

  • Very harsh or violent shifts
  • Slipping under load
  • Transmission stuck in one gear
  • Delayed engagement into drive or reverse
  • Check engine light on

What to check, in order

  1. Check whether the solenoid circuit is being held energised electrically before assuming a mechanical jam.
  2. Very harsh shifts put shock loads through driveline components. Do not drive it like this any longer than necessary.
  3. Check the fluid level and condition first. It is the cheapest check and it explains more transmission complaints than every electrical fault combined.
