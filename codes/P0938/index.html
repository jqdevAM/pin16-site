P0938

Hydraulic Oil Temperature Sensor Range/Performance

Severity
Medium
Driving
Drivable — book it in
System
Powertrain
Group
Transmission and control modules

The hydraulic oil temperature sensor is reading within its electrical range but the value is not plausible - it does not rise as the system warms, rises too fast, or sits at an unlikely figure. The sensor is answering; it is just not telling the truth.

Possible causes

  • CommonFailed or drifting temperature sensor
  • CommonLow fluid level leaving the sensor uncovered
  • CommonHigh resistance in the wiring skewing the reading
  • PossibleFluid genuinely overheating from a pump running constantly
  • PossibleWrong sensor fitted at a previous repair

What you might notice

  • Slow or harsh gear changes
  • Temperature reading implausible on the display
  • Transmission warning message
  • Check engine light on

What to check, in order

  1. Watch the temperature climb from cold on the live data screen. It should rise smoothly and settle.
  2. Check the fluid level first - a low level uncovers the sensor and produces this code with a perfectly good sensor.
  3. Consider whether the fluid really is overheating. A pump running constantly because of a leak will genuinely cook the fluid.
  4. Check for high resistance in the wiring and connector.
  5. Read all codes for pressure or pump faults alongside.
