P2613

A/C Refrigerant Distribution Valve Control Circuit High

Severity
Low
Driving
No driving restriction
System
Powertrain
Group
Computer output circuits and control module

The air conditioning refrigerant distribution valve circuit is reading high - typically shorted to voltage or a lost earth. The module cannot direct refrigerant between the evaporators.

Possible causes

  • CommonFailed distribution valve solenoid
  • CommonCorroded connector or damaged wiring
  • PossibleBlown fuse on the circuit
  • PossibleLow refrigerant charge preventing normal operation
  • RareFailed control module driver

What you might notice

  • Air conditioning cooling unevenly between front and rear
  • Rear climate zone not cooling
  • Air conditioning performance poor
  • Check engine light on
  • No effect on how the car drives

What to check, in order

  1. Disconnect the valve and check whether the high reading persists at the harness.
  2. Check whether one zone works and another does not. That pattern points at the distribution valve rather than at the system as a whole.
  3. Check the fuse for the circuit.
  4. Inspect the connector and wiring at the valve.
  5. Measure the solenoid winding resistance against specification.
  6. Have the refrigerant charge checked - a low charge produces poor cooling that looks like a distribution fault.
