P0502

Vehicle Speed Sensor "A" Circuit Low Input

Severity
Medium
Driving
Drivable — book it in
System
Powertrain
Group
Vehicle speed control, idle control and auxiliary inputs

The vehicle speed sensor "A" signal is low. A signal that is absent, weak or jumping usually means wiring or a mechanical problem at the sensor rather than a module fault.

Possible causes

  • CommonDamaged or corroded wiring and connector
  • CommonFailed vehicle speed sensor
  • PossibleExcessive gap between sensor and reluctor ring
  • PossibleMetal debris on the sensor tip
  • PossiblePoor ground connection

What you might notice

  • Speedometer dropping to zero or jumping
  • Gear changes becoming harsh or erratic
  • Cruise control disengaging
  • Warning light appearing and clearing

What to check, in order

  1. Inspect the connector for water and corrosion - this is the most common cause on an exposed sensor.
  2. Watch the live speed value while driving slowly over a rough surface; a dropout points at wiring rather than the sensor.
  3. Remove the sensor and check the tip for metal debris, which is common on a sensor reading a steel ring.
  4. Check the sensor's ground.
  5. Replace the sensor once the wiring and mounting have been eliminated.
