P0572

Brake Switch "A" Circuit Low

Severity
Medium
Driving
Drivable — book it in
System
Powertrain
Group
Vehicle speed control, idle control and auxiliary inputs
Important. Brake lights stuck on will flatten the battery overnight and will confuse drivers behind you. Brake lights that do not work at all are an immediate safety problem and an inspection failure.

The brake switch "A" signal is stuck low - typically shorted to earth, or the switch stuck closed. The module gets the same answer whether or not you are braking, so cruise control, the shift interlock and the torque converter clutch will all behave unpredictably.

Possible causes

  • CommonMisadjusted or failed brake pedal switch
  • CommonWorn or broken striker on the pedal bracket
  • CommonWiring or connector fault in the footwell
  • PossibleBlown brake light fuse
  • PossibleAftermarket alarm or cruise control spliced into the circuit

What you might notice

  • Brake lights stuck on or not working
  • Cannot shift out of park
  • Cruise control will not engage or will not cancel
  • Stop-start system disabled
  • Check engine light on

What to check, in order

  1. Unplug the switch and check whether the low reading persists at the harness.
  2. Look at the brake lights. Working brake lights that go off when the pedal is released eliminate most of this without any tools.
  3. Check the switch adjustment at the pedal - these are adjustable and drift as the plastic striker wears.
  4. Watch the brake switch state on the live data screen while someone presses the pedal.
  5. Inspect the connector in the footwell, where feet catch it.
  6. Ask whether an aftermarket alarm or cruise control was fitted.
