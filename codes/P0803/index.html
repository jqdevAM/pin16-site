P0803

1-4 Upshift (Skip Shift) Solenoid Control Circuit

Severity
Low
Driving
No driving restriction
System
Powertrain
Group
Transmission

A fault in the control circuit for the skip shift solenoid. On some manual-transmission vehicles the module deliberately blocks second and third gear at light throttle, forcing a shift straight from first to fourth to save fuel. This code means that solenoid circuit has faulted - so either the block stops working, or it stays engaged and you cannot select second or third at low throttle openings.

Possible causes

  • CommonFailed skip shift solenoid
  • CommonWiring or connector fault at the solenoid
  • CommonCorroded connector - the solenoid sits on the gearbox
  • PossibleBlown fuse on the circuit
  • PossibleAftermarket skip shift eliminator fitted
  • RareFailed control module driver

What you might notice

  • Cannot select second or third gear at light throttle
  • The skip shift block no longer happens
  • Skip shift lamp not working
  • Check engine light on

What to check, in order

  1. Establish whether the block is stuck on or stuck off. Being unable to select second gear at light throttle is a stuck-on solenoid; the feature simply never happening is a stuck-off one.
  2. Inspect the connector at the solenoid on the gearbox.
  3. Measure the solenoid winding resistance against specification.
  4. Ask whether a skip shift eliminator has been fitted. These devices are common and they set exactly this code by design.
  5. Check the fuse for the circuit.
