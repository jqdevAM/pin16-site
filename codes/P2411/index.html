P2411

Fuel Cap Sensor/Switch Circuit High

Severity
Low
Driving
No driving restriction
System
Powertrain
Group
Auxiliary emission controls

The fuel cap sensor circuit is reading high, typically shorted to voltage or with a lost earth. This switch tells the module whether the fuel filler cap is fitted and sealed, so it knows not to run a leak test with the tank open to the air. Start with the cap itself rather than the switch: a cap that is loose, cross-threaded or has a perished seal is by far the most common reason for this code.

Possible causes

  • CommonFuel cap loose, cross-threaded or not clicked home
  • CommonWorn or perished seal on the cap
  • CommonFailed cap sensor or switch
  • CommonCorroded connector at the filler neck
  • PossibleWiring damage in the filler area, where water collects
  • PossibleNon-original cap that does not operate the switch correctly

What you might notice

  • Check engine light on
  • Failed emissions test
  • Occasionally a smell of fuel
  • Usually nothing else - EVAP faults rarely affect how the car drives

What to check, in order

  1. Take the fuel cap off and refit it properly, until it clicks. This is the cheapest fix in motoring and it clears a surprising proportion of these codes - do it before anything else.
  2. Inspect the cap's rubber seal for cracking, hardening or a missing section, and check that the cap is the correct one for the vehicle. Cheap replacements often do not operate the switch.
  3. Inspect the connector and its wiring before condemning the component. Everything in this system lives under the vehicle in the path of road salt and spray, and a corroded connector produces these codes far more often than a failed part does.
  4. Check the sensor's signal on live data with the cap on and off. A signal that does not change between the two confirms the switch rather than the cap.
