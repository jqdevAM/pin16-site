P0959

Auto Shift Manual Mode Circuit Intermittent

Severity
Low
Driving
No driving restriction
System
Powertrain
Group
Transmission and control modules

The auto shift manual mode circuit is faulting intermittently. Manual mode works, then briefly does not. Connections and worn contacts are more likely than a failed module.

Possible causes

  • CommonLoose or corroded connector pin
  • CommonWorn switch contacts
  • CommonSticky residue from a spilled drink
  • PossibleChafed wiring under the console
  • PossibleWorn selector gate mechanism

What you might notice

  • Manual mode dropping out and returning
  • Mode indicator flickering
  • Occasional ignored shift request
  • Check engine light coming and going

What to check, in order

  1. Move the selector between modes repeatedly while watching the reported state.
  2. Wiggle-test the connector under the console.
  3. Inspect the selector for spilled drink.
  4. Check the gate mechanism for wear.
  5. Confirm automatic shifting is normal, in which case this is minor.
