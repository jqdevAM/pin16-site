P0116

Engine Coolant Temperature Circuit Range/Performance

Severity
Medium
Driving
Drivable — book it in
System
Powertrain
Group
Fuel and air metering

The coolant temperature reading is implausible in context rather than out of range - for example not rising as the engine warms, or disagreeing with intake air temperature after the car has stood overnight.

Possible causes

  • CommonSensor drifting with age
  • CommonThermostat stuck open, so the engine genuinely never warms properly
  • PossibleLow coolant level
  • PossiblePoor connection adding resistance

What you might notice

  • Check engine light on
  • Worse fuel economy
  • Heater slow to warm up
  • Temperature gauge behaving oddly

What to check, in order

  1. Watch coolant temperature through a full warm-up on the live data screen. It should rise steadily to the normal operating range.
  2. If it rises very slowly or plateaus low, suspect the thermostat rather than the sensor.
  3. Check the coolant level.
  4. Compare against intake air temperature on a fully cold engine - they should agree closely.
