P0621

Generator Lamp/L Terminal Circuit

Severity
Medium
Driving
Drivable — book it in
System
Powertrain
Group
Computer output circuits and control module

A fault in the generator lamp or L-terminal circuit. The L terminal is the connection that lights the battery warning lamp and, on many alternators, also provides the initial excitation the alternator needs before it will start charging. That second job is why this code sometimes means no charging at all rather than just a lamp fault.

Possible causes

  • CommonBlown battery warning lamp bulb - on older systems this alone stops the alternator exciting
  • CommonWiring or connector fault on the L terminal circuit
  • CommonFailed alternator regulator
  • PossibleCorroded connector at the alternator
  • PossibleFailed instrument cluster lamp driver

What you might notice

  • Battery warning light not working, or permanently on
  • No charging at all
  • Battery going flat
  • Check engine light on

What to check, in order

  1. Check whether the battery warning lamp lights during the ignition-on bulb check. If it never lights and the vehicle is not charging, the bulb may be the whole fault - a genuinely cheap repair that is easy to overlook.
  2. Measure charging voltage at the battery to establish whether the alternator is working at all.
  3. Inspect the connector at the alternator for corrosion and heat damage.
  4. Check continuity on the L terminal wire between the alternator and the cluster.
  5. Check the earth connections at the alternator.
