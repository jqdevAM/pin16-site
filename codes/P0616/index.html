P0616

Starter Relay Circuit Low

Severity
Medium
Driving
Drivable — book it in
System
Powertrain
Group
Computer output circuits and control module

The starter relay circuit is reading low - typically shorted to earth or open circuit. The module cannot control the starter relay properly, so the engine may not crank, or the starter may be engaged when it should not be.

Possible causes

  • CommonWiring shorted to earth or open circuit between the module and the relay
  • CommonFailed relay
  • CommonCorroded relay socket
  • PossibleWater ingress into the fuse box
  • RareFailed control module driver

What you might notice

  • Engine will not crank
  • Starter engaging on its own or continuing to crank
  • Grinding noise from the starter
  • Check engine light on

What to check, in order

  1. If the starter engages on its own or keeps cranking, disconnect the battery immediately. A starter driven against a running engine destroys itself and the flywheel ring gear within seconds.
  2. Check whether the low reading persists with the relay removed. That separates the relay from the wiring.
  3. Swap the relay with an identical one and see whether the fault follows.
  4. Inspect the socket for corrosion and heat damage.
  5. Check the fuse box for water ingress.
