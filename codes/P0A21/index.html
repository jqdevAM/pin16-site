P0A21

Motor Torque Sensor Circuit Intermittent

Severity
Medium
Driving
Drive only to a garage
System
Powertrain
Group
Hybrid propulsion
Important. High-voltage hazard nearby. The transaxle on a hybrid contains high-voltage components and orange cabling. Reading codes and checking connectors on low-voltage circuits is safe; do not disturb anything orange.

The drive motor torque sensor signal is dropping out intermittently. The vehicle drives normally and then restricts itself, often recovering after a restart. As with all intermittent circuit faults, connections are far more likely than the sensor.

Possible causes

  • CommonLoose, corroded or backed-out connector pin
  • CommonChafed or fatigued wiring
  • CommonLow 12 volt battery voltage
  • PossibleSensor dropping out when hot
  • PossibleWater ingress into the connector

What you might notice

  • Occasional reduced power that clears after a restart
  • Hybrid system warning message coming and going
  • Performance varying between journeys
  • Fault linked to heat or to wet weather

What to check, in order

  1. Check the 12 volt battery and have it load tested. On hybrids a marginal 12 volt battery produces intermittent faults across the whole vehicle.
  2. Note when it happens - hot, over bumps, or in the wet.
  3. Inspect the connector for a backed-out pin and for corrosion.
  4. Record the freeze frame data if the tool supports it.
  5. Do not disturb anything orange.
