P2168

Throttle/Pedal Position Sensor "F" Maximum Stop Performance

Severity
High
Driving
Drivable — book it in
System
Powertrain
Group
Fuel and air metering

Throttle or pedal position sensor "F" is not reading what the module expects at the fully open end of its travel. Either the sensor is drifting, or the throttle or pedal is not physically reaching its stop - a floor mat under the pedal will do exactly that.

Possible causes

  • CommonFailed throttle or pedal position sensor
  • CommonCorroded or damaged connector
  • CommonWiring damage, particularly at the pedal where feet catch the harness
  • CommonLoss of the 5 volt reference or the sensor earth
  • PossibleCarbon on the throttle plate affecting the reported position
  • PossibleSensor not calibrated after replacement

What you might notice

  • Reduced power mode
  • Hesitation or surging
  • Engine not responding to the pedal
  • Rough or unstable idle
  • Warning light for reduced engine power

What to check, in order

  1. Check nothing is fouling the pedal - a floor mat, a mat laid on top of the original, or an object under the pedal will stop it reaching full travel, and that is worth eliminating before anything electrical.
  2. Watch all the pedal and throttle position readings together on the live data screen while pressing the pedal slowly through its full travel. The one that jumps, sticks or does not follow the others is the fault.
  3. Check the reference voltage and earth at the sensor. Several sensors usually share these, so a lost reference produces a scatter of codes from healthy sensors.
  4. Inspect the connector for corrosion and water, and the harness at the pedal for damage.
  5. Look for other codes sharing the same reference circuit before condemning this sensor.
  6. On most vehicles the pedal sensors are part of the pedal assembly and are not supplied separately - establish that before ordering.
