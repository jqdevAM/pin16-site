P0101

Mass or Volume Air Flow Circuit Range/Performance

Severity
Medium
Driving
Drivable — book it in
System
Powertrain
Group
Fuel and air metering

The mass airflow sensor is working, but its reading does not agree with what the module expects from engine speed and throttle position. The signal is plausible in isolation and wrong in context - which usually means the sensor is drifting, or air is bypassing it.

Possible causes

  • CommonDirty mass airflow sensor reading low
  • CommonAir leak between the sensor and the engine
  • PossibleRestricted air filter
  • PossibleExhaust restriction, for example a blocked catalytic converter
  • PossibleFailed mass airflow sensor

What you might notice

  • Hesitation under acceleration
  • Lack of power
  • Rough idle
  • Worse fuel economy
  • Check engine light on

What to check, in order

  1. Check the intake ducting for leaks first - a split in the pipe after the sensor is the most common cause.
  2. Clean the sensor element with mass airflow sensor cleaner.
  3. Check the air filter is clean and correctly seated.
  4. Compare the airflow reading against expected values at idle and at higher engine speed. A sensor that reads plausibly at idle but low under load is drifting.
  5. If the car feels strangled at higher speeds, consider a blocked exhaust or converter.
