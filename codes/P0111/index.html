P0111

Intake Air Temperature Sensor 1 Circuit Range/Performance

Severity
Low
Driving
Drivable — book it in
System
Powertrain
Group
Fuel and air metering

The intake air temperature reading is implausible in context - not obviously out of range, but inconsistent with the other temperatures the module can see, such as coolant temperature after a cold soak.

Possible causes

  • CommonSensor drifting out of calibration with age
  • CommonPoor connection adding resistance to the circuit
  • PossibleSensor located where it reads heat soak from the engine rather than intake air

What you might notice

  • Check engine light on
  • Slightly worse fuel economy
  • Often no noticeable symptom

What to check, in order

  1. With the engine cold after standing overnight, compare intake air temperature and coolant temperature on the live data screen. They should read close together and close to ambient.
  2. Inspect the connector for corrosion, which adds resistance and shifts the reading.
  3. Check the sensor is correctly located and not touching a hot surface.
