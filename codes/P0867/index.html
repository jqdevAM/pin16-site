P0867

Transmission Fluid Pressure

Severity
High
Driving
Drive only to a garage
System
Powertrain
Group
Transmission

The transmission fluid pressure is not what the module expects, without it specifying high or low. Line pressure is what holds the clutches together, so a pressure problem is one of the more serious things a transmission can report - too little and the clutches slip and burn, too much and the shifts become violent.

Possible causes

  • CommonLow fluid level
  • CommonBlocked transmission filter
  • CommonFailed pressure control solenoid
  • PossibleWorn pump
  • PossibleInternal leak past a worn seal or clutch
  • PossibleFaulty pressure sensor

What you might notice

  • Slipping or harsh shifting
  • Delayed engagement into drive or reverse
  • Limp mode
  • Burnt smell from the fluid
  • Check engine light on

What to check, in order

  1. Check the fluid level and condition first. It is the most common cause and the only one you can fix cheaply.
  2. Check when the filter was last changed.
  3. Read all codes for a pressure sensor or solenoid code that names the component.
  4. A line pressure test with a gauge is the definitive step here and is worth paying for.
  5. Look for clutch material in the fluid, which tells you whether the transmission is worth repairing before any money is spent.
