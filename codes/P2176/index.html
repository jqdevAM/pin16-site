P2176

Throttle Actuator Control System - Idle Position Not Learned

Severity
Medium
Driving
Drivable — book it in
System
Powertrain
Group
Fuel and air metering
Important. This is a procedure, not a part. If throttle work was just done, running the relearn is the remaining step rather than evidence that the repair went wrong.

The throttle's idle position has not been learned. These systems measure where the throttle plate rests when unpowered and use that as their zero point - and after cleaning, replacement or a battery disconnection, that reference is gone until the relearn procedure is run. Nothing has failed; a calibration is missing.

Possible causes

  • CommonRelearn never run after cleaning or replacing the throttle body
  • CommonBattery disconnected, clearing the learned position
  • CommonRelearn attempted but its conditions were never met
  • CommonAnother stored fault preventing the procedure from completing
  • PossibleCarbon preventing the plate reaching its true rest position

What you might notice

  • Idle too high, too low or hunting
  • Hesitation when pulling away
  • Reduced power mode
  • Behaviour noticeably worse since throttle body work

What to check, in order

  1. Ask what was done. Throttle body cleaning, replacement or a battery disconnection immediately before this appeared is the explanation.
  2. Run the relearn procedure. It usually requires specific conditions - a warm engine, everything switched off, the accelerator untouched - and must be allowed to finish.
  3. Clear any other stored faults first. The procedure will not complete while another fault is active.
  4. If the relearn will not complete, check the plate reaches its true rest position - carbon can hold it slightly open.
  5. Do not replace parts for this code. It is a procedure, not a component.
