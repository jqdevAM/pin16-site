P0317

Rough Road Hardware Not Present

Severity
Low
Driving
Drivable — book it in
System
Powertrain
Group
Ignition system or misfire

The module expects a rough road sensor and cannot find one. Rough road detection exists so that the bumps of a poor road surface are not mistaken for engine misfires - the crankshaft speed fluctuates in both cases. Without it, misfire detection is either disabled or prone to false alarms.

Possible causes

  • CommonRough road sensor or its wiring faulty
  • CommonSensor not fitted, or removed during previous work
  • CommonModule expecting the option because it is coded for a different specification
  • PossibleABS or wheel speed fault, where the rough road signal comes from that module
  • PossibleCorroded connector

What you might notice

  • Check engine light on
  • Misfire codes on rough roads that do not match how the engine feels
  • Misfire detection disabled
  • Often no symptom the driver notices

What to check, in order

  1. Ask whether a module has been replaced or recoded. A module coded for a specification the car does not have will look for hardware that was never fitted.
  2. Check whether the vehicle is supposed to have this sensor at all.
  3. Check for ABS or wheel speed codes - on many vehicles the rough road signal comes from that module rather than from a dedicated sensor.
  4. Treat misfire codes with suspicion until this is resolved, particularly ones that only appear on poor road surfaces.
