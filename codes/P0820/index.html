P0820

Gear Lever X-Y Position Sensor Circuit

Severity
Medium
Driving
Drivable — book it in
System
Powertrain
Group
Transmission

A fault in the gear lever X-Y position sensor circuit. On a shift-by-wire or automated manual selector there is no cable to the gearbox - the lever's position is measured on two axes and sent electrically. If the module cannot read that position it does not know what gear you are asking for, so it will refuse to select one.

Possible causes

  • CommonFailed gear lever position sensor assembly
  • CommonDrink or dirt spilled into the selector assembly
  • CommonWiring or connector fault under the console
  • PossibleLoss of reference voltage or earth
  • PossibleSelector assembly worn or damaged
  • PossibleSensor not calibrated after replacement

What you might notice

  • Cannot select a gear
  • Gear position display blank or wrong
  • Transmission staying in neutral or park
  • Warning message about the selector
  • Check engine light on

What to check, in order

  1. Watch both position axes on the live data screen while moving the lever through its gate. An axis that does not change identifies the fault.
  2. Look inside the selector assembly for spilled drink. Centre console selectors sit directly beneath cup holders and this is the most common cause of their failure.
  3. Check the reference voltage and earth at the sensor.
  4. Inspect the connector under the console.
  5. Check whether the assembly needs calibrating after replacement - many do.
