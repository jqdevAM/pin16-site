P0599

Thermostat Heater Control Circuit High

Severity
Low
Driving
Drivable — book it in
System
Powertrain
Group
Vehicle speed control, idle control and auxiliary inputs

The thermostat heater control circuit is reading high - typically shorted to voltage or a lost earth. The module cannot control the thermostat heater, so engine temperature will not be managed as precisely as intended.

Possible causes

  • CommonFailed thermostat heater element
  • CommonCorroded connector or damaged wiring
  • PossibleBlown fuse on the circuit
  • PossibleThermostat stuck, so heating it changes nothing
  • RareFailed control module driver

What you might notice

  • Engine running cooler than normal
  • Heater not as warm as it should be
  • Worse fuel economy
  • Temperature gauge slow to rise
  • Check engine light on

What to check, in order

  1. Disconnect the thermostat heater and check whether the high reading persists at the harness.
  2. Watch the coolant temperature during a normal drive. An engine that never reaches its usual operating temperature points at the thermostat itself rather than the heater circuit.
  3. Check the connector and wiring at the thermostat.
  4. Measure the heater element resistance against specification.
  5. Check the fuse for the circuit.
  6. Note that this heater does not warm the engine. It warms the thermostat so it opens earlier than it otherwise would, which the module uses to fine-tune running temperature.
