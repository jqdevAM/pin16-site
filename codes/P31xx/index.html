P31xx

Fuel and air metering

How much air and fuel the engine is receiving, and the sensors that measure it - mass airflow, manifold pressure, intake air temperature, oxygen sensors and fuel trim.

No open-source descriptions exist for this system, so its codes are read from their structure.

Mixed: manufacturer-specific and standardised. Treat as manufacturer-specific unless a specific explanation is available.
