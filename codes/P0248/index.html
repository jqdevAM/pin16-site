P0248

Turbo/Super Charger Wastegate Solenoid "B" Range/Performance

Severity
Medium
Driving
Drive only to a garage
System
Powertrain
Group
Fuel and air metering - injector circuits

Wastegate solenoid "B" is electrically sound but boost is not following what was commanded. That points at the mechanism rather than the circuit - a seized actuator, a soot-clogged variable-vane mechanism, or a leaking hose.

Possible causes

  • CommonFailed wastegate solenoid
  • CommonWastegate actuator seized, or its linkage stuck with carbon
  • CommonSplit, perished or disconnected vacuum or pressure hose
  • CommonCorroded connector or damaged wiring
  • CommonVariable-vane mechanism sticking with soot, on a diesel
  • RareFailed control module driver

What you might notice

  • Reduced power or limp mode
  • Boost too high or too low
  • Excessive smoke on a diesel
  • Whistling or fluttering from the turbo
  • Check engine light on

What to check, in order

  1. Check the hoses first. A split or disconnected boost control hose is the most common cause and the cheapest to fix.
  2. Check the wastegate actuator moves freely. On a diesel with a variable-vane turbo, soot seizing the mechanism is extremely common on a car used mainly for short journeys.
  3. Measure the solenoid winding resistance against specification.
  4. Inspect the connector for corrosion and heat damage.
  5. Watch commanded against actual boost on the live data screen through an acceleration.
