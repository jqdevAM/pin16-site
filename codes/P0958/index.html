P0958

Auto Shift Manual Mode Circuit High

Severity
Low
Driving
No driving restriction
System
Powertrain
Group
Transmission and control modules

The auto shift manual mode circuit is stuck high - typically shorted to voltage or open circuit. The module sees the same mode state regardless of the selector, so manual mode will either be permanently unavailable or permanently reported.

Possible causes

  • CommonSwitch stuck or failed
  • CommonWiring shorted to voltage or open circuit in the console
  • CommonDrink or dirt in the selector assembly
  • PossibleCorroded connector under the console
  • RareFailed control module input

What you might notice

  • Manual mode unavailable or permanently active
  • Mode indicator stuck
  • Check engine light on

What to check, in order

  1. Watch the mode state without touching the selector.
  2. Unplug the switch and check whether the high reading persists at the harness.
  3. Inspect the selector for spilled drink.
  4. Check the connector under the console.
  5. Confirm automatic shifting is unaffected.
