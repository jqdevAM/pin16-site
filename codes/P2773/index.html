P2773

Four Wheel Drive (4WD) Low Switch Circuit Low

Severity
Medium
Driving
Drivable — book it in
System
Powertrain
Group
Transmission

The four-wheel drive low range switch signal is stuck low - typically shorted to earth or a stuck switch. The module has no usable indication of whether low range is selected.

Possible causes

  • CommonFailed or worn switch
  • CommonWiring or connector fault
  • CommonCorroded connector - underbody switches take road spray and salt
  • PossibleSwitch out of adjustment or its linkage worn
  • PossibleDamaged clock spring, on steering-wheel-mounted controls

What you might notice

  • Low range unavailable
  • Mode indicator stuck
  • Shift points wrong
  • Check engine light on

What to check, in order

  1. Unplug the switch and check whether the low reading persists at the harness.
  2. Inspect the connector for corrosion and water.
  3. Follow the harness under the vehicle looking for chafe and impact damage.
  4. Check the switch continuity in each position against specification.
