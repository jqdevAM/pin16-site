P0145

O2 Sensor Circuit Slow Response (Bank 1 Sensor 3)

Severity
Low
Driving
Drivable — book it in
System
Powertrain
Group
Fuel and air metering

Oxygen sensor 3 on bank 1, after the catalytic converter, is responding too slowly. Usually this means an aged sensor. Because downstream sensors are used for monitoring rather than fuel control, the practical effect is a warning light and a failed emissions test rather than a drivability problem.

Possible causes

  • CommonOxygen sensor worn out with age and mileage
  • PossibleSensor contaminated by oil or coolant
  • PossibleExhaust leak near the sensor

What you might notice

  • Check engine light on
  • No drivability change
  • Failed emissions test

What to check, in order

  1. Check the sensor's age - downstream sensors are frequently still original at high mileage.
  2. Watch the response on the live data screen against the upstream sensor for comparison.
  3. Check for exhaust leaks nearby before replacing it.
