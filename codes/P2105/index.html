P2105

Throttle Actuator Control System - Forced Engine Shutdown

Severity
Critical
Driving
Stop driving
System
Powertrain
Group
Fuel and air metering
Important. An engine that shuts down while the car is moving takes the power steering assistance and the brake servo with it. Both go at the same moment, and the steering becomes very heavy while the brakes need far more pedal effort. Have the vehicle recovered rather than driven.

The module has shut the engine down deliberately. It found a throttle system fault serious enough that it judged running the engine unsafe - most often because it can no longer be certain the throttle will close when asked. Shutting down is the safest available response, but it means the engine can stop while the car is moving.

Possible causes

  • CommonAnother throttle or pedal fault already stored - this code is the module's response to it
  • CommonThrottle or pedal position sensor disagreement
  • CommonCarbon making the throttle plate stiff
  • CommonWiring or connector fault on a throttle or pedal circuit
  • PossibleLow or unstable supply voltage
  • PossibleFailed throttle actuator control module

What you might notice

  • Sudden loss of power - reduced-power mode
  • Engine will not rev beyond a low limit
  • Rough or unstable idle
  • Warning light for reduced engine power
  • Engine may not start

What to check, in order

  1. Do not keep restarting and driving. If the module has shut the engine down once it can do so again, and it will happen without warning.
  2. Have the vehicle recovered. This is not a fault to drive to a garage.
  3. Read all codes. This one records what the module did, not what went wrong - the fault that triggered it is stored alongside, and that is the one to diagnose.
  4. Check battery voltage and the earth connections. An unstable supply produces sensor disagreements that look like component failures.
  5. Inspect the throttle body for carbon and the connectors for corrosion.
  6. Compare commanded against actual throttle position on the live data screen.
