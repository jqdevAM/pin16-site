P2754

Transmission Fluid Cooler Control Circuit Low

Severity
Medium
Driving
Drivable — book it in
System
Powertrain
Group
Transmission

The transmission fluid cooler control circuit is reading low - typically shorted to earth or an open circuit. The module cannot control the cooler path, so fluid temperature will not be managed as intended.

Possible causes

  • CommonFailed fluid cooler control valve or its solenoid
  • CommonCorroded connector or damaged wiring
  • CommonBlocked or restricted fluid cooler
  • PossibleBlown fuse on the circuit
  • RareFailed control module driver

What you might notice

  • Transmission running hot
  • Transmission overheat warning
  • Harsh shifting when hot
  • Slow to warm up in cold weather
  • Check engine light on

What to check, in order

  1. Disconnect the control valve and check whether the low reading persists at the harness.
  2. Check the cooler and its lines for blockage, leaks and external debris.
  3. Inspect the connector and wiring at the control valve.
  4. Measure the solenoid winding resistance against specification.
  5. Watch transmission fluid temperature on a normal drive - it should rise and then hold steady.
  6. Check the fluid level and condition first. It is the cheapest check and it explains more transmission complaints than every electrical fault combined.
