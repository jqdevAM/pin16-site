P0535

A/C Evaporator Temperature Sensor Circuit

Severity
Low
Driving
No driving restriction
System
Powertrain
Group
Vehicle speed control, idle control and auxiliary inputs

A fault in the air conditioning evaporator temperature sensor circuit. This sensor stops the evaporator freezing: as it approaches freezing point the module disengages the compressor, letting it thaw. Without the reading, the module will usually limit or disable cooling.

Possible causes

  • CommonFailed evaporator temperature sensor
  • CommonCorroded connector or damaged wiring
  • CommonSensor dislodged from its position in the evaporator
  • PossibleWater ingress from a blocked evaporator drain
  • PossibleLoss of reference voltage or earth

What you might notice

  • Air conditioning not cooling, or cooling intermittently
  • Evaporator freezing and airflow dropping away
  • Compressor cycling rapidly
  • Musty smell from the vents
  • Check engine light on

What to check, in order

  1. Compare the reported evaporator temperature with ambient on a system that has been off overnight - they should be close.
  2. Check the evaporator drain is clear. A blocked drain fills the housing with water, which reaches the sensor and its connector.
  3. Inspect the connector for corrosion and water.
  4. Check the reference voltage and earth at the sensor.
  5. Note that this sensor exists to stop the evaporator freezing solid. If airflow drops away after ten minutes of use, the evaporator is icing and the sensor is not doing its job.
