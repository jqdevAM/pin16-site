P0790

Normal/Performance Switch Circuit

Severity
Low
Driving
No driving restriction
System
Powertrain
Group
Transmission

A fault in the circuit for the switch that selects between normal and performance shift modes - the sport, economy or winter button on many automatics. The transmission will default to its normal programme. The car drives entirely normally; what you lose is the ability to change the shift character.

Possible causes

  • CommonFailed mode selector switch
  • CommonWiring or connector fault at the switch
  • PossibleWorn switch contacts from repeated use
  • PossibleDrink or dirt spilled into a centre console switch
  • RareFailed transmission control module input

What you might notice

  • Sport, economy or winter mode will not select
  • Mode indicator light not working or stuck on
  • Shift character fixed in one mode
  • Check engine light on

What to check, in order

  1. Watch the switch state on the live data screen while operating it.
  2. Inspect the switch and its connector. Console switches suffer from spilled drinks more than anything else.
  3. Check the wiring between the switch and the module.
  4. Confirm the transmission behaves normally in its default mode - if it does, this is a comfort fault and can wait.
  5. Check for other transmission codes. A mode switch fault alone is minor; alongside shift complaints, deal with those first.
