P0215

Engine Shutoff Solenoid

Severity
High
Driving
Drivable — book it in
System
Powertrain
Group
Fuel and air metering - injector circuits
Important. One thing this code does NOT describe: an engine revving uncontrollably on its own and not responding to the key. That is a diesel running on its own oil rather than on fuel, it will not stop by any normal means, and it can destroy itself or catch fire. If that happens, get everyone clear of the vehicle and call for help rather than trying to stop it.

A fault with the engine shutoff solenoid. On a diesel this solenoid is what cuts the fuel supply when you turn the key off - a diesel has no ignition to switch, so stopping it means stopping its fuel. Depending on how the fault falls, the engine may refuse to start, or may keep running after the key is removed.

Possible causes

  • CommonFailed shutoff solenoid
  • CommonCorroded connector or damaged wiring
  • CommonBlown fuse or failed relay
  • PossibleSolenoid sticking mechanically
  • PossibleFailed ignition switch or immobiliser output

What you might notice

  • Engine will not start
  • Engine keeps running after the key is turned off
  • Engine cutting out while driving
  • Check engine light on

What to check, in order

  1. If the engine will not stop when the key is turned off, you can normally stall it: with the vehicle stationary and the handbrake on, select a high gear and let the clutch out against the brakes. That is a way of stopping it safely, not a repair.
  2. Check the fuse and relay for the solenoid circuit before anything else.
  3. Check for battery voltage at the solenoid with the ignition on and again with it off.
  4. Measure the solenoid winding resistance against specification.
  5. Inspect the connector for corrosion.
