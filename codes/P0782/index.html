P0782

2-3 Shift

Severity
High
Driving
Drive only to a garage
System
Powertrain
Group
Transmission

The 2-3 shift did not complete as commanded - it did not happen, took too long, or flared. The module names the specific shift, which is genuinely useful: it points at the particular clutch, band and solenoid that shift depends on, rather than at the transmission as a whole.

Possible causes

  • CommonLow or degraded transmission fluid
  • CommonFailed or sticking shift solenoid for the 2-3 shift
  • CommonWorn clutch pack or band used by the 2-3 shift
  • CommonSticking valve in the valve body
  • PossibleLow line pressure
  • PossibleInternal seal leak in the circuit for that clutch

What you might notice

  • Engine speed flaring during the 2-3 shift
  • The 2-3 shift not happening, or happening very late
  • Harsh bang on the 2-3 shift
  • Limp mode
  • Check engine light on

What to check, in order

  1. Check the fluid level and condition first.
  2. Confirm what actually happens on the 2-3 shift while driving. Flaring means the clutch is not gripping; a bang means it is arriving too hard; nothing at all points at the solenoid or the valve.
  3. Read all codes. A solenoid code alongside this one usually names the cause directly.
  4. Check for a corresponding gear ratio code, which would confirm the clutch is slipping rather than the shift not being commanded.
  5. A pressure test tells you whether the hydraulics can deliver what that clutch needs.
  6. Consider the vehicle's history. A single failing shift on a high-mileage transmission that has never been serviced is usually wear rather than a component fault.
