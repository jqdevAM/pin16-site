P0941

Hydraulic Oil Temperature Sensor Circuit Intermittent

Severity
Medium
Driving
Drivable — book it in
System
Powertrain
Group
Transmission and control modules

The hydraulic oil temperature signal is dropping out intermittently. The reading is present, briefly absent, then back. Connections are far more likely than the sensor.

Possible causes

  • CommonLoose or corroded connector pin
  • CommonChafed or fatigued wiring
  • CommonFluid contamination in the connector
  • PossibleSensor dropping out when hot
  • PossibleBacked-out connector pin

What you might notice

  • Occasional slow or harsh gear change
  • Temperature reading jumping
  • Check engine light coming and going
  • Fault often linked to temperature

What to check, in order

  1. Note whether it happens hot or cold.
  2. Wiggle-test the harness with the live data screen showing temperature.
  3. Inspect the connector for a backed-out pin and for fluid.
  4. Check the fluid level and condition.
  5. Clear the code and see how long it takes to return.
