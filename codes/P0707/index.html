P0707

Transmission Range Sensor Circuit Low

Severity
High
Driving
Drive only to a garage
System
Powertrain
Group
Transmission

The transmission range sensor signal is stuck low - typically shorted to earth or open circuit. The module has no believable indication of which gear you have selected, so it will refuse to shift normally and will usually hold one gear.

Possible causes

  • CommonWiring shorted or open between the sensor and the module
  • CommonCorroded or water-filled connector at the sensor
  • CommonFailed range sensor
  • PossibleChafed harness under the vehicle
  • PossibleLoss of the sensor reference voltage or earth

What you might notice

  • Transmission stuck in one gear
  • Gear position display blank or wrong
  • Engine will not start
  • Reversing lights not working
  • Check engine light on

What to check, in order

  1. Unplug the sensor and check whether the low reading persists at the harness. If it does, the fault is in the wiring, not the sensor.
  2. Inspect the connector for corrosion and water. This connector sits low and takes constant road spray.
  3. Check the reference voltage and earth at the sensor before condemning it.
  4. Follow the harness along the transmission looking for chafe damage against the case or a bracket.
  5. Check the fluid level and condition while the vehicle is raised.
