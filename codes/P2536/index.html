P2536

Ignition Switch Accessory Position Circuit

Severity
High
Driving
Drivable — book it in
System
Powertrain
Group
Vehicle speed control, idle control and auxiliary inputs
Important. An engine that stops while the car is moving takes the power steering assistance and the brake servo with it. The steering becomes very heavy and the brakes need far more pedal effort, at exactly the moment you need them. Do not treat an occasional cut-out as something to monitor.

A fault in the ignition switch accessory position circuit. The module cannot reliably tell what position the ignition is in. That matters more than it sounds: worn ignition switch contacts can drop out of the run position while the car is moving, which stops the engine - and with it the power steering assistance and the brake servo.

Possible causes

  • CommonWorn ignition switch contacts
  • CommonHeavy keyring wearing the switch, or pulling it out of position
  • CommonWiring or connector fault at the switch
  • PossibleCorroded connector behind the steering column
  • PossibleAftermarket alarm, immobiliser or remote start spliced into the circuit
  • RareFailed control module input

What you might notice

  • Engine cutting out while driving
  • Engine will not start, or starts intermittently
  • Electrical accessories cutting in and out
  • Dashboard warning lights flickering
  • Key feeling loose or vague in the barrel

What to check, in order

  1. Take everything except the key off your keyring and see whether the fault changes. A heavy bunch of keys hanging from the barrel wears the switch and can drag it out of the run position over a bump - this is a real and well-documented failure, and it costs nothing to test.
  2. Watch the ignition position states on the live data screen while turning the key slowly through each position.
  3. Check for a position that reads ambiguously between detents - the classic sign of worn contacts.
  4. Inspect the connector behind the steering column.
  5. Ask whether an aftermarket alarm or remote start has been fitted.
  6. If the engine has cut out while driving even once, treat this as urgent rather than intermittent.
