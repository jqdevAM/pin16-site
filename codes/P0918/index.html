P0918

Gear Shift Position Circuit Intermittent

Severity
High
Driving
Drive only to a garage
System
Powertrain
Group
Transmission and control modules

The gear shift position signal is dropping out intermittently. Gear changes work, then occasionally fail, often leaving the car in neutral until it is restarted. Look at connections before components.

Possible causes

  • CommonLoose, corroded or backed-out connector pin at the gearbox
  • CommonChafed or fatigued wiring
  • CommonWater ingress into the connector
  • PossibleSensor dropping out when hot
  • PossibleWorn sensor track losing signal at one position

What you might notice

  • Occasional failure to change gear
  • Car dropping into neutral and recovering after a restart
  • Fault linked to heat, bumps or wet weather
  • Check engine light coming and going

What to check, in order

  1. Note when it happens - hot, over bumps, or in the wet. That single observation locates most intermittent faults.
  2. Wiggle-test the harness at the gearbox with the live data screen showing shift position.
  3. Inspect the connector for a backed-out pin.
  4. Run the calibration and see whether the fault returns.
  5. Record the freeze frame data if the tool supports it.
